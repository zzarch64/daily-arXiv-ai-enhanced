### [1] [Analog Courant Numbers and their Role in Analog Computing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27609)
*Arash Ghasemi*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文识别了模拟计算（阻抗网络实现矩阵行）中的一个动力学约束：最快的归一化模式不超过所有电路行中最大组合单位增益带宽（CUGBW）的2π倍。CUGBW等于行的有限增益校正单位增益带宽加上所有耦合行贡献的总和，该理论在大规模LTspice仿真中得到验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 模拟计算硬件（如阻抗网络实现矩阵运算）缺少一个类似数值时间步进方法中Courant数的物理收敛条件。作者希望确定模拟硬件能表示和解析的最大算子速率边界，从而为模拟计算的稳定性和分辨率提供理论约束。

Method: 提出CUGBW（组合单位增益带宽）概念来量化模拟计算架构的动态约束，推导最快归一化模式的上界（2π×最大CUGBW），并通过大规模LTspice仿真在CMOS和热离子真空管架构上验证，基准电路涵盖一维热方程、图半监督学习和图正则化回归。

Result: 推导出了与Courant数限制类似的上界定理：模拟硬件可表示和解析的算子速率受限于2π×CUGBW。该理论在多种架构和大规模电路仿真中得到验证。

Conclusion: CUGBW界为模拟计算提供了一种类似于Courant数限制的稳定性/分辨率准则，可用于指导模拟硬件设计中的带宽与耦合权衡，确保硬件能物理表示目标算子的动力学。

Abstract: This paper identifies a dynamical constraint on analog-computing approaches in which a row of the matrix is represented by an impedance network. It shows that the fastest normalized mode is no more than $2π$ times the largest combined unity-gain bandwidth (CUGBW) among all the circuit rows. The CUGBW of a row equals its finite-gain-adjusted unity-gain bandwidth plus the contributions of all rows coupled to it. Each contribution is the square root of the product of the two rows' unity-gain bandwidths multiplied by their coupling conductance and divided by the square root of the product of their total conductance loadings. This bound plays a role analogous to the Courant-number restriction in time-stepping methods by limiting the operator rates that analog hardware can physically represent and resolve at its outputs. The theory is validated using large-scale LTspice simulations across architectures ranging from CMOS to thermionic vacuum-tube circuits. The benchmark circuits implement a one-dimensional heat equation, a graph-based semi-supervised learning problem, and a graph-regularized regression.

### [2] [GyRot: Leveraging Hidden Synergy between Rotation and Fine-grained Group Quantization for Low-bit LLM Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27694)
*Sangjin Kim,Yuseon Choi,Byeongcheol Kim,Jungjun Oh,Hoi-jun Yoo*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: GyRot是一个通过算法-硬件协同设计来桥接旋转与细粒度分组量化之间鸿沟的低比特量化框架与硬件加速器。它引入Coarse Rotation, Fine Grouping (CoRFiG)和Harmonic-Aligned Permutation (HAP)实现旋转与分组量化的协作集成，并通过对非对称量化的重构和零点舍入策略实现全整数反量化，在INT4张量PE架构上实现了LLaMA系列模型最先进的4比特精度，同时相比基线加速器获得最高3.4倍加速和3.6倍能效提升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 低比特量化对高效LLM推理至关重要，旋转(Rotation)和细粒度分组量化(Fine-grained Group Quantization)各自都展现出潜力，但二者的组合往往因旋转的全局特性与分组缩放的局部行为不匹配而导致精度退化或硬件开销增加。作者希望解决这一矛盾，实现两者的协同集成。

Method: GyRot采用算法-硬件协同设计的方法。算法层面提出Coarse Rotation, Fine Grouping (CoRFiG)以协调旋转与分组量化的集成，以及Harmonic-Aligned Permutation (HAP)增强可量化性并放松缩放因子精度约束。硬件层面重新设计非对称量化公式，引入零点舍入策略实现完全整数反量化，并基于INT4张量PE架构实现加速器。

Result: 在INT4张量PE架构上，GyRot在LLaMA系列模型上达到了最先进的4比特精度，相较基线LLM加速器实现最高3.4倍速度提升和3.6倍能效提升。

Conclusion: 实验结果表明GyRot能够有效解决旋转与分组量化组合不匹配的问题，验证了其在可扩展且高能效的LLM部署中的实用有效性，为低比特LLM推理提供了算法与硬件协同设计的可行路径。

Abstract: Low-bit quantization is essential for efficient LLM inference, and both rotation and fine-grained group quantization have shown individual promise. However, their combination often leads to accuracy degradation or hardware overhead due to a mismatch between the global nature of rotation and the localized behavior of group scaling. We propose GyRot, a quantization framework and hardware accelerator that bridges this gap through algorithm-hardware co-design. GyRot introduces Coarse Rotation, Fine Grouping (CoRFiG) and Harmonic-Aligned Permutation (HAP) to enable cooperative integration of rotation and group quantization, enhancing quantizability while relaxing scaling factor precision. To further reduce hardware cost, we reformulate asymmetric quantization and introduce a zero-point rounding strategy that enables fully integer dequantization. Implemented on an INT4-based tensor PE architecture, GyRot achieves state-of-the-art 4-bit accuracy across LLaMA-family models, while delivering up to 3.4x speedup and 3.6x energy efficiency over baseline LLM accelerators. These results validate GyRot's practical effectiveness for scalable and energy-efficient LLM deployment.

### [3] [LightRot: A Light-Weighted Rotation Scheme and Architecture for Accurate Low-Bit Large Language Model Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27704)
*Sangjin Kim,Yuseon Choi,Jungjun Oh,Byeongcheol Kim,Hoi-Jun Yoo*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出LightRot，一种轻量级旋转方案及专用硬件加速器，用于低比特大语言模型推理。通过分组局部旋转（GLR）和离群方向对齐（ODA）算法，结合基于层级快速Hadamard变换的分层旋转单元，在28nm CMOS工艺下实现4-bit推理27.4 TOPS/W的峰值能效，并在LLaMA2-13B、LLaMA3-8B等先进模型及MT-Bench基准上验证了其有效性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大语言模型在各领域展现出卓越能力，但在低比特量化推理中面临两大挑战：旋转操作的能耗开销显著，以及此前方法多依赖高精度推理或在GPT-2等基础语言任务上评估，缺乏在先进模型和真实对话场景中的验证。

Method: LightRot设计了轻量级旋转方案与专用硬件加速器。算法层面提出分组局部旋转（GLR）和离群方向对齐（ODA）两种算法；硬件层面构建了基于层级快速Hadamard变换（FHT）的分层旋转单元，用于高效执行低比特推理中的旋转运算。

Result: 在28nm CMOS工艺上实现的加速器在4-bit推理下达到27.4 TOPS/W的峰值能效，超越此前最先进设计。在LLaMA2-13B和LLaMA3-8B等先进模型上验证性能，并在MT-Bench基准上展现出对真实对话场景的鲁棒适用性。

Conclusion: LightRot通过算法创新与硬件效率的协同，为可扩展的低比特LLM推理设立了新的范式，为可持续AI发展铺平道路。

Abstract: As large language models (LLMs) continue to demonstrate exceptional capabilities across various domains, the challenge of achieving energy-efficient and accurate inference becomes increasingly critical. This work presents LightRot, a lightweight rotation scheme and dedicated hardware accelerator designed for low-bit LLM inference. The proposed architecture integrates Grouped Local Rotation (GLR) and Outlier Direction Aligning (ODA) algorithms with a hierarchical Fast Hadamard Transform (FHT)-based rotation unit to address key challenges in low-bit quantization, including the energy overhead of rotation operations. The proposed accelerator, implemented in a 28nm CMOS process, achieves a peak energy efficiency of 27.4 TOPS/W for 4-bit inference, surpassing prior state-of-the-art designs. Unlike conventional approaches that rely on higher-precision inference or evaluate on basic language modeling tasks like GPT-2, LightRot is optimized for advanced models such as LLaMA2-13B and LLaMA3-8B. Its performance is further validated on MT-Bench, demonstrating robust applicability to real-world conversational scenarios and redefining benchmarks for chat-based AI systems. By synergizing algorithmic innovations and hardware efficiency, this work sets a new paradigm for scalable, low-bit LLM inference, paving the way for sustainable AI advancements.

### [4] [ARES: Adaptive Reasoning-Effort Steering for PPA- and Cost-Aware RTL Optimization with LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27879)
*Stef Cuyckens,Mihaela Jivanescu,Jun Yin,Chao Fang,Marian Verhelst*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出Ares系统，用于优化寄存器传输级（RTL）设计的功耗、性能与面积（PPA）。Ares引入归一化美元成本作为评估指标，发现长期记忆的工程化构建对效果影响甚微，真正关键的是自适应推理努力分配——仅在低努力层进展停滞时才升级到更深推理。在三个未见测试设计上，该策略使品质因数（FoM）降低23-27%（最佳固定努力为16-23%），在同等归一化成本下达到相当效果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有LLM智能体在优化RTL设计的PPA时存在三个问题：一是报告达到的质量时未考虑归一化成本，导致公平性缺失；二是将质量归因于工程化设计的跨设计记忆，但这种归因缺乏证据；三是对每一次LLM调用保持固定的推理努力，未能按需分配计算资源。

Method: Ares包含三项创新：(1) 提出归一化美元成本指标，与品质因数（FoM）同时报告，支持不同努力水平和优化器间的公平比较；(2) 通过成本核算发现工程化长期记忆相比简单的经验拼接并无可靠增益；(3) 提出自适应推理努力策略，通过耐心计数器（在21个训练设计上拟合）检测低努力层次的进展停滞，仅在停滞时才升级到更深推理，将推理资源分配在最需要的地方。

Result: 在三个训练时未见的设计上，努力策略将FoM降低23-27%，而最佳固定努力仅达16-23%，且归一化成本相同。Ares从LLM草稿的乘累加单元到手优化版本的差距中缩小了高达83%的差距，并以Dr. RTL更少的令牌（其12%）达到深25%的FoM。

Conclusion: Ares证明了在RTL设计优化中，推理努力的动态分配比工程化记忆的构建更能带来实际收益。归一化成本指标为不同智能体之间的公平比较提供了基准，而自适应努力策略表明按需投入深度推理能显著提升优化质量并降低开销。

Abstract: Large language model (LLM) agents optimize the power, performance, and area (PPA) of register-transfer-level (RTL) designs by iterating over edits, synthesis, and PPA analysis, paying a dollar cost for every LLM call. Prior agents report the quality reached without its normalized cost, attribute that quality to an engineered cross-design memory, and hold the reasoning effort of every call fixed. We propose Ares with three corresponding innovations. (1) We introduce a normalized dollar cost per LLM call reported alongside the figure of merit (FoM), enabling fair comparison across effort levels and optimizers. (2) Using this accounting, we find the construction of the long-term memory matters little. An engineered memory brings no dependable gain over a plain concatenation of the same experience. (3) We instead adapt the per-call reasoning effort by escalating to deeper reasoning only once progress at a lower effort stalls, via a patience counter fit on 21 training designs, allocating reasoning where it pays rather than uniformly across all iterations. On three test designs unseen during training, the effort policy lowers the FoM by 23-27% where the best fixed effort reaches 16-23%, at equal normalized cost. Ares closes up to 83% of the gap from an LLM-drafted multiply-accumulate unit to its highly hand-optimized counterpart, and reaches a 25% deeper FoM than state-of-the-art Dr. RTL at 12% of its tokens.

### [5] [Demystifying DRAM Read Disturbance: Bridging the Gap Between Experimental Characterization and Device-Level Modeling of RowHammer and RowPress Phenomena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28233)
*Haocong Luo,Longda Zhou,Ataberk Olgun,İsmail Emir Yüksel,Nisa Bostanci,Zhigang Ji,Xing Wu,Onur Mutlu*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 该论文旨在弥合DRAM读取干扰（如RowHammer和RowPress）的实验表征与器件级建模之间的差距。作者首先指出现有器件级模型在三个基本指标（位翻转方向、位翻转数量、ACmin）上与实验表征存在不一致，随后通过全面的TCAD仿真匹配实验现象，总结更新的器件级错误机制，并讨论对实验表征方法和缓解技术设计的启示。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: DRAM读取干扰位翻转（RowHammer和RowPress）对计算系统的安全性和可靠性构成严重威胁。现有工作分为两个方向：基于实验表征的经验性缓解方案，和基于物理机制研究的器件级模型。然而这两者之间存在鸿沟——现有器件级机制无法完全解释所有重要的实验观察结果，阻碍了对该问题的根本性理解和有效缓解。

Method: 1) 识别并展示现有器件级模型描述的物理机制与实验表征结果之间的差距和不一致，聚焦三个基本指标：位翻转方向、位翻转数量、ACmin。2) 进行全面且严谨的TCAD仿真，匹配实验中对RowHammer和RowPress位翻转观察到的现象。3) 根据仿真结果总结更新的器件级错误机制，并识别影响仿真与真实芯片表征匹配度的关键建模和仿真参数。

Result: 作者提出了更新的器件级错误机制来解释RowHammer和RowPress的位翻转行为，识别出若干对仿真结果与真实芯片表征匹配度有显著影响的关键建模和仿真参数，从而为理解DRAM读取干扰提供了原则性的基础。

Conclusion: 该工作弥合了实验表征与器件级建模之间的差距，为未来的DRAM读取干扰理解、表征和缓解提供了原则性基础。其成果对严格的实验表征方法论设计和有效的读取干扰缓解技术实现具有直接启示意义。

DRAM read disturbance bitflips significantly impact the safe, secure, and reliable operation of DRAM-based computing systems. Many prior works experimentally characterize these bitflips and propose mitigations based on empirical results. Other device-level works study their underlying physical mechanisms, but these mechanisms do not fully explain all major empirical observations.
  Our goal is to bridge the gap between experimental characterization and device-level modeling and understanding of RowHammer and RowPress, providing a principled foundation for future work on understanding, characterizing, and mitigating DRAM read disturbance. We first identify and demonstrate gaps and inconsistencies between the physical mechanisms of RowHammer and RowPress described by existing device-level models and experimental characterization of their bitflips. We focus on three fundamental metrics that should map to first-order physical mechanisms: 1) bitflip directions, 2) bitflip counts, and 3) the minimum number of aggressor row activations that trigger the first bitflips (i.e., ACmin). Second, we present a comprehensive and rigorous set of TCAD simulations that match phenomena observed in experimental characterizations of RowHammer and RowPress bitflips.
  From our results, we 1) summarize updated device-level error mechanisms for understanding RowHammer and RowPress bitflips, and 2) identify key modeling and simulation parameters that significantly affect whether simulation results match real-chip characterization. We discuss implications for 1) rigorous, comprehensive, and efficient experimental characterization methodologies of DRAM read disturbance bitflips, and 2) the design of DRAM read disturbance mitigation techniques.
