Across this rank and DRAM, NORmal places agent state by mutability and lifetime rather than by hotness, serving 200B-class models and persistent planner contexts in place while dynamically partitioning subchannels across agent phases. In serving-level simulation on production agent traces, NORmal is 8.9$\times$--28.0$\times$ faster than UFS offloading, within 0.4% of an unlimited-DRAM ideal, and 2.8$\times$--6.3$\times$ faster than HBF on sessions beyond 256K tokens, with 8.1$\times$--17.8$\times$ less memory energy per token.
