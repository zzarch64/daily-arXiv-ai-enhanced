### [1] [AI Hardware Accelerators for Large Language Models: Architectures and the Memory Wall](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28048)
*Siddharth Patel,Rohit Singh*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文综述了面向大语言模型（LLM）的AI硬件加速器全景，包括GPU、定制ASIC（TPU、Trainium、Groq、Cerebras）、FPGA、存内/近存计算以及新兴的神经形态和光子计算。以Transformer计算结构和roofline分析为统一框架，得出核心结论：LLM加速的决定性瓶颈不是算力而是内存——自回归解码阶段受带宽限制、KV缓存可与模型权重媲美、数据搬运主导能耗。没有单一架构在所有工作负载下最优，未来取决于硬件-算法协同设计和异构内存中心系统。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大语言模型对训练和推理硬件提出了前所未有的、仍在不断增长的需求。现有综述往往聚焦单一平台或单一实现，缺乏对完整硬件加速器生态的系统性比较。本文旨在以统一的分析框架（Transformer结构+roofline模型）全面审视各类加速器的优劣势，帮助研究者理解LLM加速的真实瓶颈所在。

Method: 以Transformer的计算结构（矩阵乘法+自回归解码）为基准，采用roofline分析作为统一框架，从计算、内存、能耗、可编程性和可扩展性五个维度对GPU、定制ASIC、FPGA、存内/近存计算、神经形态和光子计算等平台进行系统比较。分析聚焦于内存带宽、KV缓存规模和数据搬运能耗等关键约束因素。

Result: 发现没有单一架构在所有工作负载下均最优：GPU仍是灵活默认选项和训练主力；领域专用ASIC在稳定、高吞吐的工作负载下规模化优势明显；存内计算是应对内存墙最有前景的近期方案，可作为异构补充组件进入系统；神经形态和光子计算虽有潜力但尚未达到前沿规模的量产水平。核心发现是自回归解码相位受内存带宽限制，KV缓存大小可媲美模型权重，数据移动主导能量消耗。

Conclusion: LLM加速的未来取决于硬件-算法协同设计以及异构、内存中心的系统架构。核心论断是：对于大语言模型而言，"内存系统已成为计算机本身"——单纯追求算力提升无法突破内存墙瓶颈，必须围绕内存体系重构加速器设计。

Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) place unprecedented and still-growing demands on the hardware that trains and serves them. This review surveys the full landscape of AI hardware accelerators for LLMs, including general-purpose GPUs, custom ASICs such as TPUs, Trainium, Groq, and Cerebras, reconfigurable FPGAs, processing-in-memory and near-memory architectures, and emerging neuromorphic and photonic approaches across cloud and edge deployment. Using the transformer's computational structure and roofline analysis as a common framework, we show that the decisive constraint on LLM acceleration is not arithmetic but memory: the autoregressive decode phase is bandwidth-bound, the key-value cache can rival the model weights in size, and data movement dominates energy. Comparing platforms on compute, memory, energy, programmability, and scalability, we find that no single architecture is optimal across workloads: GPUs remain the flexible default and the workhorse of training; domain-specific ASICs win at scale for stable, high-volume workloads; processing-in-memory is the most promising near-term response to the memory wall, entering systems as a heterogeneous complement; and neuromorphic and photonic computing, while promising, are not yet production-ready at frontier scale. Future progress depends on hardware-algorithm co-design and heterogeneous, memory-centric systems: for large language models, the memory system has become the computer.

### [2] [Gen-TAS: A Generative AI-Aided Hardware-Software Task Allocation Framework for FPGA-GPP Heterogeneous Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28160)
*Mary Kong,Yuqin Zhao,Semih Vazgecen,Cristian Sestito,Themis Prodromakis*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出Gen-TAS，一个基于知识增强的LLM框架，用于用户特定的FPGA-GPP异构系统任务分配。该框架结合任务图分析与RAG技术，将LLM推理锚定在历史实现知识上，生成多种可解释的任务分配策略，并通过人机协同选择和确定性后端将LLM决策转化为可复现的FPGA SoC实现。在CNN和SDR负载上，延迟导向策略相比全GPP基线分别实现最高2.45倍和92.53倍的加速。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: FPGA-GPP异构系统融合了软件灵活性与可重构硬件的性能和能效优势，但确定应用任务应分配到GPP还是FPGA需要丰富的专家经验和广泛的设计空间探索，尤其在用户目标涉及延迟、通信、资源利用率和功耗等多种指标时，这一决策过程尤为困难。

Method: Gen-TAS是一个知识增强的LLM框架，包含三个关键组件：(1) 任务图分析用于理解应用结构；(2) 结合RAG技术将LLM推理锚定在历史实现知识上，生成多种针对用户指定目标的可解释分配策略；(3) 人机协同选择和确定性后端，将LLM生成的决策转化为可复现的FPGA SoC实现。

Result: 在CNN和SDR工作负载上、跨多个LLM的实验表明，Gen-TAS能够生成稳定且需求驱动的任务分配。延迟导向目标下，所选策略对应的实现相比全GPP基线分别获得最高2.45倍和92.53倍的加速；而其他目标下选择的策略则会在某些加速性能与FPGA-GPP通信、资源利用率或FPGA功耗之间进行权衡。

Conclusion: Gen-TAS通过知识增强的LLM框架有效解决了FPGA-GPP任务分配的复杂性问题，根据不同用户目标生成可解释且可复现的分配策略，在保持灵活性的同时实现了显著性能提升，为异构系统设计空间的自动化探索提供了新思路。

Abstract: FPGA-GPP heterogeneous systems combine software flexibility with the performance and energy efficiency of reconfigurable hardware. However, determining which application tasks should execute on the GPP or FPGA requires extensive expertise and design-space exploration, particularly when user objectives vary across latency, communication, resource utilisation, and power. This paper proposes Gen-TAS, a knowledge-grounded LLM framework for user-specific FPGA-GPP task allocation. By combining task-graph analysis with RAG, Gen-TAS grounds LLM reasoning in historical implementation knowledge and generates multiple explainable strategies tailored to the specified objectives. Human-in-the-loop selection and a deterministic backend connect LLM-generated decisions to reproducible FPGA SoC implementations. Experiments on CNN and SDR workloads across multiple LLMs demonstrate stable, requirement-driven allocation. Under latency-oriented objectives, implementations following the selected strategies achieve speedups of up to 2.45$\times$ and 92.53$\times$, respectively, relative to the corresponding all-GPP baselines while other objectives select strategies that trade some acceleration performance for FPGA-GPP communication, resource utilisation, or FPGA power.

### [3] [Neuromorphic architectures as numerical solvers for computational neuroscience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28387)
*Jakob Jordan,Ole Richter,Congyang Li,Mihai A. Petrovici,Rajit Manohar*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出将基于连续耦合（非脉冲）的神经元模型在分布式硬件上的仿真视为消息传递算法，并论证多比特数据包比脉冲更高效，高阶微分方程求解器能在降低计算和通信成本的同时实现更低数值误差，最终将一个现有神经形态架构改造为"无脉冲神经形态系统"作为分布式数值求解器，证明其在能耗和延迟方面具有实际优势。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的神经形态计算硬件主要围绕脉冲神经元交互设计，但计算神经科学和机器学习中存在一类基于连续耦合的"速率型"（rate-based）模型，它们不使用脉冲交互。这类模型在现有脉冲驱动的神经形态架构上难以高效仿真，缺乏针对非脉冲神经元模型的专用加速器设计原则。

Method: 将分布式仿真视为并行硬件上的消息传递算法，结合数值算法和分布式仿真领域的已有成果，提出高效数字神经形态加速器的设计步骤。核心设计原则包括：在分组交换网络中使用多比特数据包而非脉冲作为通信策略；采用高阶微分方程求解器而非基本数值积分方法以降低计算和通信成本并减少数值误差；同时考虑算术精度对性能增益的限制。

Result: 根据提出的设计原则，作者将一个现有神经形态架构转化为分布式数值求解器（称为"无脉冲神经形态系统"），用于连续耦合神经元模型的仿真，验证了理论分析，并展示了在能耗和延迟方面的实际优势。

Conclusion: 本文论证了非脉冲（速率型）连续耦合神经元模型可以在神经形态硬件上高效实现，关键在于采用多比特数据包通信和高阶数值求解器，这拓展了神经形态计算的适用范围，使其不仅限于脉冲网络的仿真。

Abstract: Neuromorphic computing is closely associated with spiking neuronal networks. However, an alternative class of so-called "rate-based" models arising from computational neuroscience and machine learning forgoes spiking interactions and instead relies on continuous coupling between neurons. Existing neuromorphic implementations designed around spike-based interactions are not well-suited for emulating such models. Here view the distributed simulation of these models as message-passing algorithms on parallel hardware. Leveraging prior art in numerical algorithms and distributed simulation, we outline steps that enable the design of efficient digital neuromorphic accelerators for non-spiking neuronal models. In particular, we show that multi-bit packets, rather than spikes, are the most efficient communication strategy in packet-switched networks and that compared to basic numerical integration methods, higher-order differential equation solvers decrease both computation and communication costs while achieving lower numerical error, but that these benefits are ultimately limited by arithmetic precision. Using our proposed design principles, we convert an existing neuromorphic architecture into a distributed numerical solver - a spikeless neuromorphic system - for continuously-coupled neuronal models. We thereby demonstrate that our theoretical considerations indeed translate into practical advantages, namely reduced energy consumption and delay.

</details>
