### [1] [Event-triggered Implicit Perturbation for Zeroth-Order Fine-Tuning of Spiking Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21223)
*Tengteng Lei,Prabodh Katti,Rashi Dutt,Houssem Sifaou,Tan Peng,Osvaldo Simeone,Kai Xu,Bipin Rajendran*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出一种面向内存计算(IMC)加速器的隐式扰动零阶优化(IPZO)架构，通过事件触发的扰动生成单元(PGU)将扰动与权重加权和直接结合，消除显式权重扰动带来的重复读改写(RMW)操作，并利用尖峰稀疏性和地址驱动的XOR重组方案(PGU-XOR)降低随机数生成器(RNG)硬件开销，在精度与软件RNG相当的同时显著降低扰动能耗。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 零阶优化(ZO)通过前向传播估算梯度，适用于非可微的脉冲神经网络(SNN)微调，但在内存计算(IMC)加速器上部署面临两大挑战：显式权重扰动导致的反复读改写(RMW)操作，以及产生统计独立逐权值扰动所需随机数生成器(RNG)的巨大硬件开销。

Method: 提出IPZO架构：(1) 事件触发扰动生成单元(PGU)将扰动和与IMC阵列的加权和直接结合，消除RMW操作并保持权重静止执行；(2) 利用尖峰稀疏性，仅对尖峰激活的权重行生成和累积扰动贡献，缩小RNG阵列行维度；(3) 引入地址驱动XOR重组方案(PGU-XOR)缓解直接RNG复用(PGU-Reuse)造成的空间相关性。

Result: (1) PGU-XOR在Spikingformer/CIFAR-10上精度76.41% vs 76.53%，在SpikeGPT/WikiText-2上PPL 54.20 vs 53.23，与软件RNG相当；PGU-Reuse精度下降9.56个百分点、PPL增加11.8。(2) TSMC 16nm工艺下，PGU-XOR较PGU-Reuse每次矩阵向量乘法面积增加40.3%-46.0%、能耗增加15.2%-48.9%，但收敛更快，等精度下总扰动能耗为PGU-Reuse的0.51x。(3) IPZO较传统显式权重扰动在B=64、T=4时扰动能耗降至0.46x-0.83x，且优势随BT减小而增大。

Conclusion: IPZO架构通过隐式扰动策略有效解决了ZO优化在IMC加速器上的RMW瓶颈和RNG硬件开销问题，在保持任务精度的同时显著降低扰动能耗，为大规模SNN在内存计算硬件上的高效微调提供了可行路径。

Abstract: Zeroth-order (ZO) optimization estimates gradients using only forward-pass evaluations, making it suitable for fine-tuning non-differentiable, event-driven spiking neural networks (SNNs). However, its deployment on in-memory computing (IMC) accelerators is constrained by the repeated read-modify-write (RMW) operations arising from explicit weight perturbation and the prohibitive hardware footprint of random number generators (RNGs) for statistically independent per-weight perturbations. To address these challenges, we propose an implicit-perturbation ZO (IPZO) architecture in which perturbation sums computed by an event-triggered perturbation generation unit (PGU) are combined with the weighted sums produced by the IMC array, eliminating perturbation-induced RMW operations while preserving weight-stationary execution of IMC. By exploiting spike sparsity, the PGU generates and accumulates perturbation contributions only for spike-activated weight rows, reducing the required row dimension of the RNG array. An address-driven XOR recombination scheme (PGU-XOR) is further introduced to mitigate the spatial correlations caused by direct RNG reuse (PGU-Reuse). The results show that (1) PGU-XOR matches software RNGs in accuracy on Spikingformer/CIFAR-10 (76.41% vs. 76.53%) and perplexity (PPL) on SpikeGPT/WikiText-2 (54.20 vs. 53.23), whereas PGU-Reuse degrades accuracy by 9.56 percentage points and increases PPL by 11.8; (2) implemented in a TSMC 16-nm CMOS technology, PGU-XOR incurs 40.3%-46.0% area and 15.2%-48.9% energy overhead per matrix-vector multiplication relative to PGU-Reuse, yet its faster convergence reduces the total perturbation energy to 0.51x that of PGU-Reuse at iso-accuracy; (3) IPZO reduces the perturbation energy to 0.46x-0.83x that of conventional explicit weight perturbation for a batch size of B=64 and T=4 time steps, with the advantage growing as BT decreases.

### [2] [SPICE: Speculative Prefetching with Low-Rank Expert Surrogates and Heterogeneous Orchestration for MoE Inference Acceleration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21240)
*Yongxiang Lyu,Ning Li,Bonian Jia*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: SPICE是一个针对MoE模型推理的推测性预取框架，通过轻量级专家预测与置信度感知的CPU-GPU协同调度，实现高达3.12倍的TPOT加速且质量损失极小。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: MoE模型虽然通过稀疏激活解耦了模型容量与计算成本，但大规模专家参数常超出GPU显存容量，导致推理延迟主要由专家加载时的host-to-device PCIe传输所主导。

Method: SPICE包含两个核心组件：1) 与目标MoE架构对齐的轻量级draft模型，采用置信度感知的自适应lookahead算法预取高置信度专家；2) 当预测未命中时，切换至成本感知的CPU-GPU异构编排——低置信度miss由常驻共享专家和低秩专家（LoRE）代理近似，而精确残差工作被卸载到CPU上，与GPU计算异步并行执行。

Result: 在DeepSeek-V2-Lite和Qwen2-57B-A14B上，跨多种GPU平台评估，SPICE实现了最高3.12倍的TPOT（每输出token时间）加速，且质量损失极低。

Conclusion: 有效的MoE推理卸载不仅需要预测未来的专家选择，还需要决定哪些miss值得近似处理、哪些需要精确恢复，以及精确残差工作应在何处执行。

Abstract: Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models are increasingly used in LLMs because sparse activation decouples model capacity from compute cost. However, the large expert parameter footprint often exceeds GPU memory capacity, making inference latency dominated by the host-to-device PCIe transfers for expert loading. To address these challenges, this paper presents SPICE, a speculative prefetching framework for MoE offloading that combines lightweight expert prediction with confidence-aware CPU-GPU orchestration. On one hand, SPICE builds a lightweight draft model aligned with the target MoE architecture, using a confidence-aware adaptive lookahead algorithm to prefetch high-confidence experts. On the other hand, when speculative predictions miss, SPICE switches to a cost-aware CPU-GPU heterogeneous orchestration: low-confidence misses are approximated by the resident shared expert with low rank expert (LoRE) surrogates, while exact residual work is offloaded to the CPU and executed asynchronously in parallel with ongoing GPU computation. Evaluated on DeepSeek-V2-Lite and Qwen2-57B-A14B across diverse GPU platforms, SPICE achieves up to 3.12 speedup in Time Per Output Token (TPOT) with minimal quality loss, showing that effective MoE offloading requires not only predicting future experts, but also deciding which misses deserve approximation, which require exact recovery, and where exact residual work should execute.

### [3] [Assessing Triple Modular Redundancy for Wide-Link, Low-Latency NoC Routers: Reliability and Physical Design Challenges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21288)
*Chen Wu,Michael Rogenmoser,Luca Benini,Angelo Garofalo*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文评估了在TSMC 7nm工艺下、面向物理AI tile加速器NoC路由器（2周期延迟、512位宽链路）实现三级TMR（粗粒度、仅状态、全TMR）的成本与可靠性权衡。结果表明仅全TMR能在超过一百万次注入故障中消除所有失效；虽然独立路由器面积开销7.04倍，但集成到完整AI tile后仅增加16.8%面积和15.2%功耗，关键路径不受影响，证明在先进工艺节点上全TMR是可行的NoC保护方案。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统可靠NoC研究多聚焦于窄链路（32位）、深流水线路由器和单粒子翻转（SEU），但当前NoC路由器已演进为超宽链路、低延迟、频率超过1GHz的先进实现，部署在恶劣环境中的物理AI tile加速器面临单粒子效应（SEE）威胁，可能导致死锁和静默数据损坏（SDC）。需要在新的架构和工艺背景下重新评估可靠性保护方案。

Method: 在TSMC 7nm工艺下对2周期延迟、512位宽链路的NoC路由器进行RTL到GDSII物理设计，实现了三种粒度的TMR（粗粒度、仅状态、全TMR），并分别在RTL级和网表级开展SEU和SET故障注入实验，同时将评估从独立路由器扩展到完整AI加速tile（含处理器和L1内存）进行系统级分析。

Result: 状态保留型TMR和粗粒度TMR无法提供充分防护；全TMR在每次实验超过一百万次注入故障中消除了所有观测到的失效。独立全TMR路由器面积开销为7.04倍，但在集成到完整AI tile后面积开销仅16.8%、功耗增加15.2%（GEMM基准下），tile关键路径完全不受影响。

Conclusion: 先进工艺节点（TSMC 7nm）提供了足够的布线资源，使得全TMR成为在恶劣环境中保护物理AI系统NoC的实用且可部署的方案，其系统级成本开销在可接受范围内。

Abstract: Protecting the Network-on-Chip (NoC) of physical-AI tile-based accelerators deployed in harsh environments against single-event effects (SEEs) is paramount for preventing NoC failures that can lead to deadlocks and silent data corruption (SDC). Prior work on reliable NoCs has largely focused on narrow links (e.g., 32-bit), deeply pipelined routers, and single-event upsets (SEUs). However, the state of the art has evolved toward low-latency NoC routers with ultra-wide links, implemented on advanced technology nodes and operating at frequencies above 1 GHz. We evaluate the cost and reliability trade-offs of implementing Triple Modular Redundancy (TMR) at three granularities (coarse, state-only, and full) for a 2-cycle-latency NoC router with 512-bit wide links. We carry out RTL-to-GDSII physical design in TSMC 7nm technology, as well as both RTL- and netlist-level SEU and SET fault injection campaigns. We evaluate the three TMR approaches in terms of reliability, cost, and physical design strategies, further extending the assessment from a standalone router to a full AI acceleration tile. Our results show that state-only and coarse-grained TMR do not provide sufficient protection against SEEs, whereas full TMR eliminates all observed failures across more than one million injected faults per experiment. Although the standalone full-TMR router incurs a 7.04x area overhead, this cost is drastically amortized once integrated into a complete AI accelerator tile with processors and local L1 memories: the same design adds only 16.8% area and 15.2% power consumption under a GEMM benchmark at the system level, with the critical path of the tile entirely unaffected. These results demonstrate that advanced technology nodes provide sufficient routing capacity to make full TMR a practical and deployable solution for protecting NoCs in Physical AI systems operating in harsh environments.
