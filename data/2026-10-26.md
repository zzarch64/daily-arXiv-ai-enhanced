### [1] [TranSAGE: Automating Spatial Accelerator Generation for Large-Scale Transformer Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32279)
*Chi Zhang,Yi Sun,Weichuang Zhang,Quan Chen,Jieru Zhao*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: TranSAGE 是一个基于编译器的框架，联合优化多算子流水线下的 Transformer 调度并自动生成对应的空间加速器实现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大规模 Transformer 的计算与存储需求增长，使高效推理面临挑战；空间加速器在片上带宽和能效上具有优势，但针对大模型的设计成本高、周期长。现有加速器生成器多面向单算子支持，忽视了多算子流水线带来的吞吐与能效收益。

Method: 提出 TranSAGE，将设计空间探索（DSE）引擎与多算子数据流优化相结合，并引入基于动态规划的图划分算法，以高效识别适合流水的算子簇及其最优数据流参数；同时设计空间架构原语与 lowering pass，自动生成高效 RTL 实现。

Result: 在四个代表性 LLM 上评估：相比商用 GPU/NPU 基线，吞吐最高提升 2.41x，能效最高提升 13.04x；相比最先进的加速器生成器，吞吐最高提升 11.33x，能效最高提升 6.07x。

Conclusion: 通过编译器层面的多算子流水线调度优化与自动化 RTL 生成，TranSAGE 在吞吐与能效上显著优于商用硬件基线及现有加速器生成器，为大规模 Transformer 的高效推理提供了可行方案。

Abstract: Large-scale Transformers have achieved remarkable success across NLP, computer vision, and robotics, but their growing computational and memory demands challenge efficient inference. Spatial accelerators offer advantages in on-chip bandwidth and energy efficiency, yet designing them for large models remains costly and time-consuming. Existing accelerator generators typically focus on single-operation support, overlooking the benefits of multi-operation pipelining, which limits throughput and energy efficiency.
  We propose TranSAGE, a compiler-based framework that jointly optimizes scheduling for large-scale Transformers under multi-operation pipelining and generates corresponding accelerator implementations. Our approach integrates a design space exploration (DSE) engine with multi-operation dataflow optimization and a dynamic programming-based graph partitioning algorithm to efficiently identify pipeline-friendly operation clusters and their optimal dataflow parameters. We further design spatial architecture primitives and lowering passes to automatically generate efficient RTL implementations.
  Evaluations on four representative LLMs demonstrate that TranSAGE achieves up to 2.41x higher throughput and 13.04x higher energy efficiency than commercial GPU/NPU baselines. Compared with state-of-the-art accelerator generators, TranSAGE achieves up to 11.33x higher throughput and 6.07x higher energy efficiency. Our code is available at https://github.com/sjtu-zhao-lab/TranSAGE.

### [2] [PARIS: Parallel Arithmetic Reduction and Integrated Selection for MSDF Inner Products](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32410)
*Malik Zohaib Nisar,Ic-Pyo Hong,Dorit Merhof,Muhammad Usman*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 提出PARIS架构，通过并行位平面归约与共享进位保留残余选择消除MSDF加速器中输入项的串行处理，使内积延迟与扇入无关，显著提升速度与能效。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有MSDF（最高有效位优先）加速器将输入项处理串行化，导致内积延迟随扇入增大而线性增长，限制了吞吐与能效。

Method: PARIS将B位操作数的所有B²个位位置对并行处理；采用并行位平面归约和共享进位保留（carry-save）残余选择，保证输出为精确MSDF结果且延迟与tile内扇入无关。在45nm、100MHz下综合784通道INT8核心，并构建16引擎与256引擎的可扩展实现；另加入偏置感知ReLU检测。

Result: 784通道INT8核心面积0.008701 mm²，单次内积88周期。两个MNIST MLP上算术工作负载相对匹配的复用MSDF实现加速240.44×和180.47×，算术能耗降低98.89%和98.58%。16引擎加速器面积0.90273 mm²，满足100MHz时序，投影吞吐24.33 GOPS、能效1581.14 GOPS/W。偏置感知ReLU检测再降周期5.52%和4.86%；256引擎RTL实现259周期完成Model A。

Conclusion: 消除输入项串行化可有效解耦MSDF延迟与扇入，PARIS在保持精确输出的同时实现大幅速度、能耗与能效提升，并具备输出并行的良好可扩展性。

Abstract: Existing most-significant-digit-first (MSDF) accelerators serialize input-term processing, causing inner-product latency to increase with fan-in. This paper presents PARIS, an MSDF architecture that eliminates input-term serialization through parallel bit-plane reduction and shared carry-save residual selection. For $B$-bit operands, PARIS processes $B^2$ bit-position pairs, independent of fan-in within the supported tile, while generating exact MSDF outputs. Synthesized in 45\,nm at 100\,MHz, the 784-lane INT8 core occupies 0.008701\,mm$^2$ and completes an inner product in 88 cycles. Evaluation on two MNIST multilayer perceptrons demonstrates arithmetic-workload speedups of 240.44$\times$ and 180.47$\times$ over a matched reused-MSDF implementation, reducing arithmetic energy by 98.89\% and 98.58\%, respectively. A synthesized 16-engine accelerator occupies 0.90273\,mm$^2$ and meets the 100\,MHz timing constraint. Assuming immediate weight availability, it delivers a projected throughput of 24.33\,GOPS and an estimated energy efficiency of 1,581.14\,GOPS/W at unchanged mapped power. Bias-aware ReLU detection further reduces modeled arithmetic cycles by 5.52\% and 4.86\%. A separate 256-engine RTL implementation completes Model A in 259 cycles, demonstrating output-parallel scalability.

### [3] [Automated Multi-Line Bug Repair Using Model Checker Aided LLMs: A Case Study on RISC-V Processors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32597)
*Debleena Das,Chandan Kumar Jha,Muhammad Hassan,Rolf Drechsler,Ansuman Banerjee,Subhra K. Das,Amlan Chakrabarti*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 该论文提出一种将大语言模型（LLM）、断言与模型检查器（JasperGold）相结合的自动化RTL缺陷修复方法，利用失败断言引导LLM生成补丁，并经形式化验证筛选，实现可扩展且可信的多行RTL缺陷修复。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: RTL功能验证是现代半导体开发的关键瓶颈，调试尤为耗时；现有LLM辅助RTL调试/修复工作多为单行缺陷、开环流程、过度依赖提示与检索、缺乏可扩展性，且常产生错误修复，亟需兼具可扩展性与形式化保证的自动化修复方案。

Method: 以失败断言作为语义引导，驱动LLM为多行缺陷生成候选补丁；随后用模型检查器JasperGold对每个候选补丁进行形式化验证，仅接受通过验证的有效修复，从而形成闭环的可信修复流程。评估采用变异测试，在BRISC-V RISC-V核上引入多种变异类型进行验证。

Result: 在BRISC-V RISC-V核上，该方法可修复高达98%的单行RTL缺陷与93%的多行缺陷，并具有可扩展性。

Conclusion: 据作者所知，这是首个在复杂RISC-V处理器设计上以可扩展方式展示LLM辅助自动化多行RTL缺陷修复结果的工作，结合断言与形式化模型检查可有效弥补LLM修复的可信度与正确性缺陷。

Abstract: Functional verification of Register-Transfer Level(RTL) designs has become a critical bottleneck in modern semiconductor development, often consuming the majority of project resources. Debugging, in particular, remains an especially time-consuming task, requiring careful analysis of simulation traces or counterexamples. Recent work has explored applying Large Language Models (LLMs) to RTL debug and repair with great promise. However, the existing approaches typically are for single-line bugs, operate in open-loop, rely heavily on prompts or retrieval, lack scalability, and often produce incorrect fixes. In this paper, we propose a novel automated RTL bug repair methodology that combines LLMs, assertions, and a model checker to provide scalable, trustworthy semantic grounding and formal guarantees. Concretely, the methodology leverages failing assertions to guide the LLM in generating patches for multi-line bugs. Each candidate patch is formally verified using a model checker (JasperGold), ensuring only valid repairs are accepted. We evaluate the efficacy and scalability of our methodology on the BRISC-V RISC-V core using mutation testing, introducing several mutation types, and demonstrate that our methodology can repair up to 98% of single-line RTL bugs and 93% of the multi-line bugs. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first work to show automated, LLM-assisted RTL multi-line bug repair results on a complex RISC-V processor design in a scalable manner.

### [4] [Embodied Emissions of 2.5D and 3D Scaling in Chipletized GPUs for Low-Carbon AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32830)
*Hannah Watson,Arjun Singh,Jennifer Stephenson,Job Soethoudt,Debjyoti Bhattacharjee,James Myers*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文利用 imec.netzero 建模平台估算 2.5D 与 3D 封装扩展下 chiplet GPU 的隐含碳排放，发现计算单元与 HBM 容量翻倍时，3D 扩展使 EE 增加 89%，而 2.5D 增加 141%，表明 3D 扩展更有利于抑制 GPU 扩展带来的碳足迹。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: GPU 算力规模随 AI 负载持续扩张，超大规模数据中心对高性能 GPU 的依赖加剧了气候变化影响；因此需要量化封装技术选择对芯片碳足迹（尤其是隐含排放）的影响，为低碳化 GPU 扩展提供依据。

Method: 以 chiplet 架构 GPU 为对象，借助 imec.netzero 建模平台，分别构建 2.5D 与 3D 扩展方案，并将计算单元数量与高带宽存储（HBM）容量同时翻倍，进而估算两种方案下的隐含排放（EE）。

Result: 在计算单元与 HBM 容量翻倍的条件下，3D 扩展使 EE 增加 89%，而 2.5D 扩展增加 141%。

Conclusion: 3D 扩展在控制 GPU 规模化所带来的隐含排放方面明显优于 2.5D，是一种有吸引力的低碳扩展路径，封装决策应纳入芯片碳足迹评估。

Abstract: GPU compute resources are scaling to support growing AI workloads, leading to further rises in the climate change impact of hyperscale data centers that rely on high-performance GPUs. To model how packaging decisions impact the carbon footprint we have estimated the embodied emissions (EE) of a chiplet-based GPU that has been scaled in 2.5D and 3D using the imec.netzero modelling platform. We doubled the number of compute units and high bandwidth memory (HBM) capacity in the 2.5D and 3D scaled systems and found that 3D scaling results in an 89% increase in EE, as compared to an increase of 141% for 2.5D scaling, positioning 3D scaling as an attractive solution to limit the embodied emissions due to GPU scaling.

</details>
