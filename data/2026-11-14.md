### [1] [SlipStream: Enabling HBF for GPU Systems by Mitigating the Latency and SRAM Overheads](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14776)

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 提出 SlipStream，一种软硬件协同设计的预取器，将预取转化为"由需求定速的生产者-消费者流水线"，用 40MB 缓冲在 HBF 上实现理想性能的 92.4%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM 推理对内存容量需求持续增长。HBF 以 NAND 闪存替代 HBM 的 DRAM die，在相近读带宽下提供最多 16 倍容量，但读延迟近 20μs（HBM 的 20 倍），按需访问会拖慢推理最多 12.8 倍。传统基于 cache line 的预取器需 20 倍的在途跟踪状态才能覆盖该延迟；张量流式设计（如 H3）按虚拟地址顺序而非内核需求顺序预取，需数百 MB 缓冲。

Method: 软件侧：SlipStream 对数据无关的 LLM 内核中可重复的权重页需求顺序进行剖析（profile），插入一个 staging warp，用非阻塞 STAGE 指令重放该顺序。硬件侧：staging warp 作为生产者，work warp 作为消费者。核心洞见是仅当需求首次访问该页时才回收对应的 STAGE 跟踪表项（而非页从 HBF 返回时），从而让需求为 staging 定速并限制其领先程度。以 4KB 页而非 cache line 为跟踪粒度，且仅当页返回时才分配缓冲槽，使缓冲大小与在途流量解耦。

Conclusion: 通过需求定速的生产者-消费者预取流水线及页粒度跟踪与延迟分配缓冲，SlipStream 以远小于先前方案的缓冲高效隐藏 HBF 高延迟，显著提升 LLM 推理性能，且具备良好的可扩展与通用性。

Abstract: Large language model (LLM) inference demands ever more memory capacity for larger models and batch sizes. High-Bandwidth Flash (HBF) replaces the DRAM dies of HBM with NAND flash and provides up to 16x the capacity at comparable read bandwidth. However, HBF can incur a read latency of nearly 20$μ$s, 20x that of HBM, so on-demand HBF accesses slow inference by up to 12.8x. Conventional cache-line prefetchers require 20x more in-flight tracking state to cover this latency. Tensor-streaming designs, such as H3, follow virtual-address order as opposed to kernel-demand order, and therefore need hundreds of megabytes of buffering.
  We propose SlipStream, a software-hardware co-designed prefetcher that turns prefetching into a demand-paced producer-consumer pipeline. In software, SlipStream profiles the repeatable weight-page demand order of data-independent LLM kernels and inserts a staging warp that replays this order with non-blocking STAGE instructions. In hardware, the staging warp acts as the producer and the work warps as consumers.
