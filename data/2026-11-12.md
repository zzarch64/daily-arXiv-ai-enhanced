### [1] [Priority-Aware Deficit Round Robin for AXI4 Bandwidth Fairness in ASAP-7nm Process](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12979)
*Tasmin Khan,Tishna Sabrina,Ch. Md. Rakin Haider*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出一种按beat计费的QoS感知DRR仲裁架构，在ASAP7 7nm上从RTL到GDSII实现，并与轮询和带防饿死的QoS优先级两种架构在统一流量下对比。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: AXI4仅规定排序规则并提供4位QoS字段，却把服务策略留给实现者，而该策略决定各主设备的等待时间与带宽分配；现有策略在突发长度不匹配时存在带宽不公平和高延迟问题，缺乏面向实现代价的量化比较。

Method: 在同一端口列表下实现三种仲裁器：轮询、QoS优先级+防饿死老化、QoS感知DRR。DRR按beat计费且保持突发完整，地址阶段只发一次以保留访存局部性。三者由同一确定性流量发生器驱动，基于ASAP7 7nm预测PDK完成RTL到GDSII流程。

Result: 在1:16突发长度失配下，轮询与QoS架构使两主设备的beat比例维持1:16，而所提设计在满载时达到1:1。优先级主设备的平均读延迟从轮询的95周期降至QoS的67周期、QoS感知DRR的37周期；500 MHz下对应74 ns与190 ns。研究给出每种策略的实现代价及基于流量特征的选型指南。

Conclusion: 按beat计费的QoS感知DRR在保持突发原子性与访存局部性的同时显著改善带宽公平性与读延迟，并提供可量化的逐策略代价与流量驱动的选型依据。

Abstract: Modern systems-on-chip place real-time clients, processor cores and bulk data engines on the same AXI4 interconnect, where they contend for shared slaves. AXI4 fixes the ordering rules and carries a 4-bit quality of service (QoS) field, but leaves the service policy to the implementer, and that policy determines how long each master waits and how much bandwidth it receives once a slave saturates. All three are driven by the same deterministic traffic generator and taken from RTL to GDSII on the ASAP7 7-nm predictive PDK. Under a 1:16 burst-length mismatch, the round robin and QoS architectures both hold the beat ratio between the two competing masters at 1:16, while our design brings it to 1:1 at full load. Mean read latency for the priority master falls from 95 cycles under round robin to 67 with QoS and 37 with QoS-aware DRR. At 500 MHz this serves the priority master in 74 ns against 190 ns. The measurements give a per-policy price and a traffic-based guide for choosing among the three.
