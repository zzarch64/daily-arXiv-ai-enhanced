### [1] [The Fallacy of Independent Ceilings: Characterizing Coupled Load-Branch Stall Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11380)
*Matthew Constant,Resit Sendag*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出共生失速延迟（SSL）概念，揭示分支预测错误与缓存缺失在同一热循环中相互暴露的交互效应。通过联合速度增益协同（JSS）指标量化独立上限分析在53个工作负载中失败的情况，并提出轻量级MPKI筛选指标SSO（共生失速机会）用于识别需要完整联合模拟的工作负载，最终建立了一个评估框架来判断独立完美模式何时足够、何时低估联合性能空间。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有性能评估通常将分支预测错误和数据缓存未命中视为独立的瓶颈，以完美分支或完美缓存的单独加速比作为上界，并假设共同上界是两者乘积。然而在不规则负载中，难以预测的分支和缓存缺失加载常出现在同一热循环中，消除一个惩罚会暴露另一个，导致独立分析低估联合性能提升空间。

Method: 1) 定义JSS（联合速度增益协同）= 联合完美分支/完美缓存加速比 ÷ 各独立加速比乘积，用于量化独立天花板分析的失效程度；2) 提出SSO（共生失速机会），一种基于MPKI的轻量级筛选指标，用于识别需要完整联合模拟的工作负载；3) 在53个模拟工作负载上进行系统性测量，并映射高SSO负载到四种软件模式：邻居访问、哈希查找、链表结构遍历和数据依赖修改；4) 将SSL与重排序缓冲占用率、冲刷率和提交停滞等微架构机制相关联。

Result: 53个模拟工作负载中，70%展现出可测的耦合效应（JSS > 1）；采用保守阈值时，40%工作负载超过独立乘积6%以上；SSO > 20的内核JSS范围达到1.23至3.29。结果表明独立分析在对压力较低情况下近似成立，但在高SSO工作负载中显著低估联合性能空间。高SSO负载可归纳为四种软件模式，并建立了从微架构机制（ROB占用、冲刷率、提交停滞）角度的解释。

Conclusion: 本文的核心贡献是一个测量框架：使用SSO进行筛选、用JSS进行验证，并在评估分支预测器、预取器、缓存或耦合的分支/内存机制时，同时报告"分支在前缓存在后"和"缓存在前分支在后"的条件增益。该框架明确界定了独立完美模式何时充分、何时低估了联合性能改进空间，为处理器设计中的联合优化提供了量化依据。

Abstract: Branch mispredictions and data-cache misses are usually evaluated as separate bottlenecks: studies report perfect-branch or perfect-cache speedups as isolated upper bounds and often treat their product as the joint ceiling. In irregular workloads, however, hard-to-predict branches and cache-missing loads often occur in the same hot loops. Removing one penalty can expose the other: faster memory reaches mispredicted branches sooner, while better branch prediction leaves more long-latency loads in the out-of-order window. We call this interaction symbiotic stall latency (SSL).

Values above one mean independent-ceiling analysis understates attainable gain. With a conservative threshold, 40% exceed the independence product by more than 6%, and kernels with SSO > 20 show JSS from 1.23 to 3.29. We introduce symbiotic stall opportunity (SSO), a lightweight MPKI-based screen for workloads that merit full joint simulation.
  We map high-SSO workloads to four recurring software patterns: neighbor access, hash lookup, linked-structure traversal, and data-dependent modification. We connect SSL to reorder-buffer occupancy, squash rate, and commit starvation under isolated perfect modes. The resulting methodology is simple: use SSO to screen, JSS to validate, and report conditional branch-after-cache and cache-after-branch gains when evaluating branch predictors, prefetchers, caches, or coupled branch/memory mechanisms. Our contribution is a measurement framework showing when isolated perfect modes are adequate and when they understate joint performance headroom.

Motivation: BDT被广泛用于延迟关键型应用，但高效硬件部署仍具挑战。现有设计多采用统一或人工调优的定点格式，导致不必要的硬件成本或精度损失，亟需面向硬件的自动量化感知训练与硬件生成方案。

Method: 提出FQTree算法实现BDT的细粒度量化感知训练，核心包括：1）面向硬件的叶子值量化方案，采用全局量化步长配合树级移位，实现紧凑的非负整数叶子表示；2）受控的裁剪/剪枝和偏置折叠以降低数据路径成本；3）在提升过程中同步应用量化，使后续树适应已量化集成产生的误差；4）通过基于编译器的QXGB流程将训练好的模型转换为低延迟硬件实现。

Conclusion: FQTree+QXGB框架在保证精度（甚至略有提升）的前提下显著降低了BDT硬件实现的资源开销，验证了细粒度量化感知训练与自动硬件生成相结合的有效性，为延迟关键场景下的BDT部署提供了可行方案。

Abstract: Boosted decision trees (BDTs) are widely used in latency-critical applications, but efficient hardware deployment remains challenging. Existing designs often rely on uniform or manually tuned fixed-point formats, which can introduce unnecessary hardware cost or accuracy loss. This work presents the FQTree algorithm{https://github.com/ecs-bristol/FQTree} for fine-grained quantization-aware training of BDTs, together with the QXGB framework for automatic hardware generation. FQTree introduces a hardware-oriented leaf-value quantization scheme that uses a global quantization step together with a tree-wise shift, enabling compact non-negative integer leaf representations, controlled clipping/pruning, and bias folding to reduce datapath cost. This work further applies this quantization during boosting so that later trees adapt to the errors of the already-quantized ensemble, and then lowers the trained model into low-latency hardware implementations through a compiler-based flow. Results on JSC, MNIST, and NID show that our method reduces LUT usage by 26-57\% compared with the state-of-the-art FPGA-based BDT designs while matching or improving accuracy.
