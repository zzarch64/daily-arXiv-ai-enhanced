Abstract: GPU architecture research requires representative benchmarks that can execute on new designs. However, many vendor-tailored workloads and library kernels cannot run directly on research architectures because their implementations are closed-source or rely on programming models unsupported by the target architecture. Migrating existing kernels requires manual effort to adapt their implementations and validate their behavior. This creates a need to automate workload migration to target platforms.

The resulting profile signature gap is supplied to an agent for iterative refinement. We evaluate the method on Vortex, an open-ISA GPGPU, using robotics as the application domain. All active kernel pairs pass both output-equivalence checks, and the case studies show that iterative refinement can reduce profile distance.

### [2] [CacheFlex: Direct Software-Managed Access to Higher-Level Cache for Scalable Vector Support](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12940)
*Jingqun Zhang,Maohua Nie,Jiayi Wang,Weihang Li,Rishi Sappidi,Shwet Chitnis,Ang Li*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: CacheFlex通过ISA与微架构协同设计，将私有L2 SRAM在运行时部分转换为软件可管理的便签存储器（SPM），实现软件直接访问L2，从而缓解向量宽度扩展带来的L1数据缓存容量瓶颈。基于Armv8-A SVE原型与gem5仿真显示，其性能与能效增益随向量长度增加而显著提升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 更宽的向量/SIMD通道提升了计算吞吐，但缓存改进仍具挑战。作者识别出L1数据缓存容量是影响性能与效率的最关键参数。然而L1容量受流水线耦合与延迟要求限制，私有L2虽容量高一个数量级却无法被软件访问，导致每指令数据需求与内存性能之间的差距不断扩大。

Method: 提出CacheFlex，一种ISA与微架构协同设计：通过轻量级ISA扩展，允许运行时将私有L2 SRAM的部分转换为便签存储器（SPM），由软件显式控制数据在L2中的放置与生命周期。CacheFlex集成到乱序流水线中，支持跨域内存一致性与操作系统上下文切换，并在12 nm后布局实现中仅带来相对L2 2.02%的面积开销。基于Armv8-A SVE ISA构建原型并在gem5中建模，使用优化的GEMM内核、FlashAttention融合注意力及LLaMA-3.2-1B与BERT-Base的全层预填充工作负载进行评估。

Result: 当向量长度从VL=4（512位）扩展到VL=16（2048位）时，CacheFlex的内核级几何平均加速比与内存子系统能耗降低分别从1.12x和26%提升至1.44x和40%。在全Transformer层上结合FlashAttention，最高实现1.78x加速与45%内存子系统节能（BERT-Base，序列长度T=4096，VL=16；LLaMA-3.2-1B为1.64x和38%）。CacheFlex与硬件预取互补。

Conclusion: CacheFlex通过软件管理的L2直接访问有效弥合了每指令数据需求与内存性能之间日益扩大的差距，且其收益随向量宽度与峰值计算吞吐的增加而增长，特别适用于计算密集型的AI内核。

Abstract: Wider vector/SIMD lanes multiply compute throughput, but cache improvements remain challenging. We identify L1 data cache capacity as the most impactful parameter for performance and efficiency. L1 capacity is constrained by pipeline coupling and latency requirements; private L2 offers an order of magnitude more capacity but is inaccessible from software. This work proposes CacheFlex, an ISA and microarchitecture co-design that enables software-managed, direct access to private L2 SRAM by allowing runtime conversion of parts of L2 into a scratchpad memory (SPM). Through a lightweight ISA extension, software explicitly controls data placement and lifetime within the L2. CacheFlex integrates into an out-of-order pipeline with cross-domain memory consistency and OS context-switch support, with merely 2.02% area overhead relative to the L2 in a post-layout 12 nm implementation. We prototype CacheFlex based on the Armv8-A SVE ISA, model it in gem5, and evaluate it using optimized general matrix multiplication (GEMM) kernels with diverse model-derived shapes, FlashAttention-based fused attention, and full-layer workloads from LLaMA-3.2-1B and BERT-Base in the prefill phase. As the vector length (VL) scales from VL = 4 (512-bit) to VL = 16 (2048-bit), CacheFlex's kernel-level geometric-mean speedup and memory-subsystem energy reduction grow from 1.12x and 26% to 1.44x and 40%, respectively, bridging the widening gap between per-instruction data demand and memory performance. On full transformer layers combining CacheFlex with FlashAttention, the design delivers up to 1.78x speedup and 45% memory-subsystem energy savings (BERT-Base, sequence length T=4096, VL = 16; LLaMA-3.2-1B: 1.64x and 38%). CacheFlex is complementary to hardware prefetching, and its benefit grows as vector width and peak compute throughput increase for compute-intensive AI kernels.

### [5] [Anechoic: Onsager-Corrected Parallel Annealing for Sub-0.1 ms Dense Max-Cut on an FPGA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13368)
*Seungki Hong,Kyeongwon Jeong,Taekwang Jang*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出 Anechoic：一种在 Ising 机中消除同步更新"回声"(Onsager 反应项)的 FPGA 实现，以极低开销显著提升稠密 Max-Cut 求解效率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 稠密问题上常用的同步随机元胞自动机(SCA)一次更新全部自旋，但会产生"回声"效应——自旋通过邻居接收到自己上一步的状态，导致大量翻转被立即撤销(K2000 上约三分之二的翻转属于此类回退)，浪费硬件步与能耗。

Method: 用动态平均场理论把回声识别为 Onsager 反应项，其一步强度等于翻转概率严格介于 0 与 1 之间的自旋数除以两倍温度；据此在每次决策中减去该修正。由于决策逻辑本就标记这些自旋，修正仅需每步一次 popcount，温度缩放形式则完全无需额外计算。基于 RTL 引擎实现稠密 2,000 自旋更新。

Result: RTL 引擎每步耗时 18.1 周期 + 每次翻转 0.14 周期。在 K2000 上，各步预算下均优于 5 种已发表二值自旋算法。AMD Alveo V80 上 12 个引擎 @250 MHz：0.05 ms 内以 99% 概率达到 ≥33,000 割，比最快已发表硬件快 5.2 倍，5.4 mJ/解；2-bit 耦合下在 51 个 G-set 图中有 49 个快于未修正 SCA。NVIDIA GH200 GPU 上慢 2.3 倍、能耗高 8.0 倍，但 240 路并行退火带来 2.3 倍吞吐。GF 22nm ASIC @1.25 GHz 下 12 引擎约 10 μs 达标，比最快已发表硬件快 26 倍。

Conclusion: 通过解析并消除同步自旋更新的回声效应，可用近乎零成本(一次 popcount)的修正获得显著的收敛速度与能效收益；FPGA 验证有效，ASIC 与 GPU 外推显示该方案具有良好的硬件可扩展性。

Abstract: Ising machines solve combinatorial optimization problems by annealing a network of spins. On dense problems, the least expensive hardware step updates all spins at once: stochastic cellular automata (SCA) decide every spin in parallel and read couplings only for the spins that flip. Such synchronous updates create an echo: the previous state of each spin returns to it through its neighbors. On K2000, a Max-Cut benchmark on a 2,000-node complete graph, two-thirds of all flips undo the same spin's flip one step earlier. This paper presents Anechoic, an FPGA Ising machine that subtracts this echo from every decision. Dynamical mean-field theory identifies the echo as an Onsager reaction; its one-step strength is the number of spins whose flip probability is strictly between 0 and 1, divided by twice the temperature. The decision logic already flags these spins, so the correction costs one population count per step; a temperature-scaled form needs none. An RTL engine executes a dense 2,000-spin step in 18.1 cycles plus 0.14 per flip, so every saved step and flip saves time. Both forms find better K2000 cuts at every step budget than five published binary-spin algorithms at their papers' settings. Twelve engines at 250 MHz on an AMD Alveo V80 reach a cut of at least 33,000 with 99% probability in 0.05 ms, 5.2 times faster than the fastest published hardware, at 5.4 mJ per solution; with 2-bit couplings, the temperature-scaled form is also faster than uncorrected SCA on 49 of 51 G-set graphs. On an NVIDIA GH200 GPU, the same engine is 2.3 times slower and needs 8.0 times more energy per solution, though its 240 concurrent anneals give 2.3 times the throughput. As a GlobalFoundries 22 nm ASIC at 1.25 GHz, twelve engines would reach the target in about 10 microseconds, 26 times faster than the fastest published hardware. The code is available at https://github.com/skethz/anechoic.
