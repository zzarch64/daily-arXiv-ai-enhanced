### [1] [SlipStream: Enabling HBF for GPU Systems by Mitigating the Latency and SRAM Overheads](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14776)
*Anish Saxena,Hritvik Taneja,Christos Kozyrakis,Aamer Jaleel*

Main category: cs.AR

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM 推理对存储容量需求持续增长。HBF 用 NAND 闪存替代 HBM 的 DRAM 裸片，可提供约 16 倍容量且读带宽相当，但读延迟接近 20 μs（HBM 的 20 倍），按需访问会使推理性能下降最多 12.8 倍。传统缓存行预取器需 20 倍的在途跟踪状态；张量流式设计（如 H3）按虚拟地址顺序而非内核需求顺序预取，需数百 MB 缓冲。

Method: SlipStream 将预取改造为需求节拍的生产者-消费者流水线。软件侧：剖析数据无关 LLM 内核可重复的权重页需求顺序，插入 staging warp，用非阻塞 STAGE 指令重放该顺序。硬件侧：staging warp 作生产者、工作 warp 作消费者。关键洞见是仅在需求首次访问该页时退休 STAGE 跟踪项（而非页从 HBF 返回时），从而由需求为暂存定速并限制超前程度。以 4 KB 页而非缓存行为跟踪粒度，且仅在页返回时分配缓冲槽，使缓冲大小与在途流量解耦。

Conclusion: 通过软件预剖析权重页需求顺序与硬件需求节拍的生产者-消费者流水线协同，SlipStream 以远小于现有方案的暂存缓冲弥补了 HBF 的高延迟，使 HBF 成为大容量、近 HBM 性能的 LLM 推理存储方案，且缓冲扩展性与延迟、负载解耦。

Abstract: Large language model (LLM) inference demands ever more memory capacity for larger models and batch sizes. High-Bandwidth Flash (HBF) replaces the DRAM dies of HBM with NAND flash and provides up to 16x the capacity at comparable read bandwidth. However, HBF can incur a read latency of nearly 20$μ$s, 20x that of HBM, so on-demand HBF accesses slow inference by up to 12.8x. Conventional cache-line prefetchers require 20x more in-flight tracking state to cover this latency. Tensor-streaming designs, such as H3, follow virtual-address order as opposed to kernel-demand order, and therefore need hundreds of megabytes of buffering.

In software, SlipStream profiles the repeatable weight-page demand order of data-independent LLM kernels and inserts a staging warp that replays this order with non-blocking STAGE instructions. In hardware, the staging warp acts as the producer and the work warps as consumers.
