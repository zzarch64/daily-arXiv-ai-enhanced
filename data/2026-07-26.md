### [1] [Hardware-Software Co-Design for Float16 On-Device Training on RISC-V Single-Core](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21130)
*Benjamin Hubinet,Pierre-Alain Moellic,Olivier Savry,Olivier Potin,Jean-Baptiste Rigaud*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出基于RISC-V Zfh（标量float16）和Zvfh（向量float16）扩展的开源框架，在资源受限的单核RISC-V上实现完整的设备端训练。相比float32，内存占用减少约50%，性能损失极小，并支持迁移学习和层冻结。基于AIfES框架，Zfh在FPGA软核上仅增加1.15% LUT6和0.05% FF开销。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在资源受限的嵌入式设备上实现完整的设备端训练（on-device training），解决传统float32内存占用过大的问题，同时利用RISC-V开源生态的低硬件开销优势。

Method: 利用RISC-V标准扩展Zfh（标量float16）和Zvfh（向量float16），在AIfES开源框架中集成float16训练支持，结合层冻结技术实现迁移学习/微调，并在RV64GC超标量乱序FPGA软核上评估硬件开销。

Result: 相比float32，float16训练内存占用降低约50%，模型精度损失极小；Zfh扩展在RV64GC软核上仅增加1.15% LUT6和0.05% FF，运行频率175MHz。

Conclusion: float16结合RISC-V标准扩展是资源受限设备端训练的高效方案，开源框架可扩展性强，为嵌入式AI训练提供了低开销、高可用的实现路径。

Abstract: By leveraging standard RISC-V extensions, namely Zfh (scalar float16) and Zvfh (vector float16), this work proposes an open-source framework to enable complete on-device training on resource-constrained RISC-V single-core. Our approach allows memory footprint reduction by about 50% as compared to using float32 and with minimal model performance degradation. We also facilitate transfer learning and fine-tuning scenarios by incorporating layer-freezing capabilities. Our work builds onto AIfES, an open-source, modular and generic DNN training and inference framework for embedded systems that can be extended with custom hardware-specific functions. The benefits of float16 is further emphasized by outlining the low area overhead of Zfh on a RV64GC super-scalar out-of-order FPGA softcore (+1.15% LUT6 and +0.05% FF at 175MHz). Finally, we discuss the architecture of a Zvfh implementation within the same RISC-V core.

### [2] [Chip Floorplanning Combining Convex and Non-convex Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21408)
*Yilihamujiang Yimamu,Guillem Pastor-Rué,Jordi Cortadella*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种多阶段固定轮廓布线规划框架，结合非凸与凸优化，通过三个阶段（初始布线、全局布线和合法化）实现芯片布局优化，在多个基准测试中达到最优线长质量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 布线规划是VLSI物理设计的关键早期阶段，其质量直接影响互连线长、芯片性能和下游设计效率，因此需要更优的布线规划方法。

Method: 采用三阶段框架：1) 初始布线阶段使用二次布局优化模块连接性；2) 全局布线阶段采用基于Adam的投影梯度法求解非凸优化（最小化线长和重叠）；3) 合法化阶段利用对数变换模型结合水平和垂直约束图消除残余重叠（凸优化）。

Result: 在MCNC、GSRC和HB+基准测试上，平均HPWL相比最先进的布线规划器降低至少1%至5%。

Conclusion: 该多阶段框架结合非凸与凸优化，在固定轮廓约束下实现了最优的线长质量，验证了方法的有效性。

Abstract: Floorplanning is a critical early stage of VLSI physical design, and its quality directly impacts interconnect wirelength, chip performance, and downstream design efficiency. This article presents a multi-stage fixed-outline floorplanning framework that combines non-convex and convex optimization. The framework operates in three successive phases. In the initial floorplanning phase, a quadratic placement optimizes module connectivity to produce a topology-aware starting configuration with strong net clustering, albeit with significant module overlaps. In the global floorplanning phase, Adam-based projected gradient method is employed to solve non-convex optimization that minimizes wirelength and overlap. In the legalization phase, a log-transformed model exploiting horizontal and vertical constraint graphs eliminates residual overlaps under a convex formulation. Experiments on the MCNC, GSRC and HB+ benchmarks demonstrate that the framework achieves state-of-the-art wirelength quality, reducing average HPWL by at least 1\% and 5\% compared to state-of-the-art floorplanners on tested benchmarks.

</details>
