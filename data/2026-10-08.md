### [1] [Compiling Semi-Ring Dynamic Programming to Tier-Aware 3D-DRAM Processing-in-Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09156)
*Mahbod Afarin,Tsung-Han Lu,Tajana Rosing*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: GenMLIR 是一个面向分层感知 3D PIM 的 MLIR 编译器，将半环广义网格更新抽象为一等 IR，自动完成分块、分层放置、PU 分配与通信生成，在 APSP 和序列比对任务上分别实现最高 5.8 倍和 16.7 倍加速。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 单片 3D（M3D）DRAM 上的存内计算（PIM）有望缓解数据密集型动态规划（DP）的内存墙问题，但当前要发挥其性能必须手写内核：程序员需手动选择分块大小、在非均匀延迟的内存层级间放置数据、在异构处理单元间划分工作，并手工插入广播与队列。这一繁琐的映射过程阻碍了该架构的易用性与性能释放。

Method: GenMLIR 将半环广义网格更新（APSP 与序列比对等共享的代数形式）编码为一等 IR 抽象，并通过四组 GenDRAM 感知的编译 pass 进行 lowering，分别推导分块 tiling、分层感知的数据放置、tile 到处理单元（PU）的分配以及显式通信。此外，论文精确刻画了该抽象所能接纳与不能接纳的 DP 递推形式。

Result: 在 GenDRAM 架构上，GenMLIR 生成的代码相比无编译器支持的 lowering，在 APSP 上最高快 5.8 倍，在序列比对中最快 16.7 倍；相比作者实现的标准仿射 tiling PIM 编译器快 1.2–1.4 倍，达到可达性能上界的 90–100%。同时，这些工作负载的表达仅需 4–9 行代码（而非 300–500 行），且编译时间占运行时的比例可忽略。

Conclusion: GenMLIR 证明了通过将半环广义网格更新抽象为一等 IR，并借助分层感知的编译 pass，可以自动、高效地将动态规划工作负载映射到 M3D PIM 架构，在显著降低编程负担的同时达到接近上界的性能。

Abstract: Processing-in-Memory (PIM) on monolithic 3D (M3D) DRAM is a promising answer to the memory wall for data-intensive dynamic programming (DP), yet extracting its performance today demands hand-written kernels: the programmer must pick a tile size, place data across non-uniform-latency memory tiers, partition work between heterogeneous processing units, and insert the right broadcasts and queues by hand. We present GenMLIR, an MLIR compiler that automates this mapping for tier-aware 3D PIM. GenMLIR encodes the semi-ring generalized grid update, the algebraic form shared by all-pairs shortest path (APSP) and sequence alignment, as a first-class IR abstraction, and lowers it through four GenDRAM-aware pass groups that derive blocked tiling, tier-aware placement, tile-to-PU assignment, and explicit communication. We also characterize precisely which DP recurrences the abstraction admits and which it does not. On the GenDRAM architecture, GenMLIR-generated code runs up to 5.8 (APSP) and 16.7 (alignment) faster than a lowering without compiler support, and 1.2-1.4 faster than a standard affine-tiling PIM compiler we also implement, reaching 90-100% of an achievable-performance bound. It expresses these workloads in 4-9 lines instead of 300-500 and compiles in a negligible fraction of runtime.

### [2] [An Interleaved Parallel Dependent Quantization Hardware Architecture for H.266/VVC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09292)
*Jun Zhang,Weizhi Bian,Hao Zhang*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 提出一种交织并行依赖量化硬件架构，通过时分复用组合逻辑实现四通道并行量化，在极小BDBR损失下大幅提升H.266/VVC依赖量化的实时性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: H.266/VVC中的依赖量化虽具有高压缩率，但其强串行性和高复杂度导致实时性能差，难以在实际场景中部署。目标是在尽量不损失压缩性能的前提下提升其实时性。

Method: 采用交织并行依赖量化硬件架构，通过时分复用大部分组合逻辑实现四通道并行依赖量化；引入CG内上下文简化方案，以及速率估计时跳过decAbsLevel的编码方案。

Result: All Intra配置下BDBR损失仅0.42%，Random Access配置下0.38%。Verilog HDL实现并在Xilinx XCZU19 FPGA上，QP=22、27、32、37时量化速度分别达4K@33.2、91.4、331.2、454.1 fps。TSMC 28nm标准单元库ASIC实现下，对应速度达4K@84.0、231.0、837.0、1147.7 fps。

Conclusion: 该交织并行架构以极小的压缩性能损失显著提升了依赖量化的实时性，FPGA与ASIC实现均验证了其高吞吐优势，具备实际部署潜力。

Abstract: While dependent quantization in H.266/VVC delivers a high compression ratio, its strong serial nature and high complexity result in poor real-time performance, making it difficult to deploy in practical scenarios. To improve the real-time performance of dependent quantization with minimal degradation to its compression performance, we propose a interleaved parallel dependent quantization hardware architecture with low BDBR loss, which achieves four-channel parallel dependent quantization by time-division multiplexing most combinational logic. This architecture adopts the proposed intra-CG context simplification scheme and the encoding scheme that skips decAbslevel during rate estimation. The proposed design incurs a BDBR loss of only 0.42% under the All Intra configuration and 0.38% under the Random Access configuration, respectively. Implemented in Verilog HDL, the dependent quantization hardware architecture achieves quantization speeds of 4K@33.2, 91.4, 331.2, and 454.1 fps at QP = 22, 27, 32, and 37, respectively, when implemented on the Xilinx XCZU19 FPGA. When implemented on ASIC using the TSMC 28nm process standard cell library, the corresponding quantization speeds reach 4K@84.0, 231.0, 837.0, and 1147.7 fps.

### [3] [BenchmarkAnything: Agent-Driven Construction of Simulator-Ready Microarchitecture Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09447)
*Xiangfeng Sun,Ceyu Xu,Chen Bai,Yuan Xie*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 该论文指出SPEC等小型基准套件被过度依赖会导致体系结构设计"过拟合"，提出用AI智能体自动将开源仓库转化为仿真器可用可执行文件，从而快速构建包含数百个应用的大规模基准套件，并证明其评估保真度更高且能揭示SPEC无法暴露的新体系结构洞见。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 计算机体系结构研究中，基准负载的选择至关重要，它决定了衡量与引导体系结构创新的标尺。然而数十年来，仅包含数十个工作负载的SPEC套件一直是学术界事实上的标准，并被当作主要的评估与优化目标。如此小规模的套件被如此重度依赖，会带来"体系结构过拟合"风险：研究与其先前工作表明，过于狭窄的关注点会因高估某些创新而误导设计决策，造出在SPEC上表现出色、却在更广泛真实负载上表现不佳的核。

Method: 缓解过拟合的自然方案是采用大规模、全面的基准套件，但将软件剥离为仿真器所需的干净、无干扰的二进制可执行文件需要巨大工程投入，往往极不现实。作者提出由AI智能体驱动的自动化工作流，无需人工再策划静态基准套件，即可自主将任意开源仓库转化为仿真器就绪的可执行文件，使负载收集高度可扩展，快速从公共仓库采集数百个多样化应用。随后将该智能体生成的套件与SPEC进行对比分析。

Result: 对比分析显示，智能体生成的基准套件不仅实现了更高保真度的性能评估，还揭示了传统静态套件无法暴露的新颖体系结构洞见。

Conclusion: AI智能体为大规模基准负载构建提供了优雅且可扩展的解决方案，能有效缓解因过度依赖SPEC等小型套件而产生的体系结构过拟合问题，为体系结构研究提供更具代表性和发现力的评估基础。

Abstract: The selection of benchmark workloads is of paramount importance in computer architecture, as it establishes the yardstick against which architectural innovations are measured and guided. Yet for decades, the SPEC benchmark suites, comprising merely tens of workloads, have been the de facto standard in academic architectural research, where they are frequently treated as a principal evaluation and optimization target. When a suite this small is relied upon so heavily, it risks architectural overfitting; as our research and prior studies demonstrate, an overly narrow focus can mislead design decisions by overvaluing certain innovations, producing cores that excel on SPEC benchmarks yet underperform on broader, realistic workloads.
  To mitigate this overfitting, adopting a large, comprehensive benchmark suite is the natural solution. However, the immense engineering effort required to strip software into the clean, interference-free binary executables demanded by simulators often makes this highly impractical. In this work, we demonstrate that AI agents provide an elegant solution to this challenge. Rather than manually curating yet another static benchmark suite, we introduce an agent-driven workflow capable of autonomously transforming arbitrary open-source repositories into simulator-ready executables. This automated approach makes workload collection highly scalable, allowing us to rapidly harvest hundreds of diverse applications from public repositories into our benchmark suite. Through a comparative analysis of our agent-generated suite against SPEC, we show that it not only achieves higher-fidelity performance assessments but also uncovers novel architectural insights that traditional, static suites fail to expose.

### [4] [ReSAFT: An Efficient Stuck-at Fault-Tolerant Scheme for ReRAM-based Process-in-Memory Accelerators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09999)
*Aniseh Dorostkar,Hamed Farbeh,Hamid R. Zarandi*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 提出一种面向模拟ReRAM存内计算加速器的容错方案，结合冗余硬件与故障感知权重映射，以低冗余开销恢复CNN推理精度，并通过多目标优化确定最优冗余行列数。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 提出容错方案：包含冗余硬件解决方案与故障感知映射方法；分析不同冗余行/列数量对精度与设计指标的影响；将冗余行列数确定建模为多目标优化（MOO）问题并求解，权衡可靠性、面积、能耗与延迟；针对双crossbar结构提出故障感知权重映射以进一步补偿SAF引起的精度下降。

Conclusion: 所提冗余硬件结合故障感知映射的容错方案，可在较小冗余开销下有效补偿SAF导致的精度损失，并通过多目标优化在可靠性、面积、能耗与延迟之间取得折中，显著提升MTTF与能效/面积效率。

Abstract: Analog ReRAM-based process-in-memory (PIM) accelerators provide high parallelism and energy efficiency for deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) inference. Furthermore, a fault-aware weight mapping is proposed for dual-crossbar structures to further compensate for the accuracy degradation caused by SAFs. Simulation results show that, for the SimpleNet model using the MNIST dataset, the inference accuracy is recovered by approximately 22.39%, on average, across four configurations of optimal solutions, each offering a trade-off between reliability and area, energy consumption, and latency overheads. The mean-time-tofailure (MTTF) improves by about 61x on average compared to the baseline. These selected configurations also reduce energy and area overheads by 32%, on average, in comparison to row-only and column-only configurations.

### [6] [SUSpMV: A High Frequency Sparse Matrix Vector Multiplier on HBM Enabled FPGA written in SUS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10403)

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: SUSpMV 是基于新硬件描述语言 SUS 实现的稀疏矩阵向量乘（SpMV）加速器，利用 SUS 的延迟计数与推断机制构建深流水线，极少设计开销地驱动 Alveo U280 上全部 32 个 HBM 通道与 32 个计算单元，并采用密度自适应存储格式，实现 144.9 GFLOPs 峰值吞吐，较同平台前期工作几何平均提升 79%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
