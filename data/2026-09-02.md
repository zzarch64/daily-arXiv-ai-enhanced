### [1] [DynaNDE: Dynamic Near-Data Expert Scheduling for Batched MoE Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00407)
*Xiaoyang Lu,Belthangady Akash Vi Narayana Pai,Xian-He Sun*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: DynaNDE是一个动态近数据专家调度框架，利用NPU-NDP协同执行加速批量MoE推理，通过分析性能模型和复用感知运行时实现，相比现有方法在prefill和decoding阶段分别获得2.6倍和2.2倍的平均加速比。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: MoE模型在NPU系统上部署时存在显著的数据搬运开销，现有NPU-NDP MoE系统未能充分考虑硬件异构性、动态专家级并行度以及批量推理中的时间性专家复用，导致性能受限。

Method: DynaNDE提出：(1) 一个解析性能模型，捕捉NPU-NDP协同执行中的硬件异构性、数据搬运成本和通信-计算重叠；(2) 基于该模型逐层确定专家在NPU和NDP之间的调度分配，并考虑专家级并行度；(3) 一个复用感知运行时，当专家已驻留于NPU内存时避免冗余参数搬运。

Result: 实验结果表明，相比最先进的NPU-NDP MoE服务框架，DynaNDE在prefill阶段平均获得2.6倍加速，在decoding阶段平均获得2.2倍加速。

Conclusion: DynaNDE验证了通过精细建模硬件异构性和动态调度专家，并结合复用感知的运行时管理，可以有效释放NPU-NDP协同架构在批量MoE推理中的性能潜力。

Abstract: Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models enable efficient scaling of large language model (LLM) inference but suffer from substantial data-movement overhead when deployed on neural processing unit (NPU)-based systems. Near-Data Processing (NDP) provides a promising way to mitigate this bottleneck via cooperative NPU-NDP execution. However, existing NPU-NDP MoE systems do not fully account for hardware heterogeneity, dynamic expert-level concurrency, and temporal expert reuse during batched inference. This paper presents DynaNDE, a dynamic near-data expert scheduling framework that exploits NPU-NDP collaboration to accelerate batched MoE inference. DynaNDE introduces an analytical performance model that captures hardware heterogeneity, data-movement costs, and communication-computation overlap in cooperative NPU-NDP execution. Guided by this model, DynaNDE determines per-layer expert scheduling across the NPU and NDP while accounting for expert-level concurrency. DynaNDE also incorporates a reuse-aware runtime that avoids redundant parameter movement when experts reside in NPU memory. Experimental results show that DynaNDE achieves substantial throughput improvements over the state-of-the-art NPU-NDP MoE serving framework, with average speedups of 2.6$\times$ and 2.2$\times$ for the prefill and decoding stages, respectively.

### [2] [VARA: A Voltage-Aware ReRAM-Based Accelerator for Energy-Efficient Computing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00421)
*Peng Dang,Yintao He,Huawei Li*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种电压感知的ReRAM存算一体加速器VARA，通过电压感知训练算法增强激活稀疏性，并结合共零激活重排序方案实现交叉阵列级计算跳过，在仅带来微小精度损失的情况下，将系统平均总能耗降低60.12%，能量效率提升2.68倍。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有ReRAM加速器主要关注权重优化，而忽视了激活分布对模拟域矩阵向量乘法计算能耗的影响，导致激活稀疏性的节能潜力尚未被充分利用。

Method: 提出VARA加速器及配套设计方法学，包含两个关键技术：(1) 电压感知训练（VAT）算法，在激活函数中引入预设阈值将激活分布推向零值以增强稀疏性；(2) 共零激活重排序（CAR）方案，基于共零相关性对激活维度进行聚类，并同步重排激活矩阵与对应权重，将分散的零激活聚合成连续零值区域以最大化交叉阵列级计算跳过的收益。

Result: 实验结果显示，与基线相比，VARA以微小的精度损失为代价，将平均总系统能耗降低60.12%，平均系统能量效率提升2.68倍，优于现有的稀疏激活优化加速器。

Conclusion: 通过同时考虑激活和权重的分布特性，VARA有效释放了ReRAM存算一体架构中激活稀疏性的节能潜力，为后续IMC架构的能耗优化提供了新思路。

Abstract: ReRAM-based in-memory computing (IMC) architectures are widely regarded as a promising approach to alleviating the computational bottleneck of conventional architectures. Since ReRAM crossbars perform matrix-vector multiplication (MVM) in the analog domain, their computational energy consumption is highly dependent on weight and activation distributions. However, most existing ReRAM accelerators focus primarily on weight optimization while paying limited attention to the impact of activations on computational energy consumption, leaving the energy-saving potential of activation sparsity largely underexploited. In this paper, we propose a voltage-aware ReRAM-based accelerator (VARA), along with its accompanying design methodology. Specifically, we first introduce a voltage-aware training (VAT) algorithm that incorporates a preset threshold into the activation function to steer the activation distribution toward zero values, thereby enhancing activation sparsity. Building upon this, we further propose a co-zero activation reordering (CAR) scheme for crossbar-level computation skipping. CAR clusters activation dimensions based on their co-zero correlations and consistently reorders both the activation matrix and its corresponding weights. This process consolidates scattered zero activations into contiguous zero-valued regions to maximize the benefits of crossbar-level computation skipping. Extensive experimental results demonstrate that, with only marginal accuracy loss, VARA reduces the average total system energy consumption by 60.12\% and improves the average system energy efficiency by 2.68$\times$ compared to the baseline, outperforming existing state-of-the-art accelerators for sparse-activation optimization.

### [3] [LLM Inference on IMC-NoC Architecture with Balanced Dataflow and Fine-Grained Parallelism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00857)
*Yimin Wang,Yue Jiet Chong,Xuanyao Fong*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种名为LEAP的硬件-软件协同设计框架，通过统一分布式计算、内存和通信为无缝处理-通信结构，集成IMC PE（处理静态权重）、NMC PE（处理动态数据）和INC（处理部分结果归约），并配合面向LLM服务关键指标的划分、映射和调度框架，实现了相对于商业GPU平台≥1.52倍的吞吐量提升和24.91倍的能效提升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM推理服务对内存带宽、计算密度和通信效率提出了前所未有的要求。虽然存内计算(IMC)是解决内存墙问题的有前景方案，但LLM的异构数据动态性需要互补资源来处理运行时生成的中间数据。此外，LLM中海量参数需要扩展架构，而片上数据移动往往是主要性能瓶颈。

Method: 采用硬件-软件协同设计方法。硬件方面提出可扩展架构LEAP，集成三种处理单元：IMC PE处理静态权重、NMC PE处理动态数据、INC处理部分结果归约，每层硬件执行专门任务。软件方面提出面向LLM服务吞吐量和延迟等关键指标的划分、映射和调度框架。针对prefill和decode阶段的不同计算强度，提出prefill-decode分离方法，动态重构PE组织以最大化资源利用率。

Result: 与商业GPU平台相比，所提出架构在吞吐量上提升≥1.52倍，在能效上提升24.91倍。

Conclusion: 通过统一计算、内存和通信的处理-通信结构，以及针对prefill和decode阶段差异的动态PE重配置策略，LEAP架构在LLM推理场景中显著优于商业GPU平台，展现了硬件-软件协同设计在解决LLM推理瓶颈方面的巨大潜力。

Abstract: LLM inference has become an essential service, yet it imposes unprecedented demands on memory bandwidth, computational density, and communication efficiency. While IMC is a promising solution to the memory wall issue, the heterogeneous data dynamicity of LLM requires complementary resources to handle intermediate data generated during run-time. Furthermore, the massive number of parameters in LLM necessitates scale-up architectures where on-chip data movement is often the primary performance bottleneck. This article presents a hardware-software co-design framework that unifies distributed compute, memory, and communication into a seamless processing-communication fabric. On the hardware side, we propose a scalable architecture, named LEAP, that integrates IMC PE, NMC PE, and INC. This allows each hardware layer to execute specialized tasks: IMC for static weights, NMC for dynamic data, and INC for partial result reduction. On the software side, we introduce a partitioning, mapping, and scheduling framework optimized for key metrics in LLM serving, including throughput and latency. To address the distinct computational intensities of the prefill and decode phases, we present a prefill-decode disaggregation approach that dynamically reconfigures PE organizations to maximize resource utilization. Compared to commercial GPU platforms, the proposed architecture provides a throughput and an energy efficiency improvement of $\geq{}1.52\times$ and $24.91\times$, respectively.

### [4] [Hardware Acceleration of Block-Diffusion LLM for Edge Devices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01084)
*Wei-Hsing Huang,Kiseok Lee,Ming-Yen Lee,Weiyu Sun,Cheng-Jhih Shih,Gayatri Tanksali,Arpit Khandelwal,Pin-Jun Chen,Yingyan Celine Lin,Shimeng Yu*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出了一套用于边缘设备上扩散LLM（batch-one流式推理）的协同设计技术栈，包括WIFiV-LPDDR宽I/O内存系统、BRQ-KV前缀缓存压缩方案和DAT-FFN神经网络层压缩方案，并结合输入驻留的混合精度脉动阵列，在1.5B/7B模型上实现约3.79x/3.96x的算术平均能耗降低和2.88x/4.44x的延迟加速，同时模型基准分数下降小于1个百分点。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 单流（batch-one）边缘推理无法跨请求摊销权重的传输开销；全注意力扩散LLM每一步都要重算整个序列；原生块扩散虽使已完成的块不可变且可精确缓存，但精炼阶段仍需流式传输前缀KV和FFN权重，导致巨大的访存能耗与延迟开销。

Method: 提出三项协同设计：(1) WIFiV-LPDDR：支持精度标签读取的宽I/O LPDDR内存系统；(2) BRQ-KV：一种规范的低秩加INT8残差前缀表示，带查询相关的逐条目精度；(3) DAT-FFN：采用漂移映射的规范替换、邻级校正的低位增量或缓存状态沿用，保持活跃激活不量化。以上均映射到输入驻留的混合精度脉动阵列上。

Result: 在建模的Jetson级平台上评估1.5B/7B扩散LLM：完整技术栈提供3.79x/3.96x算术平均能耗降低因子和2.88x/4.44x算术平均延迟加速（在报告的DAT-FFN设置下）；每个对应的压缩模型基准分数相对基线下降均小于1个绝对百分点。

Conclusion: 该技术栈通过存储与计算协同设计，在不显著损失模型精度（基准分下降<1个百分点）的前提下，大幅降低了边缘扩散LLM推理的能耗与延迟，证明了对batch-one流式推理场景采用宽I/O混合精度架构是有效的优化方向。

Abstract: Single-stream (batch-one) edge inference cannot amortize weight traffic across requests. Full-attention diffusion LLMs recompute the entire sequence at every step; native block diffusion makes completed blocks immutable and exactly cacheable, yet refinement still streams prefix KV and FFN weights. We co-design WIFiV-LPDDR, a wide-I/O LPDDR system for precision-tagged reads, BRQ-KV for a canonical low-rank-plus-INT8-residual prefix with query-dependent per-entry precision, and DAT-FFN for drift-mapped canonical replacement, adjacent-stage-corrected low-bit delta, or cached-state carry while keeping live activations unquantized. Both map to an input-stationary mixed-precision systolic array. For the evaluated 1.5B/7B models on modeled Jetson-class platforms, the full stack provides arithmetic-mean energy-reduction factors of 3.79x/3.96x and arithmetic-mean latency speedups of 2.88x/4.44x at the reported DAT-FFN settings; every corresponding compressed model-benchmark score drops by less than one absolute percentage point from its baseline.

</details>


### [5] [Performance Characterization of SPEC CPU 2026 on AMD EPYC 9755 Processor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01527)
*Kunal Kashyap,Rajiv Ramanathan,Shayantika Bhattacharya*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文首次在AMD EPYC "Zen 5"微架构上对SPEC CPU 2026基准测试套件进行基于微架构的性能特征分析，引入"规模分析"方法对比单副本与全系统行为，识别出三类行为簇（前端控制流密集型、高计算效率型、内存带宽受限型），并揭示了仅在全系统利用时才显现的SMT调度争用和L3容量干扰等规模依赖效应。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: SPEC CPU 2026是自2017年以来行业标准CPU基准套件的首次重大更新，但尚未有研究对其在新一代微架构上进行深入的性能特征分析；同时，传统特征分析方法只能揭示单副本层面的行为，无法暴露仅在系统全负载下才显现的瓶颈。本研究旨在填补这一空白，为下一代数据中心处理器的架构研究和负载驱动设计决策建立经验基础。

Method: 采用"多镜头"（multi-lens）方法论，涵盖流水线效率、控制流行为、缓存层级压力及指令混合等多个分析维度；同时分析了SPECrate和SPECspeed两套基准。创新性地引入"规模分析"（scale analysis），对比单副本运行与全系统运行的行为差异，以暴露传统特征分析方法无法识别的系统级瓶颈。

Result: 分析揭示了套件内部显著的多样性，并通过多镜头分析识别出三类截然不同的行为簇：(1) 前端控制流密集型负载，主要考验分支预测器的吞吐量而非准确性；(2) 高效计算密集型负载，在规模扩展时遭受SMT争用；(3) 内存带宽受限负载，即使在单副本场景下L3过滤效果也很差。规模依赖效应（包括SMT分发争用导致的吞吐率降低和L3容量干扰）仅在全系统利用时才显现。

Conclusion: 该研究建立了SPEC CPU 2026在Zen 5微架构上的经验特征基准，发现的规模依赖效应证明了仅依赖传统单副本特征分析在揭示系统级瓶颈方面的局限性，为面向下一代数据中心处理器的架构研究和以负载驱动的设计决策提供了重要实证依据。

Abstract: SPEC CPU 2026 is the first major update to the industry-standard CPU benchmark suite since 2017. This paper presents the first microarchitecture based performance characterization of the new suite, conducted on AMD EPYC "Zen 5", also the first SPEC CPU characterization study on this microarchitecture. Using a multi-lens methodology spanning pipeline efficiency, control flow behavior, cache hierarchy pressure, and instruction mix, we analyze both SPECrate and SPECspeed suites. We introduce scale analysis, comparing single-copy to full-system behavior to expose system-level bottlenecks invisible to conventional characterization. Our analysis reveals substantial behavioral diversity across the suite, and the multi-lens analysis identifies three distinct behavioral clusters: frontend control-flow-dominated workloads that stress branch predictor throughput rather than accuracy, high-efficiency compute workloads that suffer SMT contention at scale, and memory bandwidth-bound workloads with poor L3 filtering even at single-copy. Scale-dependent effects, including SMT dispatch contention causing throughput reduction and L3 capacity interference, emerge only at full system utilization. This work establishes an empirical foundation for architectural research and workload-driven design decisions targeting next-generation datacenter processors.
