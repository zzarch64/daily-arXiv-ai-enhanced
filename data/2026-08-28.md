### [1] [Ankhdjet: An Open-Source Compiler for Mask-Programmed Ternary Compute-in-ROM on an Open PDK](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26206)
*Mohnish Pai*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出了Ankhdjet，一个完全开源的编译器，可将HuggingFace三元量化模型（BitNet b1.58）编译为开放SKY130 PDK上固定compute-in-ROM宏的via-mask程序，实现从权重到掩膜的端到端验证。这是首个在可制造开放PDK上进行完整开源工具链签核的weights-to-mask compute-in-ROM编译器，也是首个提交制造的开源PDK compute-in-ROM宏。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM推理主要由权重搬运主导（每生成一个token需要重读所有权重）。三元量化（BitNet b1.58）将权重压缩至1.58比特，在20亿参数规模达到与全精度相当的效果，使得将权重硬编码进只读掩膜变得可行。尽管商用芯片已验证硬编码权重，但缺乏完全开放的、可复现的模型专用硅片开发流程，本文旨在实现这一点。

Method: 该方法包括：1) 一个开源的编译器，将HuggingFace三元检查点（BitNet b1.58及其变体）转换为SKY130 PDK上compute-in-ROM宏的via-mask程序；2) 完全数字化的读取方案——全摆幅位线采样至标准单元，可通过综合将发射端口移植到任何节点，替代了原始模拟比较器读出方案（面积仅为后者的八分之一）；3) 逐比特级别的对抗性验证方法学，用于捕获DRC不可见的静默短路。

Result: 在完整开源工具链签核下完成两次流片：KLayout DRC零错误、netgen LVS零错误、时序干净，两次使用不同权重矩阵、通过相同流程仅掩膜不同。读取能量通过抽取寄生仿真测得为0.98-1.73 pJ/权重。已提交TinyTapeout/ChipFoundry SKY130 shuttle（ttsky26c）制造，预计2027年获得硅片。同时报告了在130nm工艺下每晶体管存储两个三元权重的负结果，以及各工艺节点下可行的模拟感应技术。

Conclusion: 本文证明了模型专用硅片可以完全通过开源基础设施实现可复现制造，并建立了首个端到端的权重到掩膜的开源编译流程。作者明确声明不声称有能量测量数据，制造的载体仅为演示用途。该工作为开放、可复现的AI专用芯片设计奠定了基线。

Abstract: Large-language-model inference is dominated by weight movement: every generated token re-reads every weight. Ternary quantization (BitNet b1.58) shrinks each weight to 1.58 bits with reported parity at the 2B-parameter scale, small enough that hardwiring the weights into a read-only mask becomes plausible, and a commercial chip (Taalas HC1) has validated hardwired weights on an advanced node behind closed tooling. This paper asks whether model-specific silicon can be made reproducible with entirely open infrastructure. We present Ankhdjet, an open-source compiler that lowers a HuggingFace ternary checkpoint (BitNet b1.58 and its kin) to a via-mask program of a fixed compute-in-ROM macro on the open SKY130 PDK, verified end to end with open tools. We defend two claims: (1) the first open-source weights-to-mask compute-in-ROM compiler on a fabricable open PDK, taken through full open-toolchain signoff (KLayout DRC zero, netgen LVS zero, clean timing) twice with two different weight matrices through an identical flow in which only the mask differs; and (2) the first compute-in-ROM macro submitted for fabrication on an open PDK (TinyTapeout/ChipFoundry SKY130 shuttle ttsky26c, silicon expected 2027). The compiler's read is fully digital (full-swing bitline sampling into standard cells, so the emission ports to any node by synthesis); an analog comparator readout of the same machine is retained as a measured variant, replaced by the digital readout at one eighth the area. We additionally report the per-level adversarial verification methodology that caught three DRC-invisible silent shorts, a negative result on storing two ternary weights per transistor at 130 nm, and which analog sense techniques survive at which nodes. Read energy is from extracted-parasitic simulation (0.98-1.73 pJ per sensed weight); no energy measurements are claimed, and the fabricated vehicles are demonstrators.

### [2] [MeshReduce-U: Compiler-Guided Communication Reduction for Irregular Neural Reductions on Mesh NoCs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26220)
*Amirreza Khorasanian*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: MeshReduce-U是一个面向网格NoC空间加速器的编译指导通信缩减与路由框架，通过在路由前重写可归约的神经网络通信（合并共置源、形成局部聚合岛、阻塞兼容扇入结构的信道、选择容量可行的汇点、融合使用感知成本路由）来消除流量，在20个工作负载上相比基线平均降低延迟40.3%、TLU 56.0%、FusedTLU 48.7%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 许多不规则的神经网络工作负载会引发偏斜的多对一归约，具有重复邻域和非局部通信。传统NoC映射器仅针对固定通信图优化布局和路由，而忽略了结合性归约在路由前就提供了合法的流量消除机会。

Method: MeshReduce-U包含四个关键步骤：1) 归并共置源（coalesces colocated sources）；2) 形成局部聚合岛（local aggregation islands）；3) 阻塞具有兼容扇入结构的信道；4) 选择容量可行的汇点，并使用融合使用感知成本对剩余固定宽度载体进行路由。此外，采用确定性路由回放模型分别报告调度派生的通信延迟、总链路使用（TLU）和融合链路使用（FusedTLU）。

Result: 在20个工作负载的神经网络测试集上，相比ABC风格源聚合基线，平均降低延迟40.3%、TLU 56.0%、FusedTLU 48.7%，且三个指标在所有工作负载上均有改进。在40个合成不规则归约上，平均降低延迟12.3%和TLU 19.7%。30实例逐遍研究表明结构重写将全局载体数量降低60.9%，回放延迟降低63.0%。

Conclusion: 在路由前重写可归约的神经网络通信，比在未缩减的流量图上进行更深入的搜索更有效。MeshReduce-U通过编译指导的通信缩减框架，证明了将归约语义纳入NoC映射可以显著提升空间加速器上的不规则神经网络工作负载性能。

Abstract: Many irregular neural workloads induce skewed many-to-one reductions with repeated neighborhoods and nonlocal communication. Conventional NoC mappers optimize placement and routes for a fixed communication graph, even though associative reductions expose legal opportunities to eliminate traffic before routing. We present MeshReduce-U, a compiler-guided communication-reduction and routing framework for mesh-NoC-based spatial accelerators.
  MeshReduce-U coalesces colocated sources, forms local aggregation islands, blocks channels with compatible fan-in structure, selects capacity-feasible sinks, and routes the remaining fixed-width carriers using fused usage-aware costs. A deterministic route-replay model reports schedule-derived communication latency, total link usage (TLU), and fused link usage (FusedTLU) separately.
  Across a 20-workload lowerable neural-network zoo, MeshReduce-U reduces mean latency, TLU, and FusedTLU by 40.3%, 56.0%, and 48.7%, respectively, relative to an ABC-style source-aggregation baseline, improving all three metrics on every workload. Across 40 synthetic irregular reductions, it reduces mean latency and TLU by 12.3% and 19.7%. A new 30-instance pass-by-pass study further shows that the structural rewrites reduce the global carrier count by 60.9% and replay latency by 63.0%. These results show that rewriting reducible neural communication before routing can be more effective than searching harder over an unreduced traffic graph.

### [3] [Beyond Edge Cuts: Activity-Weighted Multicast Hypergraph Mapping for Spiking Neural Networks on Mesh NoCs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26223)
*Amirreza Khorasanian*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出M-HySMap，一种路由感知、活动加权的多播超图映射框架，用于将尖峰神经网络（SNN）映射到神经形态多核平台。通过将每个源神经元建模为到其后突触扇出集的超边并按活动性加权，结合活动感知的图/QAP种子初始化和精确增量增益评估，在115个作业上相比基线方法将路由多播跳数降低10.6%~41.1%，并将精化速度提升4.7~12.7倍。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有SNN到神经形态多核平台的映射通常采用图划分和成对放置成本抽象，但这种抽象与底层物理通信事件不匹配：单个尖峰从源神经元被传递到一组突触后目的地，且到多个目的地的路由可在网格链路上共享。传统的成对成本模型无法捕捉多播通信和多链路共享的真实情况，导致映射质量受限于不准确的成本模型。

Method: M-HySMap的核心方法包括：(1) 将每个源神经元建模为指向其后突触扇出集的超边（有向超图），并按活动性（spike频率）加权；(2) 从活动感知的图/QAP种子开始初始化映射；(3) 优化不同目标核心的扇出、确定性网格路由的并集以及链路拥塞；(4) 利用"局部性"观测（移动一个神经元只影响其自身源根超边及其前驱的超边）实现精确增量增益评估，同时缓存所有不受影响的路由贡献；(5) 推导保守的放置下界，并设计分区与放置邻域的迁移策略组合以保留最优解。

Result: 在115个作业的验证集上（Potjans启发的递归SNN和4×4至6×6的网格NoC，外加7×7压力测试），M-HySMap相比Activity+QAP将路由多播跳数降低10.6%~19.6%，相比Edge+QAP降低19.7%~41.1%；增量更新使精化加速4.7~12.7倍，同时与全量重计算的数值精度完全一致。

Conclusion: M-HySMap通过超图抽象精确刻画了SNN多播通信的本质，并利用局部性实现了高效的增量优化，在映射质量和计算效率上均显著优于现有基于成对成本模型的方法，为神经形态系统上的SNN部署提供了更准确高效的映射方案。

Abstract: Mapping spiking neural networks (SNNs) onto neuromorphic many-core platforms is often formulated with graph partitioning and pairwise placement costs. That abstraction is convenient, but it does not match the physical communication event: one spike from a source neuron is delivered to a set of postsynaptic destinations, and routes to several destinations can share mesh links. We present M-HySMap, a route-aware, activity-weighted multicast hypergraph mapping framework. Each source neuron induces a directed hyperedge to its postsynaptic fanout, weighted by profiled activity. The mapper starts from strong activity-aware graph/QAP seeds and then optimizes distinct destination-core fanout, the union of deterministic mesh routes, and link congestion. The central algorithmic observation is locality: moving one neuron can change only its own source-rooted hyperedge and the hyperedges of its predecessors. This permits exact incremental gain evaluation while caching every unaffected route contribution. We expose this combinatorial structure in detail, derive a conservative placement lower bound, and describe a portfolio of partition and placement neighborhoods that preserves the best incumbent. Across a 115-job evidence suite on Potjans-inspired recurrent SNNs and mesh NoCs from 4 x 4 to 6 x 6, plus a 7 x 7 stress case, M-HySMap reduces routed multicast hops by 10.6-19.6% over Activity+QAP and 19.7-41.1% over Edge+QAP. Incremental updates accelerate refinement by 4.7-12.7x while matching full recomputation to numerical precision.

### [4] [Redwood: A Frontier AI Accelerator Designed, Verified, and Deployed from Scratch in 2 Weeks by AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26418)

Main category: cs.AR

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代AI工作负载与硬件演进的时间尺度严重不匹配：芯片架构定义比大规模量产早数年，而目标工作负载数月就会变化。设计决策在深度不确定性下做出，并且要付出双重代价——既为对冲不确定性而增加通用性，又因新工作负载在冻结硅片上映射不佳而受损。随着摩尔定律趋于停滞，专用化成为性能功耗比的主要来源，需要在工作负载变化的节奏下运行设计周期。

Conclusion: 据作者所知，这是首个由AI系统端到端设计、达到生产级别并成功运行现代AI模型的AI加速器。该工作展示了AI驱动硬件设计在实践中的可行性，并朝着递归自我改进方向迈出了初步但重要的步伐。

Abstract: Modern AI workloads and the hardware that runs them evolve on different timescales: architectural definition precedes volume silicon by years, while target workloads shift in months. Design decisions are therefore committed under deep uncertainty and paid for twice, once in the generality added as a hedge, and again when new workloads map poorly onto frozen silicon. As Moore's Law stagnates, specialization is the main remaining source of performance-per-watt and demands a design cycle that runs at the cadence of the workloads. We present an end-to-end AI system that collapses the software-to-silicon stack into a single optimization loop, where hardware and software are co-designed and verified under one objective. Specification changes were reverified and redeployed to hardware in under 48 hours. Redwood Nano, its ultra-low-power FPGA variant, runs multi-billion-parameter models like Llama and Qwen. Projected onto Samsung 8 nm, the Jetson Orin Nano's process class, Redwood delivers 1.75x the throughput at 1.9x lower power, a 3.4x performance-per-watt gain against a measured Jetson baseline on the same models. Qwen running on Redwood also helped design next-generation Redwood, an early step toward recursive self-improvement. To our knowledge, this is the first production-worthy AI accelerator designed end-to-end by an AI system and running a modern AI model.
