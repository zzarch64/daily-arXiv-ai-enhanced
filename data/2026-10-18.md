### [1] [A LoRa-Compatible Receiver with Programmable Noise-Power Trade-Off and Integrated Wake-Up Mode](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20677)
*Marco Gonzalez,Pol Maistriaux,Jérôme Louveaux,David Bol*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 提出首个兼容LoRa、集成唤醒模式且具有可编程噪声-功耗折衷的接收机，在4.57 mW下实现-138 dBm灵敏度，可节省62.6%功耗（代价8.3 dB灵敏度下降）；唤醒模式下以1.5 mW、32 ms延迟达到-126 dBm灵敏度，功耗比主接收机最低噪声配置低3倍。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 可编程接收机可适应无线信道的多变条件。对噪声-功耗折衷的深入分析有助于构建内嵌唤醒电路的主接收机，从而实现唤醒接收机（WuRX）与射频接收机的无缝集成，避免系统在功耗、面积、复杂度和成本上的额外开销。

Method: 对接收机的噪声-功耗折衷进行扩展分析，并据此设计一款将唤醒电路嵌入主接收机的LoRa兼容架构，支持主接收模式与唤醒模式两种工作状态，其中噪声-功耗折衷可编程配置。

Result: 主接收模式下，功耗4.57 mW时灵敏度为-138 dBm，可在灵敏度下降8.3 dB的代价下最多节省62.6%功耗；唤醒模式下，32 ms延迟、1.5 mW功耗时灵敏度达-126 dBm。由于锁相环（PLL）设计欠优，唤醒模式功耗仍相对偏高，但仍比主接收机最低噪声配置低约3倍。

Conclusion: 集成唤醒模式的LoRa兼容接收机验证了噪声-功耗可编程折衷的可行性与系统级优势；后续可通过优化PLL设计进一步降低唤醒模式功耗。

Abstract: Programmable receivers allow an optimal performance in the variable conditions of the wireless channel. An extensive analysis of their noise-power trade-off enables the development of a main receiver with embedded wake-up circuitry. This makes the seamless integration of wake-up receivers (WuRXs) in RF receivers possible, avoiding power, area, complexity, and cost overheads in the system. In this paper, we propose the first LoRa-compatible receiver with an integrated wake-up mode and a programmable noise-power trade-off. In main-receiver mode, it consumes 4.57 mW for a sensitivity of -138 dBm, but it can save up to 62.6% of power consumption at the cost of an 8.3-dB sensitivity degradation. In wake-up mode, sensitivity down to -126 dBm is achieved for a 32-ms latency and 1.5 mW of power consumption. The power consumption remains relatively high due to a sub-optimal design of the the phase-locked loop (PLL). In the end, the wake-up mode consumes 3X less than the lowest-noise configuration in main-receiver mode.

### [2] [Helix: A Unified Programmable Accelerator for Diverse Krylov Subspace Solvers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21096)
*Wei Chen,Yizhi Yuan,Linghao Song*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: Helix 是一种面向 Krylov 子空间方法算法族的 FPGA 加速器，通过抽象可编程原语内核与可编程数据通路，实现单一架构支持八种求解器、零切换延迟，并在 U55C 上相较现有 FPGA 加速器取得显著速度与能效优势。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 领域专用加速器性能与能效高，但缺乏对可扩展算法族的支持；现有 Krylov 子空间方法 FPGA 加速器通常仅支持单一求解器（如 JPCG）或需重新配置硬件，通用性不足。

Method: 提出系统化的算法族设计方法：先从多种 Krylov 求解器中抽象出公共计算原语，围绕高效可编程原语内核构建架构，再引入可编程数据通路协调原语执行；架构解耦硬件实现与求解器特定执行，实现 HBM 复用与零求解器切换延迟；并提供 DSL 编译器将不同求解器映射到架构上，同时针对各求解器优化向量运算性能。

Result: 在 Xilinx Alveo U55C 上原型验证并编译执行八种求解器：相较仅支持 JPCG 的 Xilinx XcgSolver 与 SOTA FPGA 加速器 Callipepla 分别取得最高 7.91x 和 1.16x 加速；在八种求解器上达到 NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 GPU 吞吐的 47.5%–96.9%，能效高 2.29x–4.66x，单位峰值内存带宽吞吐高 1.89x–3.85x。

Conclusion: Helix 以可编程性换取算法族通用支持，可编程组件仅带来 0.25%–0.57% 执行时间开销及 4.7% LUT、4.0% FF 资源开销，开销可忽略，验证了架构的实用性与高效性。

Abstract: Domain-specific accelerators deliver high performance and energy efficiency through specialization but often lack support for extensible algorithm families. Existing FPGA accelerators for Krylov subspace methods, which iteratively solve sparse linear systems, typically support a single solver or require separate hardware configurations. Helix follows a systematic algorithm-family design methodology that first abstracts common computational primitives from diverse Krylov solvers and builds the architecture around efficient, programmable primitive kernels. Helix then introduces programmable datapaths to coordinate these primitives. Its architecture decouples hardware implementation from solver-specific execution, enabling HBM reuse and zero solver-switching latency. Finally, Helix provides a DSL compiler that efficiently maps different solvers onto the architecture while optimizing vector-operation performance for each solver. We prototype Helix on a Xilinx Alveo U55C FPGA and evaluate Helix's practicality and performance by using it to compile and execute eight solvers. Helix achieves up to 7.91x and 1.16x speedups over Xilinx XcgSolver and the state-of-the-art FPGA accelerator Callipepla, respectively, both of which support only JPCG. Across the eight solvers, Helix sustains 47.5% - 96.9% of the throughput of an NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 GPU while delivering 2.29x - 4.66x higher energy efficiency and 1.89x - 3.85x higher throughput per unit peak memory bandwidth. The programmable components incur 0.25% - 0.57% execution-time overhead and 4.7% LUT and 4.0% FF overhead, demonstrating negligible overhead for programmability.

</details>


### [3] [PSA: A Pruning-Friendly Sparse-Aware Accelerator for Efficient Sparse Convolution in SNNs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21313)
*Sanhe Fu,Kainan Wang,Bo Wang,Aili Wang*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: PSA 是一款面向脉冲神经网络（SNN）的剪枝友好、稀疏感知加速器，通过 SAMA 数据流与 SIG-im2col 卷积降维机制高效执行不规则稀疏卷积，在 ResNet-18/CIFAR-10 上相较稠密基线实现 4.45x 能效提升与 18.52x 的 EDP 降低。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: SNN 的事件驱动稀疏计算具备高能效潜力，神经元剪枝可进一步减少冗余运算；但在实际硬件加速器中，脉冲稀疏性与剪枝稀疏性导致工作负载高度不规则，现有方法无法同时支持稀疏输入执行、低开销稀疏卷积降维（lowering）以及剪枝友好的执行。

Method: 提出 PSA 加速器，包含三项核心设计：(1) Sparse-Aware Merge-Accumulation（SAMA）数据流，对齐稀疏脉冲索引，将不规则激活转化为连续计算流以高效执行稀疏卷积；(2) Sparse Implicit Group-wise im2col（SIG-im2col）卷积降维机制，在片上以低映射成本与低内存开销将稀疏卷积转换为 SpMM；(3) 轻量级剪枝友好门控机制，直接支持已剪枝 SNN。

Result: 在 ResNet-18/CIFAR-10 上，PSA 相较稠密基线实现 4.45 倍能效提升，能量-延迟积（EDP）降低 18.52 倍。

Conclusion: 通过数据流对齐、稀疏卷积降维与剪枝友好门控的协同设计，PSA 有效解决了 SNN 中脉冲与剪枝双重稀疏带来的不规则执行难题，显著提升了能效与能量-延迟表现。

Abstract: Spiking neural networks (SNNs) are promising for energy-efficient artificial intelligence due to their event-driven sparse computation, and neuron pruning can further reduce redundant operations. However, in practical hardware accelerators, spike sparsity and pruning-induced sparsity produce highly irregular sparse workloads that are difficult to execute efficiently. Existing approaches do not jointly support sparse-input execution, low-overhead sparse convolution lowering and pruning-friendly execution. This paper presents PSA, a Pruning-friendly Sparse-aware Accelerator for efficient sparse convolution in SNNs. PSA features a Sparse-Aware Merge-Accumulation (SAMA) dataflow that aligns sparse spike indices and converts irregular activations into continuous computation streams for efficient sparse convolution execution. It further introduces a Sparse Implicit Group-wise im2col (SIG-im2col) convolution lowering mechanism that converts sparse convolution into SpMM on-chip with low mapping cost and memory overhead. In addition, PSA integrates a lightweight pruning-friendly gating mechanism to directly support pruned SNNs. Results on ResNet-18/CIFAR-10 show that PSA achieves a 4.45x improvement in energy efficiency and an 18.52x reduction in energy-delay product (EDP) over the dense baseline.

### [4] [Minimum RISC-V: Maximum FPGA Efficiency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21552)
*Martin Langhammer,Bogdan Pasca,Gregg Baeckler*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 该文设计了一个可在中端FPGA上运行于700 MHz以上、理想条件下每时钟退休一条指令的轻量深流水RISC-V核，并提出将应用二进制与专用处理器实例同时编译的协同优化方法，通过应用特定指令编码与冗余逻辑裁剪，得到仍保持汇编兼容但不再二进制兼容通用RISC-V的精简核，最小仅170 ALM，在Agilex-5上时序收敛于775 MHz，且可确定性布点、持续流水执行，支撑可预测的多核密度规划。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: FPGA厂商已普遍用RISC-V替代专有软核CPU，但现代FPGA资源丰富而软处理器系统（含存储器）占用面积过大、性能却相对器件潜力偏低，存在"面积-性能"失衡问题。

Method: 先设计轻量深流水RISC-V核（>700 MHz、IPC理想值为1）；再提出应用二进制与专用处理器实例同步编译的优化方法：将每条指令扩展为应用特定编码，在综合阶段移除未使用的数据通路与译码逻辑，产物保持汇编兼容但放弃通用二进制兼容；在RV32I、RV32M、RV32F配置上实现示例设计。

Result: 优化后核最小仅170 ALM，在Altera Agilex-5上时序收敛于775 MHz；此类处理器可打包进确定性面积并连续"step-and-repeat"部署而性能无实质损失。

Conclusion: 面向应用的指令编码与处理器协同定制能显著压缩面积并保持高频性能，且面积可确定性预测，为多核密度规划提供可预测性。

Abstract: RISC-V processors are now widely available for FPGAs, with most FPGA vendors replacing their proprietary soft-CPU architectures with this instruction-set architecture (ISA). Although contemporary FPGAs are increasingly resource- and feature-rich, a soft processor system---including memory---often occupies a disproportionately large footprint and delivers only modest performance relative to the potential of the target device. In this work we first design a lightweight but deeply pipelined RISC-V core that can run above 700 MHz in a mid-range device and that is capable of retiring one instruction per clock under ideal conditions. We then develop and demonstrate a new optimization method that compiles the final application binary and a specialized processor instance contemporaneously. The method expands each instruction into an application-specific encoding, removes unused datapath and decode logic during synthesis, and yields cores that remain assembler-compatible with the original program while no longer being binary-compatible with a general-purpose RISC-V. We implement example designs on RV32I (base integer), RV32M (integer multiply), and RV32F (single-precision floating-point) configurations. Our optimized RISC-V is as small as 170 ALMs and closes timing at 775 MHz in an Altera Agilex-5 device. Finally, we show that these processors can be packed into a deterministic area and stepped-and-repeated continuously without material impact on performance, enabling predictable multi-core density planning.

</details>


### [5] [Independent Data-Integrity Verification for LLM-Driven Functional Coverage Closure in Hardware Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21570)
*Venkata Krishna Kumar Vedantam*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文描述一个小型、与设计无关的覆盖率闭合（coverage-closure）LLM智能体：从仿真自身导出的cocotb-coverage YAML报告中读取覆盖率而非采信模型自述，并用独立于模型的scoreboard校验数据完整性；在异步FIFO、UART发送器、AHB2APB桥三个架构迥异的RTL设计上，均于8次迭代预算内将覆盖率由91.7%、88.9%、67.4%提升至100%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM智能体可编写定向测试以闭合功能覆盖率，但覆盖率仅表明某状态被到达，并不保证沿途数据正确；作者明确指出这是验证领域的长期共识，并非本文新贡献，其动机是把这一原则真正落实到LLM循环中：覆盖率只看仿真报告、数据正确性只由独立scoreboard判定。同时作者坦诚证据薄弱（仅三设计、无基线、无消融、日志来自多个智能体检查点），以严谨态度界定结论边界。

Method: 采用单一设计无关的覆盖率闭合智能体：覆盖率指标从仿真器导出的cocotb-coverage YAML报告直接读取，杜绝模型"自报"；数据完整性由独立于模型的reactive scoreboard监控器检查。在异步FIFO、UART发送器与AHB2APB桥三类架构不同的RTL设计上运行，迭代预算设为8次，记录并统计各次运行结果。此外，将scoreboard监控器挂接到FIFO的基础testbench上做验证性使用。

Result: 在全部记录运行中，所有设计均在8次迭代内达到100%覆盖率（起始分别为91.7%、88.9%、67.4%）；挂接于FIFO基础testbench的反应式scoreboard监控器发现了作者自身激励代码中的一处跨时钟域（CDC）缺陷。需注意：该监控器尚未接入LLM生成的测试，故这些测试的数据完整性并无独立校验；仅在单个FIFO测试上做了一次人工检查，属初步结果。

Conclusion: 小型设计无关的LLM智能体可在有限迭代内将覆盖率闭合至100%，且以仿真报告为唯一覆盖率来源、以独立scoreboard为数据完整性判据的框架可实际暴露真实缺陷（如CDC bug）。但证据存在明显局限：仅三个设计、缺乏基线与消融实验、日志来自多个智能体检查点，且数据完整性校验尚未覆盖LLM生成测试，结论应视为初步与示例性而非严格证明。

Abstract: LLM agents can write directed tests that close functional coverage, but coverage only shows that a state was reached, not that the data on the way there was correct. This is a long-known point in verification and we do not claim it as new. We describe a small coverage-closure agent that applies it to an LLM loop: coverage is read from the report that the simulation itself exports (cocotb-coverage YAML) and never taken from the model's account, and data integrity is checked by scoreboards that are separate from the model. On three architecturally distinct RTL designs (an asynchronous FIFO, a UART transmitter and an AHB2APB bridge) the agent raised coverage from 91.7%, 88.9% and 67.4% to 100% within its 8-iteration budget in every logged run, using one design-agnostic agent. A reactive scoreboard monitor attached to the FIFO's base testbench also found a clock-domain-crossing bug in the author's own stimulus code. The monitor is not yet attached to the LLM-generated tests, so their data integrity is not independently checked; one manual check on a single FIFO test is reported as preliminary. We list the weaknesses of this evidence: three designs, no baseline, no ablation, and logged runs that come from several agent checkpoints.

### [6] [Compiling Multi-Threshold Operators for Quantized Non-Monotonic and Fine-Granular Activations in Custom-Tailored DNN Accelerators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21825)
*Christoph Berganski,Felix Jentzsch,Marco Platzner*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 该论文将多阈值表示形式化推广，以支持非单调激活函数和多维（细粒度）阈值，并提出基于代数规则的自动化转换方法，集成到ONNX编译器中，在FINN框架上实现FPGA综合验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 神经网络激进量化会使激活函数成为硬件加速的瓶颈。将量化激活实现为多阈值操作是一种有前景的优化，但现有实现局限于单调递增函数、一维（逐通道）量化粒度以及少量预定义的激活-量化器组合，限制了适用性。

Method: 形式化多阈值表示，将其扩展到非单调函数和细粒度参数（多维阈值）；通过代数规则构建系统化转换方法，将量化函数组合自动转换为阈值表示，并集成到基于ONNX的编译器基础设施中；扩展FINN框架中基于二分查找的多阈值算子，用于定制FPGA加速器的端到端综合。

Abstract: Aggressive quantization of neural networks can expose activation functions as bottlenecks for efficient hardware acceleration. Implementing quantized activations as multi-threshold operations has been shown to be a promising optimization, allowing for efficient hardware implementation of few-bit activations. For automated conversion of quantized function compositions into threshold representation, we propose a systematic approach via algebraic rules integrated into the ONNX-based compiler infrastructure. We experimentally validate our solution on a selection of monotonic and non-monotonic functions by extending the binary search based multi-threshold operator that is integrated into the FINN framework for end-to-end synthesis of custom-tailored FPGA accelerators. We compare our method to prior work as well as the approach taken by the hls4ml framework. As an outlook, we demonstrate the prospects of using the multi-threshold representation during the training of a neural network as a form of quantization aware training.

</details>
