Main category: cs.AR

Across nine translation-heavy workloads, LeafPack improves geometric-mean harmonic speedup on the small and server cores by 28.7% and 28.4% over our baseline shared cache, and by 18.7% and 17.7% over an SRRIP baseline. Across twenty unseen held-out mixes evaluated after freezing the controller, LeafPack speeds up every mix with geometric-mean gains of 21.4% and 18.5%.
