### [1] [Budgeting Bytes: A Windowed Storage Roofline and Dual-Budget Architecture Ablations for Storage-Bound LLM Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04238)
*Hanhaodi Zhang*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出将"每token字节数"作为自回归解码的一等设计轴，并构建基于地址确定性分类的预取调度框架。作者发现，在带宽受限的边缘硬件上，即使具备完美预取的预言机也无法突破字节量瓶颈，真正的解决途径是压缩每token字节数使模型装入快速存储层。实验表明，Qwen3-30B-A3B经量化后在16GB统一内存设备上GPU常驻解码达11.5 tok/s（22倍加速），同时证明专家可预测性（91.2%）并不等于吞吐提升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有自回归解码优化多关注FLOPs和计算效率，但在廉价硬件上，实际瓶颈是每次生成的token所需移动的字节数（受限于内存层次中最慢的存储层带宽）。作者希望将字节传输视为核心设计维度，并系统化地理解可预测性与实际吞吐之间的鸿沟。

Method: 1) 提出地址确定性分类法（A0-A3），根据token正向传播中参数取址地址何时可知来分类参数；2) 将预取调度映射为带释放时间的单机可行性问题，推导出闭合形式的窗口化roofline模型；3) 在不同规模（<100M）进行双预算（每token字节×存储）消融实验；4) 在真实大MoE部署上验证框架预测，对比多种预取策略（时间局部性预取、迹驱动预言机）与量化压缩方案。

Result: 在8GB边缘板上，Qwen3-30B-A3B（4-bit, 18GB）超RAM，解码被eMMC带宽钉死；时间局部性预取为负收益，即便是完美预测的预言机也无济于事，因为瓶颈是饱和总线上的字节总量。将模型量化以适配16GB统一内存设备后，GPU常驻运行达11.5 tok/s（22倍）。冻结模型探针可从注意力前状态以91.2%准确率预测专家路由，但在A100 PCIe卸载路径上该可预测性转化为吞吐，而在带宽受限的边缘存储上失败。

Conclusion: 可预测性不等于加速：只有当快速存储层缓存了大部分模型且每次token传输量与计算量可比时，预取才有效。核心设计杠杆是压缩每token字节数使模型适配快速层，而非依赖预取预测。本文绘制了预取增益消失与起作用的条件边界。

Abstract: Autoregressive decoding on cheap hardware is bound not by FLOPs but by the bytes each generated token must move across the slowest populated tier of a memory hierarchy. We treat bytes-per-token as a first-class design axis, organized by an address-determinism taxonomy that classifies parameters by when their fetch address becomes known during a token's forward pass (A0: at token sampling; A1: before attention; A2: layerwise data-dependent; A3: always read). This reduces prefetch scheduling to single-machine feasibility with release times, yielding a closed-form windowed roofline. We run dual-budget (bytes-per-token times storage) ablations across three sub-100M scales, then take the framework to real large-MoE deployment and report a substantial negative result the roofline predicts: on an 8GB edge board running Qwen3-30B-A3B (4-bit, 18GB), the model overflows RAM and decode is pinned at the eMMC bandwidth ceiling; predictive expert prefetch does not help -- not temporal-locality prefetch (net-negative), not even a trace-driven oracle with perfect prediction -- because the binding constraint is byte volume over a saturated bus, which prefetch cannot reduce. The lever that works is reducing bytes-per-token until the model fits the fast tier: quantized to fit a 16GB unified-memory device, the same model runs GPU-resident at 11.5 tok/s (22x). We reconcile this with GPU-serving expert-prefetch predictors: a frozen-model probe predicts Qwen3-30B routing from the pre-attention state at 91.2%, a scale-invariant predictability property, but this converts to throughput only where the fast tier caches most of the model and per-token transfer is comparable to compute -- measured to hold on an A100 PCIe-offload path and to fail on bandwidth-walled edge storage. Predictability is not speedup; we chart where the gap closes.

</details>


### [2] [MonoMoE: An Efficient Fused Mega-kernel for Quantized MoE Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04244)
*Yu Gong,Kailash Budhathoki,Taeho Kim,Haipeng Li,Ashish Khetan*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: MonoMoE是一种面向块级量化（block-wise quantized）MoE解码的"权重主导持久化大内核"（weight-major persistent megakernel）。它将完整的解码token tile放置在细粒度张量核心N维度上，将CTA分区到专家权重tile上，消除专家局部token物化并减少填充运算；通过持久化网格融合路由、top-k选择、量化、专家投影、激活和归约于一次启动中，利用warp专用化和就绪标志重叠辅助工作。在NVIDIA H200上相对vLLM Triton Grouped GEMM加速最多1.54倍，比FlashMoE-FP8快2.20-3.84倍，端到端吞吐提升最高18.7%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的分组和批量GEMM实现采用token主序（token-major）组织方式，在自回归解码中当到达每个专家的token很少时，会产生tile填充和预处理开销、启动生命周期短的网格导致内存带宽利用不足，且量化和归约等步骤被拆分到不同阶段，整体效率低下。

Method: 提出了权重主序（weight-major）的持久化megakernel MonoMoE：1) 将完整解码步骤的token tile映射到张量核心的细粒度N维度，CTA按专家权重tile划分，避免专家局部token的重新组织和材料化；2) 使用持久化网格在一次kernel启动中完成路由、top-k、量化、两个专家投影、激活和归约的全流程融合；3) 通过warp专门化和就绪标志将辅助工作与主导的专家权重流重叠；4) 通过生成的内核特化（kernel specialization）和离线调度调优支持多种模型形状，并集成到vLLM中。

Result: 在NVIDIA H200上，MonoMoE对完整routed-MoE算子相对vLLM Triton Grouped GEMM加速最高1.54倍，相对FlashMoE-FP8在多种模型和batch大小下快2.20-3.84倍；对FP8模型端到端每输出token时间降低最高18.7%，同时保持任务精度不变。

Conclusion: MonoMoE证明了在MoE解码场景下，权重主序的持久化融合内核相比token主序的分阶段组GEMM方法能显著减少填充开销和内核启动开销，同时保持精度，是一种可落地到vLLM等推理框架的高效MoE解码方案。实现已在FlashInfer仓库开源。

Abstract: Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) layers increase model capacity without proportionally increasing arithmetic, but their sparse expert computation is difficult to execute efficiently during autoregressive decode. Existing grouped and batched GEMMs are token-major: they construct expert-local token tiles and obtain parallelism from the token dimension. When few tokens reach each expert, this organization incurs tile padding and preprocessing, launches short-lived grids that underutilize memory bandwidth, and exposes quantization, activation, and reduction as separate stages. We present \textbf{MonoMoE}, a weight-major persistent megakernel for block-wise quantized MoE decode. MonoMoE places the complete decode-step token tile on the fine-grained tensor-core $N$ dimension and partitions CTAs over expert-weight tiles, eliminating expert-local token materialization and reducing padded arithmetic. A persistent grid fuses routing, top-$k$ selection, quantization, both expert projections, activation, and reduction in one launch; warp specialization and readiness flags overlap auxiliary work with the dominant expert-weight stream. MonoMoE is integrated with vLLM and supports multiple model shapes through generated kernel specializations and offline schedule tuning. On NVIDIA H200 GPUs, MonoMoE accelerates the complete routed-MoE operator by up to $\mathbf{1.54\times}$ over vLLM Triton Grouped GEMM, is $\mathbf{2.20}$--$\mathbf{3.84\times}$ faster than FlashMoE-FP8 adaptation across various models and batch sizes, and reduces end-to-end time per output token by up to $\mathbf{18.7\%}$ across the evaluated FP8 models, while preserving task accuracy. The MonoMoE implementation and supporting artifacts are open source and available in the \href{https://github.com/flashinfer-ai/flashinfer/tree/main/csrc/fused_moe/monomoe}{FlashInfer repository}.
