### [1] [Rule-Guided and Trajectory-Aware Repair for LLM-Based HLS Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07324)
*Xianjing Zhu,Wenqi Lou,Yuanbo Wen,Teng Wang,Lei Gong,Chao Wang,Xuehai Zhou*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: MementoHLS：以可执行、可证伪的双记忆引导修复的智能体式HLS C生成框架，显著提升端到端TB∧Synth通过率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有LLM直接由自然语言规格生成功能正确且可综合的HLS-C时，难以将多轮工具反馈与可复用的HLS知识转化为有效修订，导致端到端生成成功率受限。

Method: 提出MementoHLS，围绕双记忆引导修复：(1) ERCL（可执行修复契约库）将跨任务HLS知识转为带证据门控的修复动作；(2) RFL（审阅者依据的证伪账本）将任务内修复历史转为轨迹证据，抑制无效路径并保留已验证进展。另设Same-Candidate Reviewer将Parse/Compile/TB/Synth结果绑定到对应候选，Single-Commit Arbiter每轮最多提交一个候选，以保证结果归因可靠。

Result: 在94个HLS-Eval与170个Bench4HLS任务上，同候选TB∧Synth pass@1/pass@5由41.7%/57.4%提升至58.9%/68.1%（HLS-Eval），由15.5%/32.9%提升至32.4%/50.6%（Bench4HLS）。

Conclusion: 双记忆（跨任务知识+任务内轨迹证据）与严格归因/提交机制的结合，能有效提升HLS-C端到端生成的可靠性，在两类基准上均取得大幅提升。

Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) offer a promising path for directly generating functionally correct and synthesizable HLS-C from natural-language specifications. However, existing approaches often struggle to turn multi-round tool feedback and reusable HLS knowledge into effective revisions, limiting end-to-end generation success. We propose MementoHLS, an agentic HLS generation framework built around executable and falsifiable dual-memory-guided repair. MementoHLS combines an Executable Repair Contract Library (ERCL), which converts cross-task HLS knowledge into evidence-gated repair actions, with a Reviewer-Grounded Falsification Ledger (RFL), which converts task-local repair history into trajectory evidence to suppress ineffective routes and preserve validated progress. To ensure reliable outcome attribution, a Same-Candidate Reviewer binds Parse, Compile, Testbench (TB), and Synthesis (Synth) outcomes to the corresponding candidate, while a Single-Commit Arbiter commits at most one candidate per round. Evaluated on 94 HLS-Eval and 170 Bench4HLS tasks, MementoHLS improves same-candidate TB$\wedge$Synth pass@1/pass@5 from 41.7%/57.4% to 58.9%/68.1% on HLS-Eval, and from 15.5%/32.9% to 32.4%/50.6% on Bench4HLS, demonstrating substantial improvements in end-to-end HLS generation reliability.

### [2] [A Synchronized Tri-Modality Wireless System for Multi-Point Non-Contact Cardiac Monitoring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07358)
*Wei Ren,Kaili Zhao,Huansheng Yi,Miaoyang Hu,Jianqi Wang,Canhua Xu,Fugui Qi*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 该文提出首个集成ECG、SCG与DCG的三模态无线采集系统，为雷达心脏传感方法提供带机械真值的严格验证平台。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 非接触雷达（多普勒心动图DCG）可捕捉胸壁振动并具备空间特异性，但既往研究仅以ECG等粗略参考进行对照，缺乏直接的机械学真值，导致雷达输出的细粒度评估受限；根本瓶颈在于缺少可靠的研究级硬件验证平台。

Method: 构建统一采集与控制框架下的三模态无线系统：微型可穿戴ECG/SCG模块 + 高分辨率毫米波雷达模块，实现时间对齐记录、多点胸廓采集、跨模态精确同步与面向可穿戴的规格设计，并支持空间分辨感知与特征提取。

Result: 线性运动平台实验显示DCG与SCG导出的位移高度一致，验证了位移测量的准确性；人体实验进一步表明跨模态一致性与特征点稳健提取，确立ECG为电学锚点、SCG为DCG的机械真值。

Conclusion: 该工作提供了严格、空间分辨的验证平台，推动非接触心脏传感与下一代雷达监测研究。

Abstract: Cardiac physiological monitoring is fundamental to evaluating cardiovascular health and diagnosing related diseases. Non-contact radar sensing can capture chest-wall vibrations with spatial specificity, enabling localized physiological monitoring and cardiac assessment. Progress, however, has been limited by the absence of a reliable, research-grade hardware platform for rigorous validation of radar-based methods. Prior systems typically pair radar, namely Doppler cardiography (DCG), with only electrocardiography (ECG) or other coarse references, providing no direct mechanical ground truth and thus constraining fine-grained evaluation of radar outputs. We present a tri-modality wireless system that integrates ECG, seismocardiography (SCG), and DCG under a unified acquisition and control framework for time-aligned recording and analysis. The tri-modality system combines miniaturized wearable ECG/SCG modules with a high-resolution mmWave radar module, supporting spatially resolved sensing and subsequent feature extraction. To our knowledge, this is the first integrated ECG--SCG--DCG hardware system for comprehensive cardiac monitoring; it enables multi-point thoracic acquisition, achieves precise cross-modal synchronization, and maintains specifications suitable for wearable operation. Experiments on a linear-motion platform show close agreement between DCG- and SCG-derived displacements, confirming accurate displacement measurement. Human-subject studies further demonstrate cross-modal consistency and robust extraction of feature points, establishing ECG as the electrical anchor and SCG as the mechanical ground truth for DCG. Overall, the system provides a rigorous, spatially resolved validation platform that advances non-contact cardiac sensing and supports next-generation radar-based monitoring.

### [3] [OpenRDMA: A GPU-Native Multi-Path Reliable RDMA Transport Architecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07559)
*Ziheng Ji,Chunyu Liu,Weihao Peng,Mingheng Mi,Pu Wang,Hai Zhang*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: OpenRDMA是一种面向GPU友好通信的开源多路径可靠RDMA传输架构，通过FPGA网卡将GPU控制路径开销降至8字节token，并结合MRC乱序传输与软硬件协同重传机制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大规模AI集群算力密度上升，网络通信成为系统性能瓶颈。GPU发起的通信需低开销请求提交以重叠计算与通信，但现有方案存在缺陷：GDR的控制路径仍依赖CPU参与；IBGDA要求GPU自行组装WQE，额外消耗SM资源与内存带宽。

Method: 1）极简GPU doorbell机制：GPU仅发送8字节token，由专用FPGA网卡依据预配置上下文重构完整WQE；2）基于多路径可靠连接（MRC）实现逐包多路径传输与乱序到达支持，配合选择性重传、有序DMA放置及GPU可见标志轮询保障可靠交付与安全消费；3）软硬件协同重传：网卡负责快速数据传输，CPU软件负责超时检测、状态跟踪与恢复策略，保持GPU提交路径轻量并降低FPGA硬件状态开销。

Result: 论文描述了OpenRDMA的架构与关键硬件组件，FPGA原型实现尚在进行中，暂无实验性能数据。

Conclusion: OpenRDMA通过最小化GPU控制路径开销、多路径乱序可靠传输及软硬件协同重传，为GPU发起通信提供了一条低开销、可扩展的开源RDMA设计路径。

Abstract: As the computing power density of large-scale AI clusters rises, network communication increasingly limits system performance. GPU-initiated communication requires low-overhead request submission to overlap communication with computation. However, GPUDirect RDMA (GDR) still relies on CPU involvement in its control path, while InfiniBand GPUDirect Async (IBGDA) requires GPUs to assemble Work Queue Entries (WQEs), consuming additional SM resources and memory bandwidth. We propose OpenRDMA, an open-source multi-path reliable RDMA transport architecture designed for GPU-friendly communication. First, OpenRDMA provides a minimal GPU doorbell mechanism: the GPU issues an 8-byte token, and a dedicated FPGA NIC reconstructs the complete WQE from preconfigured context. Second, building on Multipath Reliable Connection (MRC), OpenRDMA supports per-packet multi-path transport and out-of-order arrivals, while selective retransmission, ordered DMA placement, and GPU-visible flag polling provide reliable delivery and safe data consumption. Third, a hardware-software co-designed retransmission mechanism keeps the GPU submission path lightweight and reduces FPGA hardware state overhead: the NIC handles fast data transfer, while CPU software manages complex timeout detection, state tracking, and recovery policies. We describe the architecture and key hardware components of OpenRDMA; implementation of the FPGA prototype is underway.

### [4] [CAMP: Context-Aware Mapping and Placement for LLMs on 3D-IMC Arrays](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07707)
*Orhun Görkem,Athanasios Vasilopoulos,Georgios Pagonis,Victoria Clerico,Hadjer Benmeziane,Irem Boybat,Hsinyu Tsai,Abu Sebastian,William Andrew Simon*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: CAMP是一种面向3D-IMC大模型加速器的推理上下文感知权重映射算法，通过冲突矩阵刻画架构、网络与运行时的资源竞争，结合剖析驱动的反馈搜索，将3D-IMC计算利用率从42.4%提升至60.3%，吞吐最高提升2.0倍、端到端吞吐提升1.74倍。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM因加载大权重矩阵和KV cache产生严重访存开销；AIMC可在存储权重上直接计算以降低能耗与延迟，3D-IMC通过垂直堆叠存储阵列提升容量，但权重的放置方式决定了哪些MVM可并发执行，直接影响延迟与吞吐。3D-IMC权重映射的并发性问题在文献中研究不足，导致计算资源利用次优。

Method: 提出CAMP（Context-Aware Mapping and Placement）离线软件算法：以冲突矩阵（conflict matrix）刻画由架构结构、目标网络与运行时上下文引起的潜在资源竞争，实现吞吐优化的权重映射；再经剖析驱动的反馈循环精炼映射，高效剪枝搜索空间，在可接受运行时间内得到优化映射。无需硬件改动。

Result: 3D-IMC计算利用率由42.4%提升至60.3%；相较SOTA映射算法，3D-IMC吞吐最高提升2.0倍，端到端吞吐提升1.74倍。

Conclusion: 并发感知的上下文驱动权重映射是释放3D-IMC LLM加速器性能的关键；CAMP以纯软件、离线方式兼顾映射质量与搜索开销，显著提升计算利用率和吞吐，且无需硬件修改。

Abstract: Large Language Models (LLMs) incur severe memory-access overheads due to the cost of loading large weight matrices and key-value (KV) caches. Analog In-Memory Computing (AIMC) is an emerging solution that enables computation directly on static weights stored in memory, substantially reducing energy and latency. 3D-IMC further stacks memory arrays into vertical tiers, increasing capacity but revealing a new challenge: how weights are placed in memory determines which matrix-vector multiplications (MVMs) can execute concurrently, affecting latency and throughput. The concurrency aspect of 3D-IMC weight-mapping remains underexplored in the literature, leading to sub-optimal utilization of compute resources on 3D-IMC chips. In this context we propose CAMP (Context-Aware Mapping and Placement), an inference-context-aware weight mapping algorithm for 3D-IMC-based LLM accelerators. CAMP captures the potential resource contention resulting from the architecture, targeted network, and runtime context in a conflict matrix, enabling throughput-optimized weight mapping. This mapping is refined through a profiling-driven feedback loop that efficiently prunes the search space and returns optimized mappings within practical runtimes. CAMP is an offline, software-based solution requiring no hardware changes and improving 3D-IMC compute utilization from 42.4% to 60.3%, delivering up to 2.0x higher 3D-IMC throughput and 1.74x higher end-to-end throughput over state-of-the-art mapping algorithms.

### [5] [Should You Split Decode? Disaggregation Across Future Models and Accelerators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08299)
*Ramyad Hadidi*

Main category: cs.AR

  <summary>Details</summary>

We therefore find that prefill provisioning, decode memory bandwidth, links, and pool granularity decide deployments more than the split alone. A fixed hardware assignment can lose its advantage as models, fabrics, hardware assumptions, and objectives change. Re-optimizing mappings and pool sizes on the same hardware cuts early commitment's median normalized goodput loss from 36% to 9%.
