### [1] [A Multi-Engine Dataflow for MoE Decoding on Scratchpad-Based Tensor Accelerators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21137)
*Bin Ma,Wenjie Fan,Dong Li*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: CARDAN：将专家权重分解为向量量化分量+共享低秩分量，并协同设计多引擎解码数据流，在Trainium3上实现MoE解码加速。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Scratchpad型张量加速器（STA）上MoE解码主要瓶颈是专家权重搬运，而计算引擎空闲。该流量难以隐藏（专家需路由后才已知），也难以压缩（会损失质量或增加关键路径开销）。

Method: 将每个专家权重矩阵表示为向量量化分量加共享基低秩分量，并与多引擎解码数据流协同设计：该表示将专家共性工作与专家私有工作分离，数据流在多个引擎上重叠DMA与计算。

Result: 在AWS Trainium3上的五个MoE系列中，CARDAN在全部五个模型上匹配或改善BF16教师困惑度；batch-one解码相比AWS密集MoE megakernel加速1.15-1.31x，batch size 16时达1.7x。

Conclusion: 通过权重表示与数据流协同设计，CARDAN在保持质量的同时有效缓解STA上MoE解码的专家权重搬运瓶颈。

Abstract: Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) decoding on scratchpad-based tensor accelerators (STA) is dominated by moving expert weights while the compute engines sit idle. This traffic is hard to hide, because the experts are known only after routing, and hard to shrink without losing quality or adding critical-path work. We present CARDAN, which represents each expert-weight matrix as a vector-quantized component plus a shared-basis low-rank component and co-designs this representation with a multi-engine decoding dataflow. The representation separates expert-common from expert-private work, so the dataflow overlaps DMA with computation on several engines. Across five MoE families on AWS Trainium3, CARDAN matches or improves BF16-teacher perplexity across all five models and speeds up batch-one decoding by 1.15-1.31x over AWS dense MoE megakernels, rising to 1.7x at batch size 16.

### [2] [Programming AMD XDNA NPUs with Open-source Compiler Tools: A FlashAttention Case Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21264)
*Erwei Wang,Ephrem Wu,Victor J. B. Jung,Jiajie Li,Andre Rosti,Joseph Melber,Samuel Bayliss*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 在AMD XDNA空间架构NPU上，通过将FlashAttention三阶段算子融合为单kernel、把QK^T得分驻留在计算瓦片本地内存并利用级联互连归约，在XDNA 2上实现3.62 TFLOP/s端到端吞吐，为IRON设计的两倍，能效为同芯片iGPU的5.3–7.2倍。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 空间NPU将计算瓦片与小型本地内存并列，数据搬移交由软件决定，因此多阶段工作负载映射的核心问题是中间张量放在哪里。作者希望系统性地回答在FlashAttention这类多阶段负载中，中间张量应如何放置、融合应做到何种程度。

Method: 在XDNA 1与XDNA 2上基于开源IRON与MLIR-AIR流程对比四种参考设计：逐算子独立执行、两种片上算子间流式传输、以及将三个注意力阶段融合进单一kernel。融合kernel把QK^T得分保存在计算瓦片本地内存中，并通过级联互连归约部分结果，避免得分回写共享MemTile内存。同时在各内存层级进行Roofline分析。

Result: XDNA 2上融合设计达到3.62 TFLOP/s（完整端到端执行），为IRON设计的两倍，在2K tokens及以上能效为同芯片集成GPU的5.3–7.2倍，覆盖从BERT到DeepSeek的十二种LLM配置、最高128K tokens。Roofline分析表明XDNA 1脊点更低，片上流式传输已进入计算受限区，因此在XDNA 2上使吞吐翻倍的融合在XDNA 1上几乎无收益。

Conclusion: 将映射的操作强度与各内存层级的脊点比较，可在写代码前预测融合是否有效：融合直到映射越过该脊点为止，之后停止。参考设计已作为持续维护的开源项目发布。

Abstract: Spatial NPUs such as AMD XDNA place compute tiles beside small local memories and leave data movement between them to software. Mapping a multi-stage workload onto such a device is largely a question of where the intermediate tensors live. We report what we learned making those choices for FlashAttention with the open-source IRON and MLIR-AIR flows.
  We compare four reference designs on XDNA 1 and XDNA 2: one runs each operator separately, two stream between operators on chip, and one fuses all three attention stages into a single kernel. The fused kernel holds the $\boldsymbol{QK}^{\mathsf T}$ scores in compute-tile local memory and reduces partial results over the cascade interconnect, so the scores never return to shared MemTile memory. On XDNA 2, it reaches 3.62 TFLOP/s over complete end-to-end execution, twice the IRON design, with 5.3 to 7.2 times the energy efficiency of the integrated GPU on the same chip at 2K tokens and above. It covers twelve LLM configurations, from BERT to DeepSeek, up to 128K tokens.
  Roofline analysis at each memory level explains this result and shows when to stop. XDNA 1 has lower ridge points, so streaming on chip already reaches the compute-bound regime: the same fusion that doubles throughput on XDNA 2 is nearly wasted on XDNA 1. Comparing a mapping's operational intensity against each level's ridge point predicts which case applies before writing any code. Fuse until the mapping clears that ridge point, then stop. We release the reference designs as maintained open source.

### [3] [Integrating Approximate Logic Synthesis into Approximate High-Level Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21697)
*Jian Shi,Ruicheng Dai,Chang Meng,Yue Yang,Weikang Qian*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 该论文提出将近似逻辑综合（ALS）整合进近似高层次综合（HLS）流程，构建统一框架以扩展设计空间，在相同误差约束下平均降低11%硬件代价。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 近似HLS与近似逻辑综合（ALS）分别在不同粒度上工作：近似HLS通常修改控制数据流图中的指令，ALS则修改门级网表中的门和互连。二者缺乏统一框架，无法进行联合优化，限制了近似电路的质量。

Method: 提出将ALS集成到近似HLS流程中，通过引入ALS带来的细粒度近似，扩展近似HLS的设计空间，从而生成更高质量的近似电路。

Result: 实验结果表明，在相同误差界下，该方法相比现有最先进方法平均降低硬件代价11%。

Conclusion: 将ALS与近似HLS统一集成可有效扩展设计空间并提升近似电路质量，验证了跨粒度联合优化的可行性与有效性。

Abstract: Approximate high-level synthesis (HLS) and approximate logic synthesis (ALS) are two techniques for generating approximate circuits. They operate at different granularities. Approximate HLS typically modifies instructions in a control and data flow graph, whereas ALS modifies gates and interconnects in a gate-level netlist. The absence of a unified framework combining these techniques limits the potential for joint optimization. To bridge this gap, we propose to integrate ALS into the flow of approximate HLS. This integration expands the design space of approximate HLS by introducing fine-grained approximation induced by ALS, thereby generating approximate circuits with higher quality. Experimental results show that under the same error bound, our method reduces the hardware cost by 11% on average compared to the state-of-the-art methods.

### [4] [Scalable Packet Tracking on FPGAs for Erasure-Coded RDMA over Lossy WANs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21774)
*Yicheng Qian,Konstantin Taranov,Yevgeny Yankilevich,Assaf Shacham,Mahmoud Elhaddad,Abdul Kabbani,Miriam Leeser,Nadeen Gebara*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 该论文提出COMET，首个在FPGA网卡上全硬件卸载的多路径RDMA数据包到达追踪设计，通过可扩展的缓存架构解决广域网上纠删码恢复的追踪瓶颈。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代AI负载需跨数据中心互联构建"AI工厂"以突破单站点功耗与散热限制，但将RDMA扩展到广域网会引入多路径乱序、高延迟和丢包，严重损害性能。纠删码虽可恢复丢包，但其效果依赖硬件中高效的数据包到达追踪，而现有方案难以在高链路速率下扩展。

Method: COMET采用可扩展的基于缓存的架构，在FPGA网卡上实现全硬件卸载的数据包到达追踪，支持多路径RDMA在丢包广域网上的运行；其缓存架构（COMET Cache）将片上内存占用与链路带宽时延积（BDP）解耦。

Result: COMET可在400 Gbps及以上维持线速运行；其缓存架构支持比当前最先进的SoC设计多6倍的并发连接数；架构可扩展至新兴的1.6 Tbps网卡及更高。

Conclusion: 可扩展的全硬件卸载数据包到达追踪在当前数据速率下的FPGA网卡上是可行的，其架构可扩展性可延伸至1.6 Tbps及更高网卡。

Abstract: Modern AI workloads increasingly rely on scale across architectures that interconnect multiple datacenters to form a single "AI factory", overcoming the power and cooling constraints of individual sites. However, extending Remote Direct Memory Access (RDMA) across wide area networks (WANs) introduces fundamental challenges: multi-path packet reordering, high latency, and packet loss that severely degrade performance. While erasure coding (EC) has emerged as a promising mechanism for loss recovery, its effectiveness critically depends on efficient packet arrival tracking implemented in hardware. We present COmpact Multi-path Erasure-coded Tracking (COMET), the first fully hardware-offloaded packet-arrival tracking design implemented on an FPGA-based network interface card (NIC) for multi-path RDMA over lossy WANs. COMET employs a scalable cache-based architecture that supports operation at high link rates. Our evaluation shows that COMET sustains line rate operation at 400 Gbps and beyond. Critically, COMET decouples on-chip memory footprint from link Bandwidth-Delay Product (BDP), and its cache-based architecture (COMET Cache) enables supporting 6 times more concurrent connections than state-of-the-art (SOTA) SoC-based designs. These results demonstrate that scalable, fully hardware-offloaded packet-arrival tracking is practical on FPGA-based NICs at current data rates, and its architectural scalability extends to emerging 1.6 Tbps NICs and beyond.

</details>
