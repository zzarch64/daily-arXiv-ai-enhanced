### [1] [SeqFeed: Improving Agentic RTL Code Generation with Sequential Behavior Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16934)
*Yuxin Du,Juxin Niu,Tao Hu,Xi Wang,Zhe Jiang,Nan Guan*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出SeqFeed框架，通过SeQuery（SQL-like波形查询语言）和SeGraph（跨时钟周期依赖图）两种互补机制，为RTL代码生成中的智能体提供事件可寻址、依赖可追踪、可迭代查询的时序行为反馈，显著提升多LLM的RTL生成通过率。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: RTL代码生成是硬件设计的关键阶段，智能体系统为自动化此过程带来新机遇。但智能体需要理解信号在多时钟周期上的时序演化行为，而RTL代码本身不暴露特定执行下的周期级信号行为，完整仿真波形又过于庞杂且含噪，难以被LLM有效分析。现有方法缺乏向智能体有效传达时序信息的机制。

Method: 作者首先研究人类工程师推理时序行为的方式，提炼出有效反馈的三个需求：事件可寻址（event-addressable）、依赖可追踪（dependency-traceable）、可迭代查询（iteratively-queryable）。基于此提出SeqFeed框架，包含两个互补机制：(1) SeQuery——一种类SQL的波形查询语言，允许智能体锚定语义事件并在相对时间点采样信号值；(2) SeGraph——跟踪信号跨时钟周期传播的依赖图。

Conclusion: SeqFeed通过事件可寻址的查询语言和跨周期依赖图，解决了向智能体有效传达时序行为信息的难题，为自动化RTL代码生成提供了更高效的反馈机制，并验证了人工程序员式时序推理方式对LLM同样适用。

*Shashank*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: DTX是一个面向扩散模型和Transformer模型的吞吐量优先训练加速器，通过消除所有循环携带依赖（所有规约均为流水线二叉树，所有浮点算子均为II=1的两级流水线），在单个时钟内实现216 FLOP/cycle，约为传统循环携带下限的108倍。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 任何通过单一FP32加法器串行化的求和都会形成循环携带依赖，将机器性能钉在约2 FLOP/cycle的水平，这与物理设计无关。传统加速器架构因此存在根本性的吞吐量瓶颈，需要从架构层面彻底消除这种依赖链。

Method: DTX采用4槽VLIW指令字，统一64KB片内存储空间，包含：（1）8x8权重静止脉动阵列，带融合偏置/激活/类型转换尾声；（2）8通道向量单元；（3）8通道融合AdamW流水线；（4）流水线化Philox高斯随机数源。所有规约均实现为流水线二叉树，所有浮点算子均为启动间隔为1的两级流水线。

Conclusion: DTX通过架构级消除所有循环携带依赖，从根本上突破传统加速器的每时钟2 FLOP上限，展示了循环携带依赖作为吞吐量瓶颈的病理学问题。综合验证结果（功能正确性、语义收敛、DMA重叠、能效优势及物理实现）表明该架构在扩散模型与Transformer训练场景中具有实际可行性。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的能量回收（绝热）电路设计高度依赖手工且缺乏自动化工具支持。当绝热门擦除信息时，代价不是热力学下限k_B T ln2，而是完整的非绝热CV²放电，这与电路风格本来要回收的开关能量相当。因此需要一种自动化的综合工具，能够以能量为优化目标，将普通不可逆网表转换为经过验证的绝热电路，以克服绝热电路设计的瓶颈。

Abstract: We describe Renesis, an automated synthesis tool that accepts an ordinary irreversible netlist and produces a verified, technology-mapped energy-recovery (adiabatic) circuit, using energy rather than area or delay as the optimization criterion. Renesis models the netlist with a vector-space formulation that expresses simulation and justification sweeps as forward and reverse traversals whose cost is linear in the number of circuit components. The traversals populate ledgers with data tags that characterize switching, erasure, and observability information at their natural Rényi orders. The output is a logically reversible circuit mapped to one of eight energy-recovery families, with the associated parameters reported. Reversibility is treated here as a circuit-level requirement rather than a thermodynamic one. A device-level SPICE deck reproduces the tool's per-cycle energy figures on the reference family. The tool, the benchmark netlists, the validation procedure, and the run records behind every reported number are released as open source.

Abstract: Heterogeneous graph neural networks (HGNNs) are highly effective in processing heterogeneous graph data and have been widely adopted in critical domains. As real-world graph data continues to scale, performing direct inference on entire graphs becomes increasingly infeasible, making mini-batch methods the standard approach. However, in end-to-end HGNN inference, metapath-based mini-batch sampling constitutes a significant performance bottleneck due to the extensive random memory accesses induced by the irregular traversal of graph structures. Existing sampling paradigms suffer from excessive redundant traversals caused by inherent semantic redundancy, severely degrading sampling efficiency and, consequently, leading to suboptimal mini-batch inference performance.
  In this work, we propose a redundancy-aware HGNN sampling paradigm that leverages a metapath trie to reuse traversal paths, effectively eliminating redundant memory accesses. We then map it onto a multi-channel hardware sampling unit denominated ESR-HGNN. Furthermore, we introduce a reusability-driven metapath grouping technique that optimally clusters metapaths to maximize reusable traversal paths within hardware channels, enhancing efficiency in scenarios with semantic parallelism. Extensive experimental results demonstrate that ESR-HGNN achieves an average sampling performance improvement of one order of magnitude over CPU and GPU, accompanied by significant energy savings. Additionally, it delivers substantial speedup in end-to-end mini-batch inference when integrated with GPU and state-of-the-art HGNN inference accelerator.
