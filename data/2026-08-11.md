### [1] [HINT: Toward an Executable Hardware-Intent Representation Layer for LLM-Driven RTL Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07625)
*Tairan Cheng,Yi Liu,Dongsheng Zuo,Zhengyuan Shi,Hongji Zhang,Xiangfei Hu,Maoshuo He,Hao Yan,Qiang Xu*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出HINT，一种可执行的硬件意图中间表示层，位于行为规范/可执行oracle与RTL之间，使微架构显式化、支持RTL前检查并提供明确的RTL降级义务，从而提升LLM生成RTL的质量。在7个算子案例中全部成功生成可综合RTL，并在面积上优于直接C2RTL和手工RTL实现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 使用LLM直接生成实现质量的RTL仍然困难，因为直接生成必须同时解决微架构决策并产出和调试底层代码，这导致生成质量差、收敛困难。

Method: 提出HINT作为介于行为规格/可执行oracle与RTL之间的可执行硬件意图中间表示层，使RTL相关的微架构显式化，支持预RTL检查，并提供明确的RTL降级义务。评估使用最小单智能体流程和完整分阶段工作流两种方式。

Result: 七个算子案例中HINT介导路径7/7全部生成符合契约的可综合RTL；直接C2RTL和C2HLSC分别适用5个案例并成功5/5和1/5。HINT在面积上比五个手工RTL实现减少5.0%-26.2%，比五个直接C2RTL结果减少8.9%-86.1%。RealBench AES和SDC以及综合至561.67kμm²的Vortex VPU验证了规范驱动、协议丰富、内存丰富和层次化设计。

Conclusion: HINT介导的RTL生成路线在收敛性和实现质量上均优于直接生成，避免了直接生成中出现的严重实现质量退化问题。

Abstract: Generating implementation-quality RTL with large language models (LLMs) remains difficult because direct generation must resolve microarchitecture while simultaneously producing and debugging low-level code. We present HINT, an executable hardware-intent intermediate representation layer between behavioral specifications or executable oracles and RTL. HINT makes RTL-relevant microarchitecture explicit, supports pre-RTL checking, and supplies explicit RTL-lowering obligations. We evaluate HINT using both a minimal single-agent flow and a full staged workflow. Across seven operator cases, the HINT-mediated route, with no post-synthesis QoR refinement, produces contract-compliant synthesizable RTL on 7/7 cases; Direct C2RTL and C2HLSC apply to five cases and succeed on 5/5 and 1/5, respectively. Under matched Design Compiler synthesis, HINT reduces area by 5.0\%--26.2\% relative to five manual RTL implementations and by 8.9\%--86.1\% relative to five accepted Direct C2RTL results. RealBench AES and SDC, together with a Vortex VPU synthesizing to 561.67k~$μ\mathrm{m}^2$, further demonstrate specification-driven, protocol-rich, memory-rich, and hierarchical designs. In the controlled operator study, the HINT-mediated route shows better observed convergence and avoids the severe implementation-quality degradation seen in several direct-generation results.

### [2] [Hybrid ASIC-FPAA Fabric for Performance Security Trade-off](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07896)
*Ziyi Chen,Vaibhav Venugopal Rao,Kyle Juretus,Ioannis Savidis*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出一种结合ASIC与FPAA的混合架构(ASIC-FPAA)来解决模拟电路的性能与安全权衡问题,通过可编程晶体管对和虚拟晶体管对来混淆电路拓扑,并开发了子块映射算法在大规模电路中平衡性能与安全。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 模拟电路的硬件安全面临性能与安全之间的权衡困境:完全混淆会显著恶化电路性能,而缺乏安全保护又容易遭到逆向工程和IP盗用攻击。现有解决方案难以在保持高性能的同时提供足够的安全鲁棒性。

Method: 提出ASIC-FPAA混合架构,包含三个层次的安全混淆技术:(1)可编程晶体管对(TP)在晶体管对阵列中混淆电路拓扑;(2)虚拟晶体管对技术混淆有效晶体管数目;(3)整体拓扑混淆方法。同时开发了子块映射算法,针对大规模模拟电路识别最适合在FPAA织物上混淆的子块,用以平衡性能与安全权衡。

Result: 在delta-sigma调制器上,选择比较器模块在FPAA织物上实现,有效位数(ENOB)仅降低不到0.25%,功耗增加28.1%。安全性评估显示,与未混淆版本相比,所提出的技术提供了5倍的安全改进(依据所开发的度量标准),且未牺牲性能。

Conclusion: ASIC-FPAA混合架构通过分级混淆策略成功实现了模拟电路的安全加固与性能保持之间的有效平衡,为大规模模拟IC的IP保护提供了一条可行的技术路线。

Abstract: In this paper, a hybrid architecture that combines an application specific integrated circuit (ASIC) and a field-programmable analog array (ASIC-FPAA) is proposed to address the performance-security trade-off in analog circuits. A programmable transistor pair (TP) is developed that obfuscates the topology of an analog circuit within an array of transistor pairs while minimizing the degradation in circuit performance due to the implemented security features. In addition, a technique that utilizes dummy transistor pairs is proposed that further masks the topology of a circuit by obfuscating the number of active transistors that comprise the target analog circuit. A method to obfuscate the entire topology of an analog circuit is also developed. The FPAA provides the highest-level of security robustness as the entire circuit topology is programmed onto the transistor pair array, while trading-off performance. For large analog circuits requiring multiple configurable analog blocks (CABs), an algorithm is developed that identifies and maps the sub-blocks of the circuit best suited for obfuscation on the FPAA fabric, while accounting for the trade-off between performance and security. For the delta-sigma modulator, the comparator module is selected for implementation on the FPAA fabric, which results in a reduction of less than 0.25% in the effective number of bits (ENOB) and an increase of 28.1% in the power consumption as compared to an unobfuscated delta-sigma modulator. The security robustness of the proposed techniques are evaluated against the latest attack methodologies. Results indicate that the proposed techniques provide a 5x improvement in security as measured by a developed metric without compromising performance.

</details>


### [3] [ARMOR: Accelerating RTL Simulation by Mitigating the Front-End Bottleneck Using Node Compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08462)
*Jiaping Tang,Jianan Mu,Zhiteng Chao,Jingzhong Wen,Jing Ye,Huawei Li*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出ARMOR，一种通过节点压缩来缓解RTL模拟器中前端瓶颈的高效模拟器。它利用展开RTL图的数据并行性和位空间利用率不足，通过位级数据并行压缩多个节点，使单条指令序列服务于多个节点。实验表明在CPU设计上实现1.6x加速，在AI加速器上实现2.7x加速。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: RTL模拟是芯片设计中不可或缺的环节。现有高性能模拟器将RTL图中的每个节点降低为指令序列，虽然有利于激进编译器优化，但大幅增加代码足迹，严重超出指令缓存容量，导致前端瓶颈。分析显示超过50%的流水线停顿由CPU前端造成，成为最先进的RTL模拟器中的关键性能瓶颈。

Method: ARMOR的核心思想是利用展开RTL图暴露的数据并行性和逐节点降低所揭示的位空间利用不足，通过位级数据并行同时压缩多个节点，使单条指令序列服务多个节点。具体包括三个部分：(1)模块感知的同构子图识别方法，利用模块实例间的结构同构性在子图级别系统识别压缩机会；(2)对齐感知的密集打包策略，根据数据流依赖将节点分组打包，同时保留数据重用，辅以贪心合并策略提升位空间利用率；(3)统一的位级并行方案，支持压缩节点的位级并行执行。

Result: 与最先进的模拟器相比，ARMOR在CPU设计上实现1.6倍加速，在AI加速器上实现2.7倍加速。

Conclusion: ARMOR通过节点压缩有效缓解了RTL模拟器中的前端瓶颈，同时保留了完全展开RTL图所带来的优化收益，证明了位级数据并行在RTL模拟中的巨大潜力。

Abstract: RTL simulation is indispensable in chip design. High-performance simulators typically lower each node in the RTL graph into an instruction sequence. Although this per-node lowering enables aggressive compiler optimizations, it dramatically increases the code footprint, severely exceeding instruction cache capacity and causing front-end bottlenecks. Our profiling reveals that over 50% of pipeline stalls are caused by the CPU front-end, becoming a key performance bottleneck in state-of-the-art RTL simulators. However, reaping the optimization benefits of fully unrolling the RTL graph while simultaneously reducing the code footprint to mitigate front-end bottlenecks remains highly challenging. In this paper, we propose ARMOR, an efficient RTL simulator designed to alleviate the front-end bottleneck through node compression. The key idea is to exploit the data parallelism exposed by the unrolling RTL graph and the insufficient bit-space utilization revealed by per-node lowering, leveraging bit-level data parallelism to compress multiple nodes simultaneously, so that a single instruction sequence can serve multiple nodes instead of one per node. To achieve profitable node compression, we first propose a module-aware isomorphic subgraph identification method that leverages structural isomorphism across module instances to systematically identify compression opportunities at the subgraph level. We then propose an alignment-aware dense packing strategy that groups nodes into packs according to dataflow dependencies while preserving data reuse, complemented by greedy merging strategies to enhance bit-space utilization. Finally, we implement a unified bit-level parallelism scheme to support bit-level parallel execution of compressed nodes. Experimental results show that ARMOR achieves 1.6x speedup on CPU designs and 2.7x speedup on AI accelerators compared to state-of-the-art simulators.

</details>


### [4] [ReVolt: Power Delivery Network-Aware Voltage Droop Control for 2.5D PIM Chiplet Architectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08496)
*Vibhanshu Sharma,Alish Kanani,Miao Sun,Janardhan Rao Doppa,Umit Y. Ogras,Partha Pratim Pande*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: ReVolt是一个基于动态操作单元（OU）的框架，用于缓解PIM多芯片系统中的电压跌落问题。它利用LSTM驱动的PDN代理模型在运行时预测各芯片的供电电压轨迹，并通过主动调整OU大小来调节电流需求，从而防止电压违规、改善能量延迟积（EDP），同时保持ML推理精度。实验显示平均EDP降低76倍。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: PIM-based 2.5D多芯片平台是ML工作负载的使能技术，但其性能受供电网络（PDN）影响。芯片级电流需求的时空变化导致电压跌落，造成电压违规、性能下降并影响ML推理精度，亟需有效的缓解方案。

Method: 提出ReVolt框架：1) 构建基于LSTM的PDN代理模型，在运行时预测每个芯片的供电电压轨迹；2) 将OU大小作为控制旋钮，通过主动调整OU尺寸来调节芯片级电流需求；3) 在抑制电压跌落的同时保持计算精度，防止电压违规并改善EDP。

Result: ReVolt能够防止电压跌落违规，与现有的固定和动态OU基线相比，平均实现76倍的EDP降低，且不损害ML模型的推理精度。

Conclusion: ReVolt展示了通过LSTM预测与动态OU控制相结合，可以在PIM多芯片系统中有效缓解电压跌落问题，同时显著提升能耗效率并保持ML推理精度，为多芯片PIM平台的可靠运行提供了可行的解决方案。

Abstract: Processing-in-memory (PIM)-based 2.5D multi-chiplet platforms are enablers for machine learning (ML) workloads. However, their performance is affected by the power delivery network (PDN), where varying chiplet-level current demand induces spatially and temporally varying voltage droop. These droop events lead to voltage violations, degrades system performance, and impact inference accuracy for ML workloads. In this work, we propose ReVolt, a dynamic operation unit (OU)-based framework for mitigating voltage droop in PIM-based multi-chiplet systems. ReVolt leverages an LSTM-based PDN surrogate to predict per-chiplet supply voltage trajectories at runtime, enabling proactive adjustment of OU size to mitigate droop events. By treating OU size as a control knob, ReVolt regulates chiplet-level current demand while maintaining computational accuracy. This approach prevents voltage droop violations and improves energy-delay product (EDP) while preserving ML model inference accuracy. Experimental results demonstrate that ReVolt prevents voltage droop violations while achieving an average 76x reduction in EDP compared to existing fixed and dynamic OU-based baselines, without compromising inference accuracy of ML models.
