Method: 设计四个可综合外围模块：Flush-Merge Enable（FME）控制器、基于置信度的提前退出（EE）模块、带解码器的精度寄存器（PR/PD）、位串行周期控制器（CC），协同降低开关活动、字线开销与漏电。基于TSMC 65nm工艺实现，工作频率335MHz。

Result: 综合后面积1769.760 μm²，PnR后1982.131 μm²；漏电6.0706 μW，动态功耗288.2902 μW（综合后）/322.8850 μW（PnR后），总计328.9556 μW（占系统功耗0.2402%）。EE模块占动态功耗58.2%，WLG占面积58.1%。MobileNetV2（CIFAR-100）上能效从31.35 TOPS/W（8b/8b，1.2V）提升至454.91 TOPS/W（2b/8b，0.8V），提升13.33倍。

Conclusion: 该外围框架无需修改位单元或重新训练即可显著提升能效，面向利用稀疏性的CNN负载，未来将扩展至Transformer。

Abstract: Digital SRAM compute-in-memory (CIM) macros enable energy-efficient CNN inference by eliminating off-chip weight transfers; however, peripheral control logic introduces three inefficiencies: bit- slice over-activation at reduced precision (L1), unnecessary accumula- tion depth (L2), and idle precision-transition stalls (L3). To address these, we present four synthesizable peripheral blocks: a Flush-Merge Enable (FME) controller, a Confidence-Based Early-Exit (EE) module, a Precision Register with Decoder (PR/PD), and a Bit-Serial Cycle Controller (CC), co-designed to minimize switching activity, wordline overhead, and leakage. Implemented in TSMC 65-nm CMOS at 335 MHz, the peripheral framework occupies 1769.760 μm2 post-synthesis and 1982.131 μm2 post-PnR. Gate-level VCD analysis reports 6.0706 μW leakage and 288.2902 μW (post-synthesis) / 322.8850 μW (post-PnR) dynamic power, totaling 328.9556 μW (0.2402% of system power). The EE module dominates dynamic power (58.2%), while WLG dominates area (58.1%). System evaluation on MobileNetV2 (CIFAR-100) shows efficiency scaling from 31.35 TOPS/W (8b/8b, 1.2 V) to 454.91 TOPS/W (2b/8b, 0.8 V), achieving 13.33x improvement without bitcell modification or retraining. The framework targets CNN workloads exploiting sparsity, with Transformer extension as future work.
