### [1] [CHARMING: Parallel Edge AI Acceleration Across Tensor Processing and In-Memory Computation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17785)

Method: CHARMING 将注意力头在 AIE 阵列与 DRAM 内存计算单元之间划分，依据模型架构、序列长度与精度平衡执行，并选择精度退化最小的头分配；同时在 AIE 投影阶段集成量化以进一步减少数据搬运。在 AMD Versal VCK5000 与 Alveo U200 上进行端到端硬件验证，并结合实测与最新存内计算硬件仿真评估 LLM 与视频生成模型。

### [2] [Elasticy: A Runtime-Reconfigurable Architecture for Dynamic Streams](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17798)
*Konstantin J Hossfeld,Nathan Sobotka,Gina Sohn,Tun-Yu Chang,Kunle Olukotun*

Main category: cs.AR

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 提出 Elasticy：(1) 用 streaming 与 tile 抽象降低动态支持开销；(2) 用 rank-variable 表达式把动态形状编码进 stream，利用数据相关形状动态性减少片外流量与无效计算；(3) stream 内嵌重构信息以触发细粒度运行时调度；(4) 新型分布式链式流式内存 DLSM 高效利用片上内存；(5) 重构以 tile stream 为粒度、发生频率低；(6) 分布式运行时调度器重叠依赖核；(7) 支持空时复用：各阶段空间流水、随时间折叠到同一 fabric。

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 提出 SIMTurn：1）编译器在潜在阻塞循环处插入 yield 指令；2）硬件维护有界数量的可恢复子组池；3）调度器允许等待中的子组让出给能取得进展的兄弟子组，而无需为每个线程配备独立程序计数器。在开源 Vortex GPGPU 上完成编译器、模拟器与 RTL 的全栈实现。

Conclusion: SIMTurn 表明：在栈式重收敛框架内引入有限的可恢复子组与编译器辅助的 yield 即可获得前向进展，以远低于 ITS 的硬件成本接近 ITS 性能，为 SIMT 分化执行提供了一种高性价比的折中方案。

However, its fixed serialization of divergent paths can deadlock programs that rely on intra-warp synchronization: a spinning subgroup may prevent a resource-holding sibling from executing. Independent Thread Scheduling (ITS) enables finer-grained progress through per-thread execution state, but introduces additional hardware cost. SIMTurn combines a compiler-inserted yield instruction at potentially blocking loops with a bounded pool of resumable subgroups. Across the evaluated GPU thread configurations, SIMTurn introduces only 17-24% scheduler-area overhead relative to IPDOM, compared with 39-91% for ITS, and stays within 8% of IPDOM on workloads that never need cross-subgroup progress. Abstract: Wafer-scale accelerators offer massive compute capacity through hundreds of thousands of processing elements (PEs) interconnected across a single wafer, but realizing this potential requires efficient application mapping. Their scale makes programming these systems and mapping applications onto them challenging. Existing approaches rely on template-based mappings to maintain productivity, but these templates restrict the mapping design space. In particular, they impose fixed geometric layouts over the PE grid, often leaving compute and memory resources underutilized.
  In this work, we present WaferTetris, a system that removes these geometric restrictions through freeform mapping. Its Tetris-like strategy flexibly packs computation across the wafer, improving PE utilization and usable memory capacity while preserving PE-task code.
