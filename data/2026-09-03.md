### [1] [RunSoC 2.0: Scheduling and Allocating Automotive Software Tasks to Hardware Partitions in Heterogeneous MPSoCs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01614)
*Daniel Krüger,Lucas Mauser,Stefan Wagner*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出RunSoC 2.0，一个面向异构MPSoC的早期设计空间探索框架，用于任务调度与分配。它将任务集建模为DAG，受严格的端到端延迟和核心亲和力约束，并将调度分配问题构建为最小化层级内存预算违例和核间/簇间通信惩罚的多目标优化问题，支持CBC、CP-SAT和遗传算法多种求解后端，并在Renesas R-Car V4H、NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin和TI TDA4VM等真实异构平台及合成汽车任务集上验证了其有效性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 集中式汽车架构将计算密集型工作负载整合到异构多处理器SoC（MPSoC）上，产生严格的执行、内存和通信约束。RunSoC 1.0仅支持同构硬件的分配，无法应对异构平台在处理器特性、簇级组织和领域特定处理属性方面的复杂性，因此需要扩展框架以支持异构平台的早期设计空间探索。

Method: RunSoC 2.0在RunSoC 1.0基础上扩展：(1) 建模处理器特定的执行时间、簇级组织和领域特定处理属性；(2) 将任务集表示为受端到端延迟和核心亲和力约束的DAG；(3) 将调度与分配形式化为多目标优化问题，同时最小化层级内存预算违例和核间/簇间通信惩罚；(4) 支持多个求解后端（CBC、CP-SAT、遗传算法）以对比精确、约束规划和元启发式方法。

Result: 在10至500个任务的合成汽车任务集上，映射到Renesas R-Car V4H、NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin和TI TDA4VM等代表性异构MPSoC上进行评估。结果表明RunSoC 2.0能够生成可行且最优的调度方案、暴露架构瓶颈并支持平台替代方案的快速对比。CP-SAT在严格约束的硬实时调度实例中一致优于CBC和遗传算法。簇感知的通信和内存建模提升了早期MPSoC分析的现实性，同时保持了大型汽车工作负载的实际求解时间。

Conclusion: RunSoC 2.0通过引入簇感知通信与层级内存建模，显著提升了异构MPSoC早期任务调度与分配分析的现实性与实用性，能够在合理求解时间内支持大型汽车工作负载的设计空间探索，其中CP-SAT是处理严格硬实时约束下的最优求解后端选择。

Abstract: Centralized automotive architectures increasingly consolidate compute-intensive workloads onto heterogeneous Multi-Processor System-on-Chip (MPSoC), creating strict execution, memory, and communication constraints. This paper presents RunSoC 2.0, a customizable framework for early-stage design-space exploration of task scheduling and allocation on heterogeneous MPSoCs. Building on RunSoC 1.0, which targeted allocation on homogeneous hardware, RunSoC 2.0 extends the framework to heterogeneous platforms by modeling processor-specific execution times, cluster-level organization, and domain-specific processing properties. It represents task sets as directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) subjected to strict end-to-end latency and core-affinity constraints, and formulates task scheduling and allocation as a multi-objective optimization problem that minimizes hierarchical memory-budget violations and inter-core/inter-cluster communication penalties. The framework supports multiple solving backends, including COIN-OR Branch and Cut (CBC), Google OR-Tools CP-SAT, and a Genetic Algorithm (GA), enabling comparative evaluation of exact, constraint-programming, and meta-heuristic approaches. We evaluate RunSoC 2.0 using synthetic automotive task sets ranging from 10 to 500 tasks, mapped to representative heterogeneous MPSoCs, including the Renesas R-Car V4H, NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin, and TI TDA4VM. The results show that RunSoC 2.0 can generate feasible and optimal schedules, expose architectural bottlenecks, and support rapid comparison of platform alternatives. Notably, CP-SAT consistently outperforms both CBC and the GA across tightly constrained hard real-time scheduling instances. By incorporating cluster-aware communication and memory modeling, RunSoC 2.0 improves the realism of early-stage MPSoC analysis while retaining practical solution times for large automotive workloads. ### [2] [An Emerging NVM-Based On-Chip Training Architecture with Non-Ideality Mitigation Through Bipolar Weight Distributions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01948)
*Peng Dang,Youna Huang,Yintao He,Huawei Li*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种面向片上训练的忆阻器非理想性优化加速器架构NOVA，通过实验标定的二维FeFET器件模型与新型NAT算法，将权重收敛引导至器件最稳定电导区域，平均提升精度15.1%，能效相比GPU峰值提升约33.58倍。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 深度学习的高速发展对传统冯·诺依曼架构带来了严峻的能效挑战。虽然基于eNVM的存内计算架构被认为是加速神经网络训练的理想方案，但其固有的器件非理想性会导致电导更新偏离目标值，严重制约片上训练的性能表现。

Method: 作者首先制备了二维FeFET器件并开发了基于实验数据标定的电导调制行为模型；在此基础上，首次提出面向eNVM器件的非理想性规避训练（NAT）算法，通过引导权重收敛至eNVM器件最稳定的电导区域来缓解精度退化；并将二者集成于NOVA加速器架构中。

Result: 在多种基准任务上，即使面对严重的器件不对称性，NAT相比基线方法平均提升精度15.1%；同时NOVA相比GPU的峰值能效，平均获得约33.58倍的能效增益。

Conclusion: NOVA架构通过器件建模与算法协同设计，有效克服了eNVM非理想性对片上训练的限制，在精度保持和能效提升方面均展现出显著优势，为基于eNVM的神经网络训练加速提供了切实可行的解决方案。

Abstract: The rapid advancement of deep learning has presented significant energy efficiency challenges to the conventional von Neumann architecture. In-memory computing (IMC) architectures based on emerging non-volatile memory (eNVM) are widely regarded as a promising solution for accelerating neural network training due to their high parallelism and low power consumption. However, the intrinsic non-idealities of eNVM devices can cause conductance updates to deviate from target values, thereby limiting the performance of on-chip training. To address this challenge, this paper presents a Non-ideality Optimized eNVM Accelerator (NOVA) architecture for on-chip training. Specifically, we first fabricate a two-dimensional (2D) ferroelectric field-effect transistor (FeFET) and develop a conductance modulation behavioral model calibrated with experimental data. Building upon this device model, we propose, for the first time, a Non-ideality Avoidance Training (NAT) algorithm tailored for eNVM devices, which mitigates accuracy degradation by guiding weight convergence toward the most stable conductance regions of eNVM devices. Experimental results demonstrate that, even under severe device asymmetry, NAT improves the accuracy by an average of 15.1\% over the baseline methods across multiple benchmark tasks. Meanwhile, the NOVA achieves an average energy efficiency gain of approximately 33.58$\times$ compared with the peak energy efficiency of graphics processing units (GPUs).

### [3] [Atlas: Algorithm-Hardware Co-Design for On-Device City-Scale 3D Gaussian Splatting in VR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02352)
*He Zhu,Zheng Liu,Xingyang Li,Anbang Wu,Zihan Liu,Ruyang Li,Hui Wei,Yaqian Zhao,Jingwen Leng,Minyi Guo,Yu Feng*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: Atlas是一个面向移动VR设备的城市级3D高斯泼溅(3DGS)渲染框架，通过分层内存卸载机制仅加载当前帧所需的少量高斯数据，实现无需运行时互联网的可扩展渲染，相比GPU基线和最先进的3DGS加速器分别获得18.5倍和3.9倍加速，并节省92.4%能耗。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 3DGS在建筑领域受到广泛关注，但城市级3DGS场景的显存需求远超当前移动GPU的容量，在移动VR设备上实现可扩展的城市级3DGS渲染面临严峻的内存瓶颈挑战。

Method: Atlas的核心洞见是：尽管完整3DGS模型庞大，但每一帧仅需要当前姿态和细节层次要求下的一小部分高斯子集。基于此，Atlas提出：(1)分层内存卸载机制，动态仅加载所需高斯数据到设备内存；(2)时间感知的LoD（细节层次）搜索；(3)立体光栅化以避免VR中的冗余计算；(4)可与现有3DGS加速器集成且硬件开销可忽略。

Result: Atlas相比GPU基线实现18.5倍加速，相比最先进的3DGS加速器实现3.9倍加速，同时实现了92.4%的能耗节省。

Conclusion: 该工作验证了按需加载策略在大规模3DGS移动渲染中的有效性，证明分层内存卸载与时间感知LoD等优化手段可以在不依赖云端连接的前提下，使城市级3DGS在资源受限的移动VR设备上实现高性能、低能耗的实时渲染。

Abstract: 3D Gaussian splatting (3DGS) has drawn significant attention in the architectural community recently. However, enabling city scale 3DGS on mobile VR devices remains challenging, as the memory requirement of large scale scenes far exceeds the memory capacity of today's mobile GPUs. This paper presents Atlas, an on device city scale 3DGS rendering framework that enables scalable rendering without runtime Internet access. The key insight is that although the full 3DGS model is massive, each frame only requires a small subset of Gaussians under the current pose and level of detail requirement. Based on this insight, Atlas introduces a hierarchical memory offloading mechanism that dynamically loads only necessary Gaussian data into device memory. To further improve performance, Atlas proposes temporal aware LoD search and stereo rasterization to avoid redundant computation in VR. We further show that our technique can be integrated with existing 3DGS accelerators with negligible hardware overhead. Overall, Atlas achieves 18.5x speedup over the GPU baseline and 3.9x speedup over the state of the art 3DGS accelerators, with 92.4% energy savings.

### [4] [Batch Before You Time: Decision-Scoped Proxy Execution for Timing-Aware Logic Rewriting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02470)
*Pujun Su*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: BBYT（Batch Before You Time）通过将一次重写决策的所有候选方案编译进一个零延迟镜像并批量执行，避免逐候选进行时序评估的昂贵开销；当代理结果分离度足够时直接提交代理赢家，否则回退到完整时序评估。在12个均衡序列中平均减少18.05%的候选选择时间，消除32.52%的时序候选评估。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在SDF标注的开关级仿真中，功能等价的电路重写方案需要逐候选执行时序编译与回放来区分延迟相关的活性，成本高昂。零延迟代理虽可免除时序评估，但逐候选生成代理本身的代价可能超过节省的时序工作量，因此需要一种批量化的代理生成与决策策略。

Method: 提出BBYT方法：将同一次重写决策的所有候选编译进单个作用域定的零延迟镜像中批量执行；若代理结果呈现良好分离则直接选定代理赢家，否则回退至原有未修改的时序评估链路。采用均衡12序列与多工作负载语料进行实验验证，并与逐候选代理启动方式对比。

Result: 在12条均衡序列中平均减少18.05%的完整候选选择时间（两设计层级分别减少5.84%和30.25%，置信区间均高于零）；在8,192状态转换的C6288工作负载上比逐候选代理启动快9.24%；在五工作负载语料中消除了32.52%的时序候选评估，并在全部250个评估决策上与穷举时序选择结果一致。

Conclusion: 代理执行与保真度回退应共享同一决策作用域，BBYT证明了在时序感知重写选择中批量化代理编译能有效降低开销，同时保持与完整时序选择的一致性。

Abstract: Standard Delay Format (SDF)-annotated switching simulation distinguishes delay-dependent activity among functionally equivalent rewrites, but evaluating every candidate repeats timing, compilation, and replay. A zero-delay proxy can remove timed evaluations, but generating that proxy candidate by candidate can cost more than the timed work it saves. We present Batch Before You Time (BBYT), which compiles all candidates of one rewrite decision into one scoped zero-delay image and either commits a well-separated proxy winner or invokes the unchanged timed chain. Across 12 counterbalanced holdout sequences, BBYT reduces complete candidate-selection time by 18.05% on average; the design-level reductions are 5.84% and 30.25%, with both confidence intervals above zero. On a counterbalanced 8,192-transition C6288 workload, BBYT is 9.24% faster than the same gate executed with candidate-wise proxy launches. In the five-workload corpus, BBYT removes 32.52% of timed candidate evaluations and matches exhaustive timed selection on all 250 evaluated decisions. For on-demand timing-aware rewrite selection, proxy execution and fidelity continuation should use the same decision scope.
