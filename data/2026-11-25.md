### [1] [SlotFill: Repairing Lossy SoC Communication Traces for Post-Silicon Validation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31313)
*Tasnim Tabassum,Hao Zheng*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: SlotFill 利用基于 Transformer 的模型，将丢失的消息在正确的位置、以正确的类型和顺序精确回填，实现对 SoC 后硅验证 trace 中丢失消息的高精度恢复。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 后硅验证依赖 SoC 组件间交换的消息 trace，但 trace buffer 和监视器会丢弃消息，且往往不留下任何丢失痕迹，导致验证信息不完整、难以重建完整消息流。

Method: 为剩余消息之间的每个间隙分配固定数量的槽位；用 Transformer 模型为每个槽位填充一条消息，或在无消息丢失时留空，从而使输入本身不透露丢失程度；同时利用存活消息中的监视器字段（时间戳、事务 ID、地址）辅助推断。

Result: 在 4 核与 8 核系统的全程序 gem5 trace（20.7 亿条消息）上：15% 丢失率下最多可精确恢复 97% 的丢失消息；50% 丢失率下最多恢复 87%；在时间戳粗糙的情况下，15% 丢失率下恢复率为 76–83%。

Conclusion: SlotFill 通过固定槽位设计与 Transformer 建模，有效解决了丢失量未知条件下消息 trace 的精确重建问题，在高丢失率和低质量时间戳场景下仍保持较高恢复精度，为后硅验证的 trace 完整性提供了可行方案。

Abstract: Post-silicon validation relies on traces of messages exchanged by SoC components, but trace buffers and monitors drop messages, often leaving no mark of what was lost. We present SlotFill, which puts each deleted message back in the right gap, with the right type and order. SlotFill gives every gap between the remaining messages a fixed number of slots, and a Transformer-based model fills each slot with a message or leaves it empty when nothing was deleted there, so the input never reveals how much was lost. The model also uses monitor fields (timestamps, transaction IDs, addresses) from surviving messages. On whole-program gem5 traces of 4- and 8-core systems (2.07 billion messages), SlotFill restores up to 97% of deleted messages exactly at 15% loss and up to 87% at 50% loss, and 76-83% at 15% loss when timestamps are coarse.

### [2] [ExitLUT: Boolean Early Exits for Efficient FPGA-Based Neural Network Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31540)
*Oliver Cassidy,Marta Andronic,George A. Constantinides*

Main category: cs.AR

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: FPGA 上基于置信度（softmax）的早退机制会引入算术控制路径，与 LUT 架构匹配度差，占用本可用于主数据通路的资源。

Method: 提出 ExitLUT：将早退决策建模为学习得到的布尔函数，直接实现为 FPGA LUT；在 CNN 上以 fpgaConvNet 为静态空间数据流基线、ATHEENA 为其 softmax 早退扩展进行对比，并重新运行设计空间探索将节省资源再投入数据通路；同时应用于 LUT 型神经网络（NeuraLUT-Assemble）作为嵌入式退出。

Result: 决策路径 LUT 用量较 softmax 路径降至 1/2.58，决策路径 DSP 用量归零，精度与退出行为保持不变；重新探索设计空间后吞吐较 fpgaConvNet 最高提升 4.08×，较 ATHEENA 提升 1.82×；LUT 型网络嵌入 ExitLUT 退出后，平均决策时间加速 1.41–1.89×，LUT 开销低于 1%。

Conclusion: 将早退决策以布尔函数形式直接映射到 LUT，可显著降低决策路径资源开销并使资源回归数据通路，是一种与 FPGA LUT 架构高度契合的早退实现方式。

Abstract: Early exit neural networks reduce average inference cost by terminating once sufficient confidence is reached. On FPGAs, conventional confidence-based exits add arithmetic control paths poorly matched to LUT fabrics, consuming resources that could otherwise support the main datapath. We present ExitLUT, which replaces softmax-style exit decisions with learned Boolean functions mapped directly into FPGA LUTs. In our CNN evaluation, fpgaConvNet is the static spatial-dataflow baseline, while ATHEENA is its softmax-based early exit extension. ExitLUT reduces ATHEENA's exit-decision LUT usage by 2.58$\times$ over the softmax path and eliminates decision-path DSP usage, while preserving accuracy and exit behavior. Rerunning design-space exploration reinvests these savings into the datapath, improving throughput by up to 4.08$\times$ over fpgaConvNet and 1.82$\times$ over ATHEENA. For LUT-based NNs, embedded ExitLUT exits achieve 1.41-1.89$\times$ average time-to-decision speedup over static NeuraLUT-Assemble designs with under 1% LUT overhead.
