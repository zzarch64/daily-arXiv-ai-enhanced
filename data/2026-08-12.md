### [2] [CARB: A Characterization-Guided Framework for CNN Inference Cost Prediction and Deployment Screening](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10506)
*Linh Nguyen,Zhixin Pan*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文对13,419个CNN配置在RTX 5090和RTX 3080两个GPU平台上的推理成本（能量、延迟、峰值内存）进行了系统性的负载特征刻画研究。研究发现三个目标具有不同的伸缩行为：能量与延迟在高计算负载下分歧达3倍，跨GPU可迁移性因目标而异（能量和延迟需要平台专属模型，而内存可以良好迁移）。基于这些发现，作者开发了CARB级联混合集成模型（R²≈0.99）联合预测三个目标，并设计了二阶段部署筛选工作流，可在数秒内消除90%以上的候选配置，将大规模设计空间缩减为经真实硬件验证的Pareto优先短名单。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: CNN在资源受限GPU平台上的部署越来越普遍，准确预测推理成本（能量、延迟、峰值内存）至关重要。现有方法依赖FLOPs、延迟测量或单设备profile作为能量代理，忽略了架构设计与硬件负载之间的非线性交互，导致预测精度有限。

Method: 在RTX 5090和RTX 3080两个GPU平台上，通过GPU遥测对13,419个CNN配置进行负载特征刻画，分析能量、延迟和内存的不同伸缩行为及跨GPU可迁移性。基于刻画发现，构建CARB（cascade-blended ensemble）级联混合集成模型联合预测三个目标，并开发二阶段部署筛选工作流。

Result: 能量与延迟在高计算需求下分歧达3倍；能量和延迟的预测需要平台专属模型，而内存预测在两个平台上可良好迁移。CARB模型在三个目标上达到R²≈0.99，二阶段工作流能在数秒内消除90%以上的候选配置。

Conclusion: CNN推理成本的不同维度（能量、延迟、内存）具有异质的伸缩和迁移特性，统一的代理指标（如FLOPs）不足以准确预测；通过级联混合集成和分阶段筛选，可以在部署前高效准确地预测推理成本，并将大规模设计空间快速缩减为可验证的Pareto优先候选集。

Abstract: Accurate pre-deployment estimation of CNN inference cost--energy, latency, and peak memory--is increasingly critical as models are deployed on resource-constrained GPU platforms. Existing approaches rely on FLOPs, latency measurements, or single-device profiling as energy proxies, overlooking the non-linear interactions between architectural design and hardware load. We present a workload characterization study of 13 419 CNN configurations on two GPU platforms (RTX 5090 and RTX 3080) under GPU telemetry, revealing that energy, latency, and memory exhibit fundamentally distinct scaling behaviors: energy and latency diverge by 3x under high computational demand, and cross-GPU transferability differs by target--energy and latency require platform-specific models while memory transfers well across the two tested platforms. Building on these characterization findings, we develop CARB, a cascade-blended ensemble that jointly predicts all three targets with R2 ~0.99, and a two-stage deployment screening workflow that eliminates over 90% of candidates in seconds, reducing large design spaces to a Pareto-prioritized shortlist validated against real hardware.

### [3] [Adaptive Matrix Multiplication for Dynamic Shapes on Ascend NPUs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10803)
*Yuhang Zhou,Jiang Peng,Qianyu Jiang,Zhibin Wang,Xinghui Tian,Jianwei Zhou,Songxiang Zhu,Jingyi Zhang,Junsong Wang,Chen Tian*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 提出AdaptCore框架，解决Ascend NPU上因动态张量形状导致的矩阵乘法"泛化危机"，通过空间切分与指令编排的解耦、硬件感知的2D切分分类法以及确定性解析性能模型，实现O(1)开销的运行时调度，在80,000种输入形状上平均加速1.85倍，端到端模型较ACLNN加速最高1.48倍。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 动态张量形状导致矩阵乘法面临"泛化危机"，尤其在Ascend NPU上，其显式控制的架构和严格的物理约束使得现有GPU中心的优化方法失效，亟需针对Ascend NPU的高性能通用矩阵乘法框架。

Method: AdaptCore将算子优化系统化解耦为空间切分(spatial tiling)与指令编排(instruction orchestration)两个层面。首先将动态形状映射为硬件感知的2D切分分类法，以平衡片上容量限制与多核并行性；其次集成可组合优化库与确定性解析性能模型，通过数学评估硬件状态变更，主动选择并缓存最优实现，实现O(1)开销的运行时调度。

Result: 在80,000种输入形状上实现平均1.85倍加速，在代表性端到端模型中相对高度调优的厂商原生库ACLNN实现最高1.48倍加速。

Conclusion: AdaptCore通过系统性解耦优化策略与硬件感知建模，有效解决了Ascend NPU上动态形状矩阵乘法的性能泛化问题，验证了在专用架构上超越厂商原生优化的可行性。

Abstract: Matrix Multiplication (MatMul) faces a "generalization crisis" driven by highly dynamic tensor shapes. This crisis is particularly acute on Ascend NPUs, where explicitly controlled architectures and strict physical constraints render existing GPU-centric optimizations ineffective. To resolve this, we propose AdaptCore, an adaptive framework for universally high-performance MatMul on Ascend NPUs. AdaptCore systematically decouples operator optimization into spatial tiling and instruction orchestration. It first maps dynamic shapes into a hardware-aware 2D tiling taxonomy to balance on-chip capacity limits and multi-core parallelism. Furthermore, it integrates a composable optimization library with a deterministic analytical performance model. By mathematically evaluating hardware state mutations, AdaptCore proactively selects and caches optimal implementations, enabling O(1) overhead runtime dispatching. Evaluations demonstrate that AdaptCore delivers a remarkable 1.85x mean speedup across 80,000 input shapes, and achieves up to a 1.48x acceleration in representative end-to-end models over the highly-tuned native vendor library (ACLNN).

### [4] [You Only Charge Once 2.0 : A End-to-End Analog Computing-in-Memory Architecture with Reconfigurable Switched Capacitors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11116)
*Zihao Xuan,Yewen Li,Jia Chen,Wei Xuan,Xiao Huo,Fengbin Tu*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: Charge-CIM通过将开关电容电荷重分配作为统一的计算与转换基底，解决模拟存内计算(ACiM)加速器中ADC墙（模数转换器能耗和面积开销大）的瓶颈问题。同样的电容阵列同时执行输入转换、模拟MAC、加权移位相加和读出量化，并通过差分读出路径在ADC量化期间合并成对的部分和，相比最先进的电荷域CIM加速器可实现91.7%的ADC能耗降低、2.7倍能效提升和2.0倍吞吐量提升。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代ACiM加速器受限于"ADC墙"问题：模数转换器消耗大量能量和面积，且位切片执行需要反复调用这些转换器。现有方案要么采用低分辨率读出（损失输出保真度），要么采用时间复用（引入序列化开销），无法在保真度和开销之间取得理想平衡。

Method: Charge-CIM采用开关电容电荷重分配作为统一的计算与转换基底。同一电容阵列同时承担输入转换、模拟MAC、加权移位相加和读出量化功能，消除独立的转换器开销和中间ADC调用。通过差分读出路径在ADC量化阶段合并成对的部分和，实现紧凑高效的阵列集成。同时提供数据流架构支持。

Result: 在从CNN到Transformer的DNN基准测试集上评估，Charge-CIM相比最先进的电荷域CIM加速器，在评估设置下将ADC能耗降低91.7%，能效提升2.7倍，吞吐量提升2.0倍。

Conclusion: Charge-CIM通过统一计算与转换基底的方式有效突破了ACiM加速器的ADC墙瓶颈，在降低能耗的同时提升能效和吞吐量，为大规模DNN推理提供了紧凑且节能的解决方案。

Abstract: Analog Computing-in-Memory (ACiM) accelerates deep neural networks by keeping weights inside memory arrays and executing dot products in the analog domain. However, modern ACiM accelerators are often limited by the "ADC wall": analog-to-digital converters consume a large fraction of energy and area, while bit-sliced execution repeatedly invokes these converters. Existing designs reduce this cost with low-resolution readout or time multiplexing, but they either lose output fidelity or introduce serialization overhead.
  Charge-CIM addresses this bottleneck by using switched-capacitor charge redistribution as a unified computing and conversion substrate. The same capacitor fabric performs input conversion, analog MAC, weighted shift-and-add, and readout quantization, reducing both standalone converter overhead and intermediate ADC invocations. A differential readout path further combines paired partial sums during ADC quantization, providing a highly compact and energy-efficient solution for array integration. With dataflow architecture support, we evaluated Charge-CIM on a suite of DNN benchmarks, from CNNs to Transformer models, and experimental results show that Charge-CIM reduces ADC energy by 91.7% under our evaluation setup and improves energy efficiency by 2.7x and throughput by 2.0x compared to the state-of-the-art charge-domain CIM accelerator.

</details>
