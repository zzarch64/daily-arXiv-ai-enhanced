### [1] [PDD: Unleashing Economical and Flexible Heterogeneous LLM Inference via Cross-Datacenter Prefill-Decode Disaggregation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13161)
*Yida Wang,Xiuhong Li,Jianping Ma,Gan Sun,Yunshen Xu,Buhe Han,Jingxu Ng,Yuhao Luo,Ke Hong,Guohao Dai,Boxun Li,Yu Wang*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 论文提出PDD三层解耦架构（Prefill、RelayDecode、MainDecode），用于跨数据中心广域以太网上的LLM推理，通过解码侧RadixCache、Extend-Decode Handoff与多级流水线编排，缓解KV缓存跨集群传输带来的TTFT延迟，并采用低成本异构部署提升性价比。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 地理分布集群通过广域以太网互联为LLM推理提供了可扩展、低成本的方案，但跨数据中心解耦导致集群间KV缓存传输量巨大，显著增加首Token延迟（TTFT）；该问题对长上下文、高缓存命中率、短输出的agentic负载尤为严重。因此需要一种新的解耦架构与调度机制来隐藏KV传输延迟。

Method: 提出PDD三层解耦架构：Cluster A上的Prefill实例完成预填充，RelayDecode（RLD）实例通过高速RDMA接收KV缓存并立即开始解码，从而与基于TCP以太网向Cluster B的KV传输重叠；Cluster B上的MainDecode（MD）实例接收KV缓存及RLD产生的token，解码从RLD无缝移交给MD。三大核心机制：解码侧RadixCache缓解带宽瓶颈、Extend-Decode Handoff实现RLD与MD间的平滑控制迁移、多级流水线编排应对高并发与长期服务下的跨层依赖。并设计低成本细粒度异构部署方案，以边际成本最大化延迟掩盖效率。

Result: 相比数据中心内同构PD基线，PDD将计算密集型H100与内存带宽优化的H200进行跨数据中心映射，在满足SLA的有效吞吐（goodput）上实现最高37.5%的收益成本比（BCR）提升。

Conclusion: PDD通过三层解耦与配套的缓存、交接与流水线机制，有效掩盖跨数据中心KV缓存传输延迟，降低TTFT；结合H100/H200的细粒度异构部署，显著提升跨数据中心LLM推理的性价比与SLA达标吞吐。

Abstract: Interconnecting geographically dispersed clusters over wide-area Ethernet provides a scalable and cost-effective alternative to dedicated intra-datacenter heterogeneous clusters for Large Language Model (LLM) inference. However, this cross-datacenter disaggregation imposes heavy KV-cache transfers between clusters, causing substantial Time-to-First-Token (TTFT) latency, which is especially detrimental for agentic workloads characterized by long contexts, high cache hit rates, and short outputs.
  We propose PDD, a three-tier disaggregation architecture built upon prefill-decode (PD) disaggregation, consisting of Prefill, RelayDecode (RLD), and MainDecode (MD) instances. On Cluster A, Prefill instances perform the prefill computation, while RLD instances immediately receive the KV cache via high-speed RDMA and begin decoding, thereby overlapping the KV transfer to Cluster B over TCP-based Ethernet. MD instances on Cluster B receive the KV cache along with the tokens produced by RLD, and decoding is then seamlessly handed off from RLD to MD for completion.
  To maximize overall efficiency, PDD employs three core mechanisms: Decode-side RadixCache to alleviate bandwidth bottlenecks, an Extend-Decode Handoff mechanism for smooth control migration between RLD and MD, and multi-stage pipeline orchestration to manage complex inter-tier dependencies under high concurrency and long-term serving. We further design a low-cost, fine-grained heterogeneous deployment scheme that maximizes latency-masking efficiency at marginal cost. Compared to the intra-DC homogeneous PD baseline, PDD's cross-datacenter mapping of compute-intensive H100s and memory-bandwidth-optimized H200s achieves a Benefit-Cost Ratio (BCR) up to 37.5% higher in SLA-compliant goodput.

### [2] [SIMT-Aware Lockstep Verification and Functional-Coverage Closure Methodology for an Open-Source RISC-V GPGPU: A UVM 1.2 Environment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13311)
*Samuel Moussa,Steven Ibrahim,Ahmad Sudky,Ahmad Fawzy,Abanoub Nabil,Alhassan Sayed,Hossam Hassan,Hyung-Min Yoon*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 该论文构建了一套针对开源RISC-V GPGPU（Vortex）的UVM 1.2验证环境与方法学，通过以SimX为黄金模型、双向计分板与逐指令逐lane锁步比较器、以及首个公开的SIMT功能覆盖率层，实现了对带指令内核回归的可量化签核验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 开源RISC-V GPGPU（如Vortex）仅提供定向内核回归，缺乏参考模型校验、功能覆盖率模型与签核规范，验证完备性无从量化。

Method: 基于UVM 1.2构建环境，将总线主设备式SIMT DUT用角色反转agent封装；通过DPI-C将Vortex功能模拟器SimX集成为按配置的黄金模型；采用两个注入限定的检查器（双向末态计分板与在五条SIMT对齐规则下的逐指令、逐lane锁步比较器）给出判决；以两趟加载值馈入使无栅栏多核竞态程序达到指令粒度可验证；构建三层覆盖率模型，含首个公开的SIMT功能覆盖层（发散深度、bank冲突、合并类别）。

Result: 残留为零（5,432次retirement）；三层覆盖率自闭合达98.1% covergroup-bin / 94.7%总量（ISA层83.1% bin / 89.3%加权）；未激励的D扩展不影响任何功能bin，仅拉低总数。发现缺陷包括JALR LSB ISA偏差、watchdog常数不随规模缩放（已上游修复）、复位中继X窗口、缺失的AXI错误路径（由故障注入证明）、以及由锁步发现的参考模型取指bug。

Conclusion: 该方法与同DUT上的并发RTL GPU模糊测试器FuzzGPU（USENIX Security 2026）互补：模糊测试找bug，本方法量化签核，二者独立发现同一JALR偏差；所有发现收录于带证据引用的登记表，每个数字均可溯源，方法边界被明确陈述而非豁免。

Abstract: Open-source RISC-V GPGPUs such as Vortex ship with directed-kernel regressions but no reference-model checking, functional-coverage model, or sign-off discipline. This paper presents a UVM 1.2 environment and methodology that closes that gap. The environment wraps a bus-master SIMT DUT with role-inverted agents, integrates Vortex's functional simulator (SimX) as a per-configuration golden model over DPI-C, and renders verdicts via two injection-qualified checkers: a bidirectional end-state scoreboard and a per-instruction, per-lane lockstep comparator under five SIMT alignment rules. A two-pass load-value feed makes racy fenceless multi-core programs instruction-granularity verifiable (residual zero over 5,432 retirements), with the interrupt-timing boundary stated. A three-layer coverage model adds, to our knowledge, the first published SIMT functional-coverage layer for RTL GPU verification (divergence depth, bank conflicts, coalescing classes), closing its own layers at 98.1% covergroup-bin / 94.7% total (ISA layer separately: 83.1% bins / 89.3% weighted) under machine-generated, RTL-cited exclusions and a blocking waiver-integrity gate; an unstimulated D-extension elaboration affects no functional bin, only lowering totals. The checking depth surfaced real defects on both sides of the comparison: a JALR LSB ISA deviation, a non-scaling watchdog constant (since fixed upstream), a reset-relay X window found by restoring a silenced assertion, a missing AXI error path proven by fault injection, and a reference-model fetch bug found by the lockstep itself. FuzzGPU (USENIX Security 2026), a concurrent RTL GPU fuzzer on the same DUT, is complementary: fuzzing finds bugs, this methodology quantifies sign-off; both independently found the JALR deviation. All findings ship in an evidence-cited register; every number carries provenance and the method's boundaries are stated rather than waived.

### [3] [NeuroFlex: Lossless Element-Level ANN-SNN Co-Execution for Efficient Sparse Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14092)
*Varun Manjunath,Pranav Ramesh,Gopalakrishnan Srinivasan*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: NeuroFlex 是首个可将每个输出元素独立分配给 ANN 或 SNN 执行模式的加速器，在无精度损失前提下实现细粒度模式切换，显著提升 PE 利用率并降低能耗延迟积。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 稀疏 DNN 加速器通常专用于 ANN 或 SNN 执行，当层内工作负载特征变化时，会浪费能耗或延迟。混合加速器虽可在层或 tile 粒度切换模式，但同一时刻仅一类核心工作，导致另一核心闲置，PE 利用率低。

Method: 将整数精确的 ANN-SNN 等价性从层粒度扩展到单个输出元素，使模式切换无转换误差；并设计离线代价引导调度器，按每个元素的边际能耗-延迟权衡打分，并将工作跨 PE 打包分配。

Result: PE 利用率达 97-99%，而层粒度混合方案仅为 40-45%；相较强 ANN-only 基线，EDP 降低 57-67%；相较双稀疏 SNN-only 基线，最高获得 2.5 倍加速；代价引导调度器在视觉、语言和 transformer 工作负载上相比随机元素分配提升吞吐 16-19%。

Conclusion: 通过将 ANN-SNN 等价性细化到元素级并结合代价引导调度，NeuroFlex 实现了高 PE 利用率、低 EDP 和显著加速，验证了元素级异构执行模式分配的有效性。

Abstract: Sparse DNN accelerators specialize in ANN or SNN execution, leaving energy or latency on the table when workload characteristics vary within a layer. Hybrid accelerator designs that switch modes at layer or tile granularity suffer from low PE utilization since one core type idles whenever the other is active. NeuroFlex is the first accelerator to assign every output element independently to ANN or SNN execution mode with zero accuracy loss. We extend integer-exact ANN-SNN equivalence from layers to individual output elements, thereby enabling mode switching with no conversion error. An offline cost-guided scheduler scores each element by its marginal energy-delay trade-off and packs work across PEs, achieving 97-99% PE utilization compared to 40-45% for layer-wise hybrids. NeuroFlex reduces EDP by 57-67% over a strong ANN-only baseline and delivers up to 2.5x speedup over a dual-sparse SNN-only baseline. Our cost-guided scheduler improves throughput by 16-19% over random element assignment across vision, language, and transformer workloads.

</details>
