### [1] [Ankhdjet: An Open-Source Compiler for Mask-Programmed Ternary Compute-in-ROM on an Open PDK](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26206)
*Mohnish Pai*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: 本文提出Ankhdjet，一个开源编译器，可将HuggingFace三元量化（BitNet b1.58）检查点直接编译为SKY130开源PDK上的只读via-mask compute-in-ROM宏程序，实现端到端开源工具链验证。作者声称这是首个在可流片开源PDK上完成全开源工具链签核（DRC/LVS零错误、时序干净）的weights-to-mask compute-in-ROM编译器，并首次提交compute-in-ROM宏在开源PDK上流片（TinyTapeout shuttle，硅片预期2027年）。编译器采用全数字高速读出，模拟比较器读出作为对比变体仅占其八分之一面积。文中还报告了逐层对抗验证方法（发现三个DRC不可见的静默短路）、130nm下每晶体管存两个三元权重的负结果，以及各节点适用的模拟传感技术。读能耗来自提取寄生仿真（0.98-1.73pJ/权重），无实测能耗声明。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大语言模型推理的主要瓶颈是权重搬运——每生成一个token都要重读所有权重。三元量化（BitNet b1.58）将每权重压缩至1.58比特，在2B规模已达到与全精度相当的报道效果，权重规模小到可以把权重硬连线到只读掩膜中，且商用芯片（Taalas HC1）已在先进节点验证了该方案（但工具链封闭）。本文的动机是：能否用完全开源的基础设施实现模型专用硬件的可复现设计流程。

Method: 1) 设计Ankhdjet编译器：将HuggingFace三元检查点（BitNet b1.58类模型）降级为固定compute-in-ROM宏的via-mask程序；2) 在开源SKY130 PDK上实现完整的weights-to-mask编译流程，用两条不同权重矩阵走完全相同流程（仅掩膜不同）做两次签核；3) 采用全数字读出（全摆幅位线采样进入标准单元，通过综合即可输出到任意节点），并用模拟比较器读出作为对比变体；4) 实现逐层对抗验证方法学来捕获DRC盲区中的静默短路；5) 在TinyTapeout/ChipFoundry SKY130 shuttle提交流片。

Result: 1) 首个可在开源PDK上流片的、轻过全开源工具链签核的weights-to-mask compute-in-ROM编译器，KLayout DRC零错误、netgen LVS零错误、时序干净；2) 首次在开源PDK提交compute-in-ROM宏流片（ttsky26c shuttle，硅预计2027年）；3) 对抗验证方法发现三个DRC不可见的静默短路；4) 130nm下每晶体管存储两个三元权重的方法失败（负结果）；5) 数字读出面积仅为模拟比较器读出方案的八分之一；6) 提取寄生仿真显示读能耗为0.98-1.73pJ/权重（无实测声明）。

Conclusion: 本文证明了模型专用硅（compute-in-ROM）可以通过完全开源的基础设施实现可复现的设计与流片：编译器流程在开源PDK上以两次独立签核验证了可复现性，数字读出方案以更小面积替代模拟读出并保持兼容性。研究同时指出了一些负面结果（130nm下双三元权重存储失败）和尚未解决的问题（读能耗仅为仿真值，未做实测），表明该方向仍需进一步验证，流片验证（2027年硅片回片）是后续关键里程碑。

Abstract: Large-language-model inference is dominated by weight movement: every generated token re-reads every weight. Ternary quantization (BitNet b1.58) shrinks each weight to 1.58 bits with reported parity at the 2B-parameter scale, small enough that hardwiring the weights into a read-only mask becomes plausible, and a commercial chip (Taalas HC1) has validated hardwired weights on an advanced node behind closed tooling. We present Ankhdjet, an open-source compiler that lowers a HuggingFace ternary checkpoint (BitNet b1.58 and its kin) to a via-mask program of a fixed compute-in-ROM macro on the open SKY130 PDK, verified end to end with open tools. We additionally report the per-level adversarial verification methodology that caught three DRC-invisible silent shorts, a negative result on storing two ternary weights per transistor at 130 nm, and which analog sense techniques survive at which nodes. Read energy is from extracted-parasitic simulation (0.98-1.73 pJ per sensed weight); no energy measurements are claimed, and the fabricated vehicles are demonstrators.

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: MeshReduce-U包含五个核心步骤：合并同源（co-located）源节点、形成本地聚合岛、阻塞具有兼容扇入结构的信道、选择容量可行的汇聚（sink）节点、以及使用融合使用感知代价（fused usage-aware costs）路由剩余的固定宽度载体。同时提出一种确定性路由回放模型，分别报告调度推导的通信延迟、总链路使用量（TLU）和融合链路使用量（FusedTLU）。

  <summary>Details</summary>

Large language models (LLMs) extend this trajectory by enabling direct translation from design intent to hardware implementations. In most of the EDA literature, LLM-based solutions are typically assisting siloed design stages or tasks, however this obscured the drivers by which capability emerges and systems scale. In this Perspective, we instead define three hierarchical roles that reveal how capability accumulates: a Generator that produces design artifacts in a single pass, an Agent that refines outputs through iterative tool feedback, and an Orchestrator that coordinates decisions across EDA-stages. Across published systems, this reveals a syntax trap in which models are trained to produce plausible code rather than physically correct hardware, compounded by fragmented tools and loss of design context that obscure how decisions affect later stages. Comparisons across the three roles show that current approaches struggle to scale to industrial designs, motivating a shift towards a standardised, physics-aware orchestrator that connects tools and agents across the EDA flow for more reliable and accessible hardware design.
