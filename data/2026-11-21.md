### [2] [Compressing Page-Table Leaves in Shared Caches](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24992)
*Samarth Narang,Aamer Jaleel,Brandon Reagen*

Main category: cs.AR

TL;DR: LeafPack 是一种共享缓存组织方案，通过在标准缓存路中原地压缩页表叶子行，并在运行时用集合对偶控制器调节页表占用，从而在地址翻译覆盖与应用程序数据容量之间取得平衡。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 硬件页表遍历器经缓存层次取描述符，页表遍历与应用数据争夺共享缓存容量。在片上保留叶子页表行可避免昂贵的 DRAM 读取，但当工作负载映射足迹增大时，这些行会驱逐应用数据，拖慢对缓存敏感的协同运行任务，形成权衡矛盾。

Method: 利用相邻描述符在物理帧号与属性上的规律性，将最多十六个 64 字节叶子行连同元数据打包进单个 80 字节槽位，在缓存路内原地压缩。叶子行随遍历取用增量打包，按需重建单个行，不规则行保持不压缩；在遍历器标记叶子请求的前提下，保持内存页表格式与硬件遍历算法不变。另设运行时集合对偶控制器，在压缩与非压缩路上限之间选择以调节页表占用。

Result: 在 Arm gem5 全系统仿真中，覆盖小核与服务器级乱序核、1 至 8 核工作负载。九个翻译密集型负载上，相比基线共享缓存的几何平均调和加速为 28.7% 与 28.4%，相比 SRRIP 基线为 18.7% 与 17.7%。冻结控制器后评估的二十个未见混合负载全部获得加速，几何平均增益为 21.4% 与 18.5%。

Conclusion: 通过对页表叶子行的原地压缩与自适应占用控制，LeafPack 有效缓解了页表容量占用与应用数据容量之间的竞争，在多种核心规模与多核配置下均带来稳定且可泛化的性能提升。

Abstract: Hardware page-table walkers fetch descriptors through the cache hierarchy, so page-table walks compete with application data for shared-cache capacity. Retaining leaf page-table lines on chip avoids costly DRAM reads during address translation, yet as a workload's mapped footprint grows, these lines displace application data and slow cache-sensitive co-runners.
  We introduce LeafPack, a shared-cache organization that resolves this trade-off by compressing architectural page-table leaf lines in place within standard cache ways. By exploiting regularity in the physical frame numbers and attributes of neighboring descriptors, LeafPack packs up to sixteen 64-byte leaf lines into a single 80-byte slot including metadata. It packs leaf lines incrementally as walks fetch them and reconstructs individual lines on demand while leaving irregular lines uncompressed, preserving both the in-memory page-table format and the hardware walk algorithm as long as walkers tag leaf requests. A runtime set-dueling controller adapts page-table occupancy by choosing between compressed and uncompressed way ceilings to balance translation coverage against application-data capacity. We evaluate LeafPack in full-system Arm gem5 simulations across a small and a server-class out-of-order core on workloads spanning one to eight cores. Across nine translation-heavy workloads, LeafPack improves geometric-mean harmonic speedup on the small and server cores by 28.7% and 28.4% over our baseline shared cache, and by 18.7% and 17.7% over an SRRIP baseline. Across twenty unseen held-out mixes evaluated after freezing the controller, LeafPack speeds up every mix with geometric-mean gains of 21.4% and 18.5%.

  <summary>Details</summary>
